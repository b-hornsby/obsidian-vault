# So stick to kde?

### USER
So stick to kde?

### GEMINI
Stick to KDE Plasma. Since you’re already on CachyOS and using an NVIDIA 3070, switching to GNOME right now would be a lateral move at best and a downgrade in control at worst. Here’s the "brutally honest" logic: 1. Granular Control (The "Logistics" Mindset) In your professional life, you deal with high-volume logistics; you know that visibility and control are everything. KDE gives you Window Rules. You can literally force the OS to treat Vesktop as a high-priority "VIP" process. GNOME treats you like a passenger. It automates power saving and frame throttling, and if it decides to lag your stream to save 2 watts of power, you have very few "knobs" to turn to stop it. 2. The NVIDIA "Flicker" Fix KDE's compositor (KWin) is currently more flexible when it comes to handling NVIDIA’s specific synchronization quirks. By using the Window Rules I gave you (setting "No Lowered Priority" and "Focus Stealing Prevention"), you are manually overriding the bugs that cause the lag. In GNOME, you’re just hoping the automation works—and clearly, for your streaming, it isn't. 3. CachyOS Optimization CachyOS is built for performance and customization. KDE matches that DNA perfectly. You’ve already done the "full send" to Linux; KDE allows you to tune the engine while it's running. GNOME is a "hood-welded-shut" experience by comparison. Your "No-Nonsense" Priority List to Fix the Issue: If you want to stop the flickering and lag today, do these three things in this order: Force Native Wayland: Use those Exec flags in your .desktop file. This stops the "translation" flicker. Apply KDE Window Rules: Stop the OS from "starving" Vesktop of resources when you’re gaming. Kill Adaptive Sync (VRR): This is the #1 cause of flickering for NVIDIA users on Wayland during screen capture. Turn it OFF in Display Settings when you plan to stream. The Opportunity Cost If you spend your time hopping to GNOME, you’ll just be trading one set of bugs for another, and you’ll lose the ability to fix them manually. Stay on KDE, apply the manual overrides, and get your stream stable so you can focus on your DeFi bot or your gaming.
