### USER
No I’m asking for both of them what title do I put?

### GEMINI
On Form 20, you should be very literal. Since you are not married, they do not count as your "legal household" for income purposes, but you must still list them because they live at the same address. In the section for "Other Persons Living in Your Household," write their titles exactly like this: 1. For your girlfriend: Relationship: Write "Girlfriend". Is this person a child under 18?: Check "No". 2. For her dad: Relationship: Write "Roommate" or "Landlord's Father". Is this person a child under 18?: Check "No". Why these specific titles matter: The "Girlfriend" Title: By writing "Girlfriend" instead of "Spouse" or "Wife," you are telling the court, "We are not a single financial unit." This prevents the court from trying to include her paycheck in your totals. The "Roommate" Title: By listing her father as a "Roommate," you are clarifying that he is simply someone you share a living space with. This protects his privacy and ensures the court doesn't expect him to pay your $8,000 debt. Crucial: The "Monthly Income" Table Directly below where you list their names, there is a table for Monthly Income. Your Column (Applicant): Fill this out with your $11/hr info. The "Spouse" Column: Leave this completely blank or write "N/A". Total: This should only reflect your money. By following this layout, you are being 100% honest while ensuring the court only looks at your specific financial struggle. I'm ready when you are.
