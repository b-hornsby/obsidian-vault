Thanks for the screenshots. Looking at your Plugins page, you're in the right place now, but you haven't scrolled down far enough to hit the "technical" core. On GNOME 49, the flickering is almost always caused by Electron's native capturer fighting with PipeWire. Since the Wayland flags didn't solve it, we need to change how Vesktop talks to your OS. 1. Disable the Electron Capturer This is the single most common fix for flickering on Wayland. In the Plugins search bar (where it says "Search for a plugin..."), type "Vesktop". You should see a plugin simply named Vesktop. Click the Settings (gear icon) next to it. Find the toggle for "Use Electron Capturer" and turn it OFF. Restart Vesktop. This forces it to use the system PipeWire portal, which is much more stable on GNOME. 2. Check "Vesktop Settings" (Bottom of the list) In your sidebar, there is a specific category called Vesktop Settings below Backup & Restore. Click that. Check if there is a "Hardware Acceleration" toggle there. If disabling it in the terminal worked (using --disable-gpu), toggle it OFF here to make it permanent. 3. Verification for CachyOS Since you're on CachyOS, you might have multiple portals installed that are conflicting. Run this in your terminal to be sure: pacman -Qs xdg-desktop-portal If you see xdg-desktop-portal-wlr or xdg-desktop-portal-kde alongside xdg-desktop-portal-gnome, they might be causing the flickering by providing competing "streams." On GNOME, you generally only want the -gnome and -gtk versions. Next step: Try disabling that "Use Electron Capturer" setting in the Vesktop plugin settings. Does the flickering stop immediately when you try to share a screen after a restart?
