### GEMINI
Ah, I understand! You're asking about the ReShade_Setup.exe file itself, not the ReShade files installed within your games. You absolutely can put the ReShade_Setup.exe file in the Recycle Bin and empty it. The ReShade_Setup.exe is just the installer/uninstaller tool. It's not a program that runs in the background or installs anything permanently on your system outside of the specific game directories you choose. Think of it like this: ReShade_Setup.exe: This is like a hammer. You use it to install ReShade into a game, and you use it again to uninstall ReShade from a game. ReShade files (e.g., dxgi.dll, ReShade.ini, ReShade-Shaders folder): These are the nails that the hammer puts into the game's folder. Once you've used the "hammer" (ReShade_Setup.exe) to uninstall ReShade from all the games you want, you no longer need the hammer itself unless you plan to install ReShade on new games or reinstall it on existing ones in the future. Where you typically find ReShade_Setup.exe: Your Downloads folder: This is the most common place, as it's where your browser usually saves downloaded files. Wherever you specifically saved it: If you chose a different location when you downloaded it. So, yes, once you've utilized the ReShade_Setup.exe to perform any necessary uninstallations within your games, you can safely delete the ReShade_Setup.exe file from your computer without affecting any existing ReShade installations (or lack thereof) in your games.
