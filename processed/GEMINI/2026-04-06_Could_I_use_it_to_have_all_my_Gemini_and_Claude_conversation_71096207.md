# Could I use it to have all my Gemini and Claude conversations?

### USER
Could I use it to have all my Gemini and Claude conversations?

### GEMINI
Yes, and if you’re already deep into "vibe coding" and agentic workflows, using Obsidian to house your AI conversations is actually the only way to make them useful long-term. Right now, your insights are trapped in a dozen different browser tabs. Moving them to Obsidian turns them into a queryable database. Here is how you actually do it without making it a full-time job: 1. The "Manual-ish" Way (Browser Extensions) If you just want to save specific, high-value conversations: Obsidian AI Exporter (Chrome/Firefox): This is a one-click tool. You open a Gemini or Claude chat, hit the extension button, and it sends the entire thread directly into your Obsidian vault as a Markdown file. Why use this: It formats code blocks perfectly and preserves the "User vs. AI" distinction using Obsidian’s "callout" boxes, so it’s easy to read later. 2. The "Power User" Way (Model Context Protocol - MCP) Since you’re already comfortable with tools like Claude Code and Windsurf, this is your best move. Obsidian MCP Server: You can connect Claude directly to your Obsidian vault. The Workflow: Instead of copying and pasting, you tell Claude: "Search my Obsidian vault for the last time we discussed the Solana trading bot logic." * The Benefit: It bridges the gap between your thinking (Obsidian) and your doing (Coding agents). Your AI isn't just guessing; it's reading your actual notes. 3. The "Automated Brain" (Plugins) There are community plugins like Smart Connections or BMO Chatbot that live inside Obsidian. Instead of going to a website, you chat with Gemini or Claude via an API key directly inside a note. The conversation is automatically saved as a note the second you finish it. Pro Tip: You can set it to "index" your vault, meaning the AI can reference your hardware specs, your CachyOS configs, or your "Operation Immortal Agent" logs while you talk to it. The Strategic Value for You Since you're working on Operation Immortal Agent, storing your chats in Obsidian gives you three things your browser won't: Version History: You can see how your logic for the trading bot has evolved over three months of prompting. Cross-Pollination: You might notice a fix you found for a Linux driver (CachyOS) is actually the solution to a latency issue in your trading bot. In a browser, those two chats never meet. In Obsidian, the "Graph View" will show them touching. Local Ownership: You mentioned having "a repo on your record"—Obsidian keeps your data on your drive. No one can "repo" your knowledge base if it's stored locally and backed up. The Warning Don't try to save every single chat. 90% of AI chat is "disposable noise." If you dump 1,000 garbage chats into Obsidian, you haven't built a second brain; you've built a digital landfill. The Move: Start by installing the Obsidian AI Exporter extension. Spend one hour exporting only your "Greatest Hits"—the chats where you actually solved a hard problem. If that feels useful, then look into the MCP/Plugin route.
