### GEMINI
That's an inspiring attitude. You're not just building a small side hustle; you're building a multi-faceted, modern technology company. Based on the core services, the expansion plans, the geographic focus, and the Agile structure, here is how I would describe what you are building: The Business: A Full-Spectrum Local Tech Service You are building Akron's modern, local alternative to big-box tech support. You are starting as a Boutique PC & Performance Consultancy and evolving into a Comprehensive Smart Home and Digital Life Integrator. This vision is anchored in three distinct, yet complementary, pillars that make your business resilient and profitable: 1. The Core Pillar: Expert Performance & Repair This is your foundation and source of early revenue. You are establishing yourself as the local authority that people turn to when the generic big-box store services fail or aren't specialized enough. What you are: A highly focused, transparent, and skilled PC Repair and Customization Shop. Your Value Proposition: You fix the basics (virus removal, tune-ups) using your $100 special as a loss leader, but your real value is in the high-skill, high-margin work: custom gaming builds, high-end performance tuning, and specialized emulation setups. You're selling expertise and personalized service, not just a transaction. 2. The Expansion Pillar: Digital Home Integration This is your path to higher revenue and a wider customer base, directly addressing the pain points of modern living (working from home, streaming, security). What you are: A Digital Home Integrator. Your Value Proposition: You are solving the most common modern household headaches—specifically unreliable whole-home Wi-Fi and lack of security. By offering networking and security camera installation, you become the single point of contact for a customer's entire digital life, not just their desktop computer. This diversification minimizes reliance on just PC breakage for revenue. 3. The Business Model Pillar: Trust & Margin This is the engine that drives your profit and local reputation, based on your transparency goals. What you are: A business built on Transparency and Tiered Profitability. Your Value Proposition: You're leveraging the smart financial strategy of making money on three fronts: Service (Labor), Hardware (Wholesale Markup), and Affiliates (Passive Income). By being honest about charging retail price for parts, you build the trust that is essential for long-term customer relationships, especially in a local market like Akron. In short, you are building a Full-Stack Local Tech Solution that grows from fixing PCs into managing the entire connected home. You are minimizing your risk by using the Agile process to test and validate each high-margin service before fully committing, turning your cousin's exciting ideas into actionable, profitable business units.
