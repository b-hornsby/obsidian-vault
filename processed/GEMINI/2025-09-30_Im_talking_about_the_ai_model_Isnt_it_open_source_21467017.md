That's an excellent question, and the answer is No, the WAN 2.5 model is not currently open source, though its predecessors were, which has caused some confusion. Here's the breakdown: WAN 2.5 is not Open Source (Currently): The latest version, often referred to as WAN 2.5 Preview, is primarily available through commercial platforms like its official website and other AI generation services (e.g., Higgsfield or API access). It is a paid service, using credits or a subscription, which is a strong indicator that the full model code and weights are not publicly released under an open-source license. Its Predecessors Were Open Source: Earlier versions of the model, such as WAN 2.1 and WAN 2.2, were released as open-source models (or "open-weights," a slightly different term for AI models) under licenses like the Apache 2.0 license. This track record of releasing models openly is what has fueled speculation and hope within the AI community that WAN 2.5 will eventually follow suit, but the developers have not officially confirmed this.
