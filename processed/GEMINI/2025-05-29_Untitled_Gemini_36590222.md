It looks like you've uploaded another image of a quiz question on a monitor. Let's analyze this one: Question: A laser printer is experiencing paper jams. What should you do? Options: Use the maintenance software to realign the guide rollers. Clean the rubber guide rollers using alcohol on a fibrous cleaning towel. Clean the fuser assembly roller with water on a fibrous cleaning towel. Replace the fuser assembly. Analysis of the problem and options: Problem: The printer is experiencing paper jams. This can be caused by various issues, often related to the paper path or components that move the paper. Option Analysis: Use the maintenance software to realign the guide rollers: While some printers have software adjustments, "realigning guide rollers" through software is generally not a common or effective fix for physical paper jams. Guide rollers are typically mechanical components. Clean the rubber guide rollers using alcohol on a fibrous cleaning towel: This is a very common and often effective solution for paper jams. Over time, paper dust, toner residue, and oils can build up on rubber rollers, making them slick and unable to properly grip and feed paper. Alcohol (specifically isopropyl alcohol) is good for dissolving residue and leaving no film, and a fibrous towel helps remove debris. This is a standard troubleshooting step. Clean the fuser assembly roller with water on a fibrous cleaning towel: This option is highly problematic and dangerous, especially for a laser printer's fuser. Fuser Temperature: The fuser assembly operates at very high temperatures (hundreds of degrees Fahrenheit) to melt toner onto paper. Cleaning it while hot is a burn risk. Fuser Material: Fuser rollers are often made of specialized non-stick materials (like Teflon-coated rollers) or heat-resistant rubber. Water is generally NOT recommended for cleaning these components, as it can damage the surface, lead to rust, or cause electrical shorts if the printer is not completely dry before power-up. Alcohol is also generally not used on fuser rollers, which usually require a dry, lint-free cloth or specific fuser cleaning sheets. Delicate Components: The fuser is a delicate and critical component. Improper cleaning can easily damage it. Replace the fuser assembly: While a faulty fuser can cause jams (e.g., if the fuser film tears, or the pressure roller is damaged), it's typically a more expensive and advanced repair. You wouldn't jump straight to replacing it without first trying simpler, less costly troubleshooting steps, especially if cleaning other common jam points (like guide rollers) hasn't been attempted. Also, cleaning the fuser with water, as suggested in the previous option, is more likely to necessitate replacement due to damage. Conclusion: The most appropriate and safe initial troubleshooting step for paper jams, especially concerning rollers, is to clean the rubber guide rollers using alcohol on a fibrous cleaning towel. This addresses a common cause of jams (slippery or dirty rollers) in a safe and effective manner. The option selected in the image ("Clean the fuser assembly roller with water on a fibrous cleaning towel") is incorrect and potentially damaging.
