# i hear restream is pretty buggy 

### USER
i hear restream is pretty buggy 

### GEMINI
You've brought up a very valid point. While Restream.io is a popular and convenient solution, it's a known fact that any service that acts as a middleman can introduce potential points of failure or "bugs." User reviews and forum discussions do occasionally mention issues. Here's a balanced view of the common "buggy" claims and why they might occur, along with why it's still often a viable choice for a user with your specs: Common Buggy Claims and Their Causes Lag and Dropped Frames: This is the most common complaint. Users will say their stream to Restream is "laggy" or "dropping frames," but when they stream directly to Twitch, the problem disappears. Likely Cause: This is almost always an issue with the user's internet connection to Restream's specific server, not a bug in the software itself. The routing from your ISP to Restream's closest server may be unstable. Restream has servers all over the world, and if you're on a suboptimal one, you could experience issues. Solution: Restream has a built-in server speed test. Before you go live, you can check which server location gives you the fastest and most stable connection and manually select it in OBS. Stream Disconnects: Users report that their entire stream will randomly disconnect from Restream's servers. Likely Cause: Again, this is often a network stability issue. While your internet speed might be good, a wobbly connection can cause a complete disconnect. Solution: Restream has a 24/7 live chat support team that can help you diagnose these specific issues by looking at your stream logs. They can tell you exactly why the stream disconnected. Out-of-Sync Audio/Video: Some users have reported that their audio and video get out of sync on one or more of their streaming platforms. Likely Cause: This can be due to a bug on a specific platform's side or an issue with the encoding settings. Solution: Adjusting settings like the keyframe interval in OBS can sometimes fix this. If the issue persists on a single platform (e.g., just TikTok), it may be a problem with how that platform receives and processes the stream. Why Restream is Still a Good Choice for You Despite these potential issues, Restream is often the best solution for the reasons we've already covered, especially given your PC's power: You can skip the TikTok stream key hassle. This is a huge advantage. As we discussed, obtaining the key can be a major barrier, and Restream's direct integration bypasses it completely. Reduced Load on Your PC: This is the primary benefit. While your PC can handle direct multistreaming with Aitum Multistream, the Restream model is more efficient. You are only encoding and uploading one video stream. This leaves your CPU and GPU completely free to handle gaming and other tasks. Reliability for Most Users: The vast majority of Restream's 2 million+ users have a positive experience. The "bugs" and issues are usually tied to individual network setups or temporary server problems, which Restream is generally quick to address. The Aitum Multistream Alternative Your concerns about Restream's bugginess make Aitum Multistream a very appealing alternative. Pros: You have complete control over every aspect of your stream. You're not relying on a third-party server to distribute your content. Your powerful PC can handle this without breaking a sweat, and if an issue occurs, it's most likely an issue with your own internet connection, not a middleman service. Cons: The primary downside is the TikTok stream key issue. If you cannot get the stream key, you simply cannot use Aitum Multistream to broadcast to TikTok. You would have to use TikTok LIVE Studio or another solution for that platform. Final Conclusion Given your initial goal of a stable stream on both platforms and the potential roadblocks with TikTok's stream keys, I would recommend the following: Start with Restream's free tier. It's the path of least resistance. You can connect to Twitch and TikTok without needing a stream key. Run a speed test to Restream's servers. In your Restream dashboard, find their speed test tool and make sure you're connecting to the best possible server for your location. Test your stream. Do a test broadcast to your channels and monitor for any issues. If you experience problems, don't give up immediately. Reach out to Restream's support, as they are very responsive and can almost always diagnose the issue. Your PC is a powerhouse. The only thing that will hold you back is your internet connection, and Restream's "bugginess" is often just a symptom of a user's network or the routing between their ISP and a server. You have the hardware to handle any solution you choose. The choice now is between the convenience of a service like Restream and the full control of a plugin like Aitum Multistream.
