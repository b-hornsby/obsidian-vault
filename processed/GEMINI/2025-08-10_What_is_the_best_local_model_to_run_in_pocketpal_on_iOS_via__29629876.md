# What is the best local model to run in pocketpal on iOS via an iPhone 15 plus? 

### USER
What is the best local model to run in pocketpal on iOS via an iPhone 15 plus? 

### GEMINI
When choosing the "best" local model to run on an iPhone 15 Plus using an app like PocketPal, you're balancing several factors: performance (speed), model quality (how good the responses are), and model size. The iPhone 15 Plus has a powerful A16 Bionic chip and ample RAM, which allows it to handle more capable models than older phones, but you still need to be mindful of resources. PocketPal supports models in the GGUF format, which are specifically optimized for on-device use. The app also lets you download models directly from Hugging Face, so you have a lot of flexibility. Here's a breakdown of what to consider and some potential models to try: Key Factors for Model Selection Model Size and Quantization: A model's size is often measured in billions of parameters (e.g., 3B, 7B, 8B). Larger models are generally more capable but are also more demanding on your phone's processor and RAM. Quantization refers to a process that compresses a model to make it smaller and faster, often with a slight trade-off in quality. GGUF models come in different quantization levels (e.g., Q4_K_M, Q5_K_M), with lower numbers representing more compression and higher numbers representing better quality but larger files. Performance vs. Quality: A smaller model (like a 3B or 4B) will run very quickly and be good for simple tasks, but may not be as smart or detailed as a larger model. A larger model (like a 7B or 8B) will provide better quality responses but may be noticeably slower. The iPhone 15 Plus can likely handle 7B models and maybe even some larger ones, but you'll need to benchmark them yourself to see what you find acceptable. PocketPal has a benchmarking feature to help with this. Model Family: Different model families have different strengths. For example, models like Llama, Gemma, and Mistral are popular and well-regarded for their general performance. PocketPal mentions supporting models like Danube 2 and 3, Phi, Gemma 2, and Qwen, which are all good starting points. Recommended Models to Consider For a Balance of Speed and Quality: Look for well-quantized 7B or 8B models. Popular choices include: Llama 3 8B Instruct: This is a very popular and highly capable model. A good GGUF quantization (like Q4_K_M or Q5_K_M) should run well on your iPhone. Mistral 7B Instruct: Known for its good performance, even in a smaller package. It's a great all-around choice. Gemma 7B Instruct: Another powerful model from Google that offers strong performance for its size. For Maximum Speed: If you prioritize instant responses, even at the cost of some quality, try smaller models. Llama 3 3B or 4B Instruct: These will be extremely fast and still capable of handling many day-to-day tasks. Phi-3-mini: Microsoft's small language model is known for being surprisingly capable for its size and would be a great option for a fast, on-device assistant. For the Best Possible Quality: While a larger model might push the limits, you could experiment with a highly-quantized 13B model. Be prepared for slower generation speeds and potentially more memory usage, which could lead to stability issues depending on the model and the app's efficiency. The best way to find the perfect model for you is to download a few different options from Hugging Face within the PocketPal app and test them out. Since the app is free and you can swap between models, you can easily find the one that strikes the right balance of speed and quality for your needs.
