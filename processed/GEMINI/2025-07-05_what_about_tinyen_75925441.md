# what about tiny.en?

### USER
what about tiny.en?

### GEMINI
Regarding tiny.en for the Whisper STT model, here's how it fits into the optimization for speed: tiny.en as the Fastest STT Setting: If your absolute top priority is the fastest possible Speech-to-Text (STT) transcription, then tiny.en would be the optimal choice for the Whisper Model setting. It is the smallest and therefore the quickest model in the Whisper family. Trade-offs with tiny.en: Accuracy: The main drawback of tiny.en is that it will be less accurate than base.en, small.en, or medium.en. It's more prone to errors, especially with accents, background noise, or complex terminology. Helpdesk Use Case: For a helpdesk AI, a balance between speed and accuracy is often desired. If tiny.en leads to frequent transcription errors, the AI might misinterpret user questions, leading to incorrect or irrelevant responses, which can be more frustrating than a slight delay. Recommendation: Try tiny.en if you are finding base.en or small.en still too slow for the STT portion. Test it thoroughly by speaking various types of questions, including those with specific technical terms your helpdesk might encounter. Observe the AI's responses: If the AI starts giving irrelevant answers because it's misunderstanding your input due to poor transcription, then tiny.en might be too aggressive, and you might need to revert to base.en or small.en for better accuracy. To change it: Go to your Oobabooga UI. Navigate to the Whisper STT section in your settings. Change the Whisper Model dropdown to tiny.en. Remember to Save settings and restart the server from the Extensions tab for the change to take effect.
