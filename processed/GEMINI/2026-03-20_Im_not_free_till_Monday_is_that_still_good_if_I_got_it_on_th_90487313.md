# I’m not free till Monday is that still good if I got it on the 3rd of march?

### USER
I’m not free till Monday is that still good if I got it on the 3rd of march?

### GEMINI
Monday, March 23rd, is perfectly fine. You are actually well ahead of the deadline. In Ohio, you have 28 days from the date you were served (the day you "got" the papers) to file your Answer. Here is the math for your situation: Date Served: March 3rd Day 1 of counting: March 4th Day 28 (The Deadline): March 31st By going on Monday, March 23rd, you are beating the deadline by 8 full days. Why filing early (Monday) is a "power move": Buffer Room: If the Clerk finds a mistake in your paperwork or asks for an extra document, you still have a week to fix it. Stopping the Clock: The second that Clerk stamps your papers on Monday, the bank's ability to get a "Default Judgment" is officially dead. They can no longer "win by default" because you have officially entered the fight. IT Mindset: Think of this like a server migration. You don't wait until the last minute of the maintenance window to start. Doing it Monday gives you the peace of mind to focus on your job search for the rest of the week. Your Monday Morning "Go-Bag" Checklist: The Answer: 3 identical copies (with your signature on all 3). Form 20 (Waiver): 1 copy (filled out, but DO NOT SIGN until you're at the window). Your ID: Driver's License or State ID. Case Number: Make sure it's on every page. Since you're going Monday, would you like me to find the exact floor and suite number for the Civil Clerk at the Stow Municipal Court so you don't waste any time wandering around the building?
