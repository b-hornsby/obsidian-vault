# How should I address the letters for each?

### USER
How should I address the letters for each?

### GEMINI
You should address each envelope as follows to ensure they reach the correct legal departments and court officials. 1. The Court Filing (Hand-Delivery) Since you are driving to the Stow Municipal Court to file your Form 20 and Answer in person, you don’t need an envelope for this step, but you should head to the Civil Division at this address: Stow Municipal Court Attn: Clerk of Court (Civil Division) 4400 Courthouse Blvd. Stow, OH 44224 2. The Lawyer for the $8,000 Lawsuit (First-Class Mail) After the Clerk stamps your copies of the Answer, place one of those stamped copies in a standard envelope addressed to the bank’s attorney: James P. McGowan 4511 Rockside Road, Suite 210 Independence, OH 44131 3. The New Collection Notice for $7,000 (Certified Mail) Address the envelope for your Debt Validation Letter exactly as it appears on the notice you received. Use Certified Mail with Return Receipt Requested for this one: Weltman, Weinberg & Reis Co., LPA 5990 West Creek Rd, Suite 200 Cleveland, OH 44131 Quick Address Tip for the Post Office: Even though Weltman (WWR) is physically located in Independence, their official mailing address for collections often uses Cleveland. Stick to the Cleveland, OH 44131 address on your envelope to match their letterhead exactly.
