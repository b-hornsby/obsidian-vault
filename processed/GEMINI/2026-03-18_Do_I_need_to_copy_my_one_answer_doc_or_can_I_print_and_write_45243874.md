# Do I need to copy my one answer doc or can I print and write it out 3 times?

### USER
Do I need to copy my one answer doc or can I print and write it out 3 times?

### GEMINI
You can absolutely write it out three times by hand. The court doesn't care if it's a photocopy or an original handwritten version, as long as all three are identical and have your original signature on them. However, if you have access to a library or a FedEx Office, making two photocopies of your one finished "master" version is usually safer because it ensures there are zero typos or differences between what the Court has and what the Lawyer has. If you choose to write it out 3 times: The Content: Every single word, including the Case Number and the "Deny" sentences, must be the same on all three. The Signature: You must sign all three individually. The Date: Use the same date on all three (the date you plan to walk into the court). Your "Final Three" Checklist: Copy 1 (The Court’s): You hand this to the Clerk. They keep it. Copy 2 (The Lawyer’s): The Clerk stamps this and gives it back to you. You mail this one to James McGowan in Independence. Copy 3 (Yours): The Clerk stamps this and gives it back to you. You keep this in a safe folder. This is your "Get Out of Jail Free" card if they ever try to claim you didn't answer. One Strategic Detail: When you get to the Stow Municipal Court (4400 Courthouse Blvd), look for the window marked "Civil Division." The Script: "Hi, I'm here to file an Answer in Case [Your Case Number]. I also have a Form 20 Fee Waiver I need to sign in front of you." Would you like me to find the closest place in Akron where you can make those two photocopies for about 20 cents so you don't have to write it all out three times?
