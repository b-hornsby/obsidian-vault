# Hey, I'm making some ribs and I was wondering how you go about the process. Do you put the mustard on and then the seasoning on afterwards?

### USER
Hey, I'm making some ribs and I was wondering how you go about the process. Do you put the mustard on and then the seasoning on afterwards?

### GEMINI
It's really up to you and what kind of flavor you're going for. Some people like to put the mustard on first as a binder for the rub, while others add it later. Do you prefer a strong mustard flavor, or are you looking for something more subtle?
