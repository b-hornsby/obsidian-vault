# With that 3 step plan what about the civil fee waiver? Where does that fit in?

### USER
With that 3 step plan what about the civil fee waiver? Where does that fit in?

### GEMINI
Actually, there’s a bit of good news here: in Stow Municipal Court, it is currently free to file an Answer. You don't need a fee waiver just to respond to the lawsuit. However, the Civil Fee Waiver (officially called an "Affidavit of Indigency") is still a vital tool for you. Here is where it fits into your 3-step plan: 1. It’s Your "Financial Shield" If the case moves forward and you need to file other documents (like a motion to dismiss or a counterclaim), the court usually charges fees. By filing the Civil Fee Waiver Affidavit now, you are pre-emptively telling the Judge: "I am low-income, and I cannot afford court costs." Since you make $11/hour, you fall well below the threshold (187.5% of the Federal Poverty Level) where the court must waive your fees under Ohio law. 2. How to Use It You should file this at the same time you file your Answer. The Form: Ask the Clerk for "Form 20" or the "Civil Fee Waiver Affidavit." The Process: You’ll list your income ($11/hr) and your basic expenses (rent, food, phone). The Notary: You must sign this in front of a Notary. The Clerk’s office at the Stow Courthouse usually has a Notary on-site who can do this for you for free or a very small fee. Do not sign it until you are standing in front of them. 3.
