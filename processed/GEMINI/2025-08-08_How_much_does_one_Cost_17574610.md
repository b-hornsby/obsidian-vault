# How much does one Cost?

### USER
How much does one Cost?

### GEMINI
The pricing for a Mac Studio with the specifications you need can vary depending on the exact configuration and where you purchase it. However, based on the most recent information, a Mac Studio with an M4 Max chip and 128GB of unified memory starts at approximately $3,499 directly from Apple. It's important to note that this is the starting price for a model with 128GB of RAM. The final price will increase if you choose to upgrade other components, such as the SSD storage. For example, a Mac Studio with the same M4 Max chip, 128GB of memory, but with a 1TB SSD could cost around $3,699.
