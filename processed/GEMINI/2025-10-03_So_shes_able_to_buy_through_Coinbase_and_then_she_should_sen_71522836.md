So she’s able to buy through Coinbase and then she should send it to phantom or another wallet like that?

### GEMINI
Yes, that is exactly the correct and easiest path forward. She buys the $\text{TROLL}$ token on the Coinbase exchange, and then she sends (withdraws) it to a Solana-compatible self-custodial wallet like Phantom. Here is a summary of the steps and why this is the best approach for her: The Recommended Easiest Path Purchase on Coinbase (Centralized Exchange/Custodial): Your aunt uses her Coinbase account to buy the $\text{TROLL}$ token (which is listed on the exchange). Set Up Phantom (Self-Custodial Wallet): She downloads the Phantom Wallet app (or Exodus/Solflare). She creates the wallet and writes down her Secret Recovery Phrase on paper. This step gives her control. Transfer (Withdraw) to Phantom: She opens Phantom and gets the receive address for $\text{TROLL}$ (making sure it's the Solana network address). She goes to her Coinbase account, selects "Send," pastes the Phantom address, selects the Solana network, and sends the $\text{TROLL}$. Why Phantom is the Best Choice for Her Solana Native: Phantom is the most widely used and user-friendly wallet specifically built for the Solana ecosystem, which is where her $\text{TROLL}$ token resides. Simple Interface: It's designed with a clean, intuitive interface, making it easy for a beginner to manage her assets, see her balance, and handle transactions. Security Focus: It gives her full self-custody over her funds, which is your goal. Critical Reminder Reiterate to her the irreversible nature of crypto transfers and the importance of the Secret Recovery Phrase: Network Check: Always confirm she is sending the $\text{TROLL}$ on the Solana network to a Solana-compatible address. Sending to the wrong network (like an Ethereum address) will result in permanent loss. Test Transfer: For her very first transfer, she should always send a small, nominal amount first (e.g., $10 worth) to confirm the funds arrive in her Phantom wallet safely before sending the entire balance. The Keys: The 12 or 24 words of her Secret Recovery Phrase are her money. If she loses them, the money is gone. If they are compromised, the money can be stolen. They must be stored offline and never typed into any website.
