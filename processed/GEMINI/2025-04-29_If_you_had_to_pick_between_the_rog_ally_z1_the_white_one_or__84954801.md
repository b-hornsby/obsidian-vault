# If you had to pick between the rog ally z1 (the white one) or the steam deck oled as sort of an extension of your desktop and just for overall enjoyment, which would you pick? 

### USER
If you had to pick between the rog ally z1 (the white one) or the steam deck oled as sort of an extension of your desktop and just for overall enjoyment, which would you pick? 

### GEMINI
Choosing between the ROG Ally Z1 (the white one) and the Steam Deck OLED as an extension of your desktop and for overall enjoyment depends largely on your priorities. Here's a breakdown to help you decide: ASUS ROG Ally Z1 (White): Pros: More powerful on paper: The Z1 chip offers more raw processing power than the Steam Deck OLED, potentially leading to better performance in demanding games, especially when docked. Higher refresh rate screen: The 120Hz VRR (Variable Refresh Rate) display offers a smoother visual experience, which can be particularly noticeable in fast-paced games. Runs full Windows 11: This provides wider compatibility with game launchers (Steam, Epic Games Store, Xbox Game Pass, etc.) and applications, making it a more versatile "extension" of your desktop. You can also run non-gaming software. Potentially better performance when docked: With more power, it might offer a slightly better experience when connected to an external monitor for desktop-like use. Lighter and more ergonomic for some: Some users find the Ally's design more comfortable for extended handheld play. Upgradable storage using a more common 2280 SSD size. Cons: Lower battery life: Generally, the ROG Ally has a shorter battery life compared to the Steam Deck OLED, especially when running demanding games. Windows can be less optimized for handheld gaming: While ASUS's Armoury Crate helps, the Windows experience might not be as seamless as SteamOS for handheld gaming. Screen is LCD, not OLED: While it has a higher refresh rate and resolution (1080p), it lacks the vibrant colors and deep blacks of an OLED display. Potentially louder fan noise under load. Can be more expensive than the Steam Deck OLED. Steam Deck OLED: Pros: Stunning OLED screen: Offers superior contrast, color vibrancy, and deeper blacks for a more immersive visual experience. Better battery life: Generally lasts longer on a single charge compared to the ROG Ally, allowing for more extended playtime. Optimized SteamOS: Designed specifically for handheld gaming, offering a more console-like and user-friendly experience, especially for games in your Steam library. Excellent emulation support: SteamOS and the community around it make it a fantastic device for retro gaming emulation. Quieter operation: Generally produces less fan noise compared to the ROG Ally. Lower starting price point. Improved Wi-Fi 6E for faster downloads on compatible networks. Cons: Less powerful than the ROG Ally Z1: Might struggle with the absolute latest and most demanding AAA games at higher settings. Lower refresh rate (90Hz) and resolution (800p): The display is not as sharp or smooth as the ROG Ally's. SteamOS compatibility limitations: While improving, it might not natively support all game launchers and Windows-specific applications as easily as the ROG Ally. Larger and heavier: Some users might find it less comfortable for very long gaming sessions. Storage upgrades can be more expensive due to the 2230 SSD size. Which to Pick for You: For a better "extension of your desktop" and wider compatibility: The ROG Ally Z1 might be a better fit due to its Windows operating system, allowing you to run a broader range of applications and game launchers more easily. If having the flexibility of Windows is crucial, the Ally has the edge. For overall enjoyment, especially focused on gaming and a more premium visual experience: The Steam Deck OLED is a strong contender. Its stunning screen, better battery life, and optimized SteamOS provide a fantastic handheld gaming experience, particularly for games available on Steam and for emulation. Consider these additional points: Your game library: If most of your games are on Steam, the Steam Deck OLED offers a seamless integration. If you have a significant library on other launchers or want to play games with anti-cheat systems that might not work well on Linux-based SteamOS, the Ally's Windows environment is more accommodating. Docked usage: If you plan to use it frequently docked to a monitor, the ROG Ally's potentially higher performance could be more beneficial. Tinkering and customization: The Steam Deck and its community are very open to customization and alternative operating systems. The ROG Ally, while running Windows, also has its own community and software ecosystem. In conclusion: If having the flexibility of Windows and potentially higher docked performance is your priority, the ROG Ally Z1 could be the better choice. However, if you prioritize a superior visual experience, longer battery life, a more streamlined handheld gaming OS, and primarily play games on Steam, the Steam Deck OLED would likely offer more overall enjoyment as a handheld gaming device.
