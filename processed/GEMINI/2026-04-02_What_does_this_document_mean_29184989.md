You have the right to ask them for the "Chain of Title" or the original contract. They will likely send you "Requests for Admission" (questions asking you to admit you owe the money). You must answer these by June 10th. Motion Deadline (June 25, 2026): This is the last day to file a "Motion for Summary Judgment." This is a request for the judge to decide the case without a trial because the facts are supposedly undisputed. Trial Date (July 30, 2026 at 3:15 PM): This is the "End Game." If you haven't settled the case by this date, you must show up at the courthouse to present your defense to Magistrate Clark. Crucial Warnings in the Order "Failure to attend the pre-trial... or failure to attend the trial may result in sanctions... or permitting the Plaintiff to proceed with the case... ex parte." The Translation: If you miss that July 30th date, they win instantly. Mark it in your calendar with three reminders. The "Full Settlement Authority" Rule: The court expects you to be ready to negotiate. Since your goal is to show you are "judgment proof" (low income), this is when you would discuss a low-cost settlement or a dismissal if they can't prove they own the debt. If you don't answer them, the court legally considers your silence as an admission that everything they say is true.
