It
 absolutely is a great talking point — and honestly the differentiation 
argument is even stronger there than with Arch. Most IT candidates have 
zero idea what a hardware wallet is, let alone self-custody, DeFi 
protocols, or how Solana works under the hood.
The question is just how you work it in without it overshadowing the IT angle or triggering the wrong reaction from conservative hiring managers.
A few ways to thread that needle:
Option 1 - Subtle:
"IT Operations | Cross-Platform (Arch/CachyOS · Win · Mac) | AI & Web3 Automation"
Option 2 - A bit bolder:
"IT Operations & Systems | Linux (Arch/CachyOS) | AI Automation | Web3 Ops"
Option 3 - Let it breathe more:
"IT Operations | Linux · Windows · macOS | Web3 & AI Automation"
Option 3 actually flows the best out loud in my opinion — it has a rhythm to it. And pairing Web3 with AI softens it for skeptical recruiters while still making it a clear talking point for the right ones.
What's your gut on those? Because honestly if Web3 feels like you, it should be in there — authenticity in a profile matters more than playing it safe.
