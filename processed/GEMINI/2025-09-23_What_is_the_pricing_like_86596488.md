# What is the pricing like?

### USER
What is the pricing like?

### GEMINI
Claude Code's pricing is structured in two main ways: subscription plans and pay-per-use API access. Subscription Plans The subscription plans are for general use of the Claude.ai platform, but the paid tiers also include access to Claude Code. These plans are best for individuals and teams who want a simple, predictable monthly cost. Free Plan: This is the base tier and is free to use. However, it does not include access to Claude Code. It's primarily for using the web chat interface with limited usage of models like Sonnet. Pro Plan: This is for power users and developers. It costs around $20 per month (often with an annual discount). It gives you significantly more usage than the free tier and includes access to models like Claude 4 Sonnet, which is the recommended model for most coding tasks. Max Plans: For heavy users, there are two "Max" tiers: Max 5x: Around $100 per month, offering 5 times the usage of the Pro plan. This tier is for frequent users and gives you more access to the most capable model, Claude 4.1 Opus. Max 20x: Around $200 per month, offering 20 times the usage of the Pro plan. This is for professional developers who use Claude Code as a core part of their daily workflow and need a high volume of requests. Team and Enterprise Plans: These are for businesses and are priced per user. The Team plan, for example, costs around $25-30 per user per month with a minimum number of users. These plans include all Pro features, plus administrative tools and more robust security. Some of these plans may have a separate "Premium" add-on for Claude Code access. Pay-Per-Use API For developers building applications or running automated workflows, the API pricing is token-based. You pay for what you use, and the cost depends on the model you select and the number of tokens (words or pieces of words) in your input and output. Claude 4 Sonnet: This model is the most balanced in terms of cost and capability. As of late 2025, it costs roughly $3.00 per million input tokens and $15.00 per million output tokens. For prompts over 200,000 tokens, the cost may increase. Claude 4.1 Opus: This is the most powerful and most expensive model. It's best for complex reasoning and tasks. The cost is approximately $15.00 per million input tokens and $75.00 per million output tokens. Claude 3.5 Haiku: This is the fastest and most affordable model, ideal for simple, high-volume tasks. It costs around $0.80 per million input tokens and $4.00 per million output tokens. The pricing for both subscriptions and the API can be a little confusing, and the actual cost depends heavily on how you use the tool. Developers have noted that even on subscription plans, it's possible to hit rate limits, and the most capable models like Opus consume tokens much more quickly, leading to higher costs.
