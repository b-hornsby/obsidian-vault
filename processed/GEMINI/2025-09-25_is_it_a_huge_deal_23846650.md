### GEMINI
Generally, no, it's not a huge deal in terms of an immediate threat to your system, but it's a significant inconvenience and a potential sign of a deeper issue that could impact you later. Here is a breakdown of the risks: 1. The Risk of the Ghost Scene Itself: LOW It's Likely a Display Glitch: Since it's unclickable, it's almost certainly a ghost entry—a display bug from the Aitum plugin's internal management (Multistream or Vertical) that OBS didn't correctly hide. Minimal Performance Impact: A phantom scene that isn't actively selected or running sources usually has little to no impact on your CPU/GPU performance. OBS only renders the active scene. 2. The Risk to Stream Stability and Use: MEDIUM This is where the problem becomes an issue you shouldn't ignore: Hotkeys/Scene Switching: If the ghost scene is between your legitimate scenes, it can throw off your numbering, making it confusing to switch scenes, especially if you rely on third-party tools like a Stream Deck or keyboard shortcuts that switch to the "next" or "previous" scene. Scene Duplication/Corruption: This kind of display bug is sometimes a precursor to more serious issues, such as scene settings getting corrupted, or the Aitum plugin failing to manage linked scenes correctly. Some users have reported issues with scene duplication or high resource usage tied to Aitum/OBS conflicts. The Unkown Factor: Because you can't click it, you don't know if it contains any active sources (like a runaway browser source) that could be silently consuming resources. The Bottom Line If your streams are working perfectly right now, you can likely ignore it for a single session. However, you should treat it as a serious bug to resolve as soon as possible because it is a clear indicator that the Aitum/OBS integration is not perfectly stable on your system. Your best next steps are to check for updates and seek specific support: Restart (If you haven't yet): Close OBS and the Aitum App completely, then restart both. This is the most common fix. Update: Ensure you are on the latest versions of OBS Studio and the Aitum plugin. Aitum Support: Search the official Aitum Discord or support channels for anyone else reporting an "unclickable scene" or "phantom scene." They will have the most specific and up-to-date fix for their own plugin.
