# Can I get the form 20 online?

### USER
Can I get the form 20 online?

### GEMINI
Yes, you can get it online. Form 20 is a standardized form from the Ohio Supreme Court that must be accepted by every court in the state, including the Stow Municipal Court. 1. Download the Forms You need two specific documents. Download them here: Form 20: Civil Fee Waiver Affidavit (PDF) – This is the form you’ll use to prove your $11/hour income. The "Answer" Form: Stow doesn't have a fillable PDF for this. As the court's website says, you can just use a plain sheet of 8.5" x 11" paper. (I have provided a template for you below). 2. How to Fill Out Form 20 Since you are making $11/hour, focus on these sections: Public Benefits: If you receive SNAP (Food Stamps) or Medicaid, check those boxes. This makes the waiver almost automatic. Income: List your $11/hour wages. If you work 40 hours, your gross monthly is about $1,906. Expenses: Be honest and detailed about your rent, phone bill, groceries, and gas. Since you "can't afford anything," your expenses should clearly meet or exceed your income. IMPORTANT: Do not sign it yet. You must sign it in front of a Notary or the Clerk at the courthouse so they can witness it. 3. Your "Answer" Template (Copy this) Since you're doing this yourself, copy this onto a piece of paper or into a Word doc: IN THE STOW MUNICIPAL COURT SUMMIT COUNTY, OHIO [Plaintiff Name from Summons] vs. Bryan [Your Last Name] Case No: [Copy from your papers] DEFENDANT’S ANSWER Defendant denies the allegations in Paragraph 1 of the Complaint for lack of knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief as to the truth of the allegations. Defendant denies the allegations in Paragraph 2 [Repeat this for every numbered paragraph in their complaint]. CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE I certify that a copy of this Answer was sent by ordinary U.S. Mail on [Date] to Counsel for Plaintiff, [Attorney Name and Address from Summons]. [Your Signature] 4. The Final "Logistics" Move Print the Form 20 and your Answer. Go to the Court (4400 Courthouse Blvd, Stow). Sign the Form 20 in front of the Clerk. Hand both to the Clerk. Cost = $0. Mail the copy to the lawyer. The $8k debt is a big number, but at $11/hr, these two pieces of paper are your best defense. They tell the collector you aren't an easy win and you have no assets to seize.
