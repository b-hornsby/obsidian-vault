Prompt Engineering (Specifically for your "vibe coding" and agentic work). Offline/Local LLM Deployment (This is your unique selling point—mentioning local inference is rare). Information Technology Operations (IT Ops) (Bridges your 8 years of logistics experience with tech). Computer Hardware Troubleshooting (Leverages your custom build and Steam Deck optimization experience). Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) (The "bridge" skill from your warehouse background). Network Administration (Justified by your work with Tailscale, Syncthing, and secure data movement). The Brutal Truth on "ERP Software" Don't just list "ERP Software." The Prioritized Plan: Kill "Forklift Operation" immediately. It is an "anchor" that keeps you in the warehouse. Replace "Gen Tech Support" with IT Operations. It sounds more senior and reflects your 8 years of experience. Add "Computer Hardware"—you know your specs (i7-12700K, etc.) better than most people already in IT roles.
