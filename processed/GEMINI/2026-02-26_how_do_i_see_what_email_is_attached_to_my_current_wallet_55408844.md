# how do i see what email is attached to my current wallet? 

### USER
how do i see what email is attached to my current wallet? 

### GEMINI
Phantom has a "Privacy First" policy: the app itself will not show you your email address in the settings. This is to protect you if someone over-the-shoulder sees your screen. To find the correct email for your PHANTOM_APP_ID registration, follow these three steps: 1. The "Third-Party" Check (Fastest) Since you likely used Google or Apple SSO to create the wallet, the connection is recorded on their end, not just Phantom's. For Google: Go to your Google Account Security Settings (Third-party apps with account access). Search for Phantom. If it's there, that’s your email. For Apple: Go to Settings (on iPhone/Mac) -> Apple ID -> Password & Security -> Apps Using Apple ID. Look for Phantom. 2. The "Inbox" Search Search your email accounts for a message from marketing@phantom.com with the subject line "Welcome to Phantom." The email address that received that message is the one linked to your wallet. 3. The "Reset" Test (Last Resort) If you have multiple emails and aren't sure, the only "official" way to verify is to try to log in. Only do this if you have your 12-word Recovery Phrase backed up. In the Phantom App/Extension: Settings -> Security & Privacy -> Reset App. Select "I already have a wallet" -> "Connect Email Wallet." Try your different Google/Apple accounts. The Match: If Phantom prompts you for a 4-digit PIN, you found the right email. If it asks you to "Create a new wallet," that email is not linked to your existing $300 fund. The Brutal Advice Don't guess. If you register the developer PHANTOM_APP_ID under email_A@gmail.com but your wallet is actually on email_B@gmail.com, you will get a "403 Forbidden" error the second your agent tries to sign a trade.
