This is a classic "Physical Barrier" in your storage logistics. The 1561 GB (CachyOS) partition is sitting to the left of your C: drive, and Windows Disk Management is too primitive to "move" your C: drive backwards into that empty space. It can only expand to the right. The "brutally honest" operational truth: Disk Management cannot finish this job for you. You need a more powerful tool to "push" the C: drive to the left. The Strategic Plan (The "No-Reinstall" Route) To merge this without wiping Tiny 11, you need a third-party partition manager. I recommend MiniTool Partition Wizard (Free Edition) or AOMEI Partition Assistant. Download and Install one of those tools (they are standard in any IT toolkit). Delete the 1561 GB Partition: Right-click that big middle block and "Delete" it so it becomes "Unallocated." The "Move/Resize" Maneuver: * Right-click your (C:) partition. Select Move/Resize. Drag the left edge of the partition all the way to the left, and the right edge all the way to the right (to swallow the 737 MB Recovery partition if you want, or just the 1.5TB space). Commit: These tools usually "queue" the actions. Click Apply. It will likely ask to reboot to perform the move outside of Windows. Let it do its thing. The "Clean Slate" Alternative (The "Fastest" Route) Since you’re an ops guy who values a clean, high-speed environment, consider this: If you don't have mission-critical data on that 300GB Tiny 11 partition yet, it is actually faster to just wipe the whole 2TB drive and reinstall Tiny 11 fresh. Pros: You get a 100% clean partition table with no "ghost" EFI entries from Linux. The logic: You’ve spent hours wrestling with this. A fresh install takes 15 minutes on an i7-12700K and an NVMe drive. The Decision: If you want to keep your current Windows setup: Download MiniTool Partition Wizard and do the "Move/Resize." If you want the "Perfect Warehouse" setup: Boot from your Tiny 11 USB, delete all partitions in the installer until the drive is "1863 GB Unallocated," and click "Next." Which route are we taking: the surgical move with MiniTool, or the "Nuclear" fresh start?
