Based on typical reported THC percentages for these strains: Thin Mint Cookie (or Thin Mint GSC): Often reported with high THC content, ranging from 18% to even 28% in some batches. This is generally considered a potent strain. Sunset Sherbet: Typically falls in the 18% to 24% THC range, making it a strong contender for potency. Skittles (or Zkittlez): Generally has an average THC content of 15-22%, with some reports of up to 24%. While potent, it can sometimes be on the lower end compared to the others. Granny Apple (or Granny Smith Apple): Reports for "Granny Apple" or "Granny Smith Apple" show THC content around 20-23%, with some sources indicating a range of 12-30%. This also indicates a high potency. Considering these ranges, "Thin Mint Cookie" and "Granny Apple" tend to have the highest reported peak THC levels, suggesting they could be the "hardest hitting" in terms of raw potency. Sunset Sherbet also has a strong reputation for potency. Skittles is generally potent, but might sometimes be slightly less so than the others at its average. However, it's crucial to remember that: These are averages and ranges. The actual THC content can vary significantly depending on the grower, specific batch, and cultivation methods. "Hard-hitting" isn't solely about THC percentage. The combination of cannabinoids and terpenes (the "entourage effect") plays a significant role in the overall experience and how "hard" a strain feels. Individual tolerance and body chemistry will also dictate how a strain affects you. To truly know which is the "hardest hitting" from this specific batch, you would need to see lab test results for each.
