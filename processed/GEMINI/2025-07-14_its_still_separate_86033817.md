### GEMINI
You're very close! The issue is that you created a new volume on only one of the unallocated spaces (the 2048 GB one). To get the full 4TB, you need to extend that newly created "4tb drive (E:)" volume to include the remaining "1678.02 GB Unallocated" space. Here's how to fix it: 🖥️ On Windows (Disk Management) Locate Your Drive (Disk 1): In Disk Management, you can see "Disk 1" has your "4tb drive (E:)" partition and the "1678.02 GB Unallocated" space next to it. Extend the Volume: Right-click on the "4tb drive (E:)" partition (the blue bar on Disk 1). Select "Extend Volume..." The Extend Volume Wizard will appear. Click "Next." The wizard should automatically select the "1678.02 GB Unallocated" space as the available space to extend into. The "Select the amount of space in MB" should show the maximum available (which will be the size of the unallocated space). Do not change this number. Click "Next." Click "Finish." Your "4tb drive (E:)" will now expand to include the previously unallocated space, giving you the full capacity of your 4TB external hard drive as a single partition.
