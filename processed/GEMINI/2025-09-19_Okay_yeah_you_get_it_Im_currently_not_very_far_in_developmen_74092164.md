Examples: A new ticket is created in Jira, a new lead is captured in Salesforce, a code merge request is opened on GitHub, an order is placed on Shopify. The Data Fetcher: This is the part that gathers all the "relevant data" and "links to documentation." Examples: Your system would look at the Jira ticket, grab the title, description, and assigned person. It might also use that information to search your internal wiki (like Confluence or SharePoint) for a specific document related to that type of task. The Message Composer & Sender: This is where the magic happens. It takes all the gathered information and formats it into a clear, concise, and actionable message. Example: A single, organized Slack message that says: "New Bug Report: 'Login page button is broken.' Assigned to: [John Doe]. Description: [Clicking the 'Login' button does nothing on Chrome 115]. Slack Workflows & Microsoft Power Automate: These are the native automation tools within each platform. They are excellent for simple, in-platform automations. How they help: You can create workflows that are triggered by a button click or a slash command, and they can send messages, collect information from users, and more.
