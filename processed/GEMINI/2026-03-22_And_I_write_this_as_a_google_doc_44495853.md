# And I write this as a google doc?

### USER
And I write this as a google doc?

### GEMINI
Yes, a Google Doc is the perfect way to do this. It keeps the letter professional, easy to edit, and—most importantly—it saves a digital "paper trail" for you. Here is exactly how to set it up so it looks like a formal legal notice. 1. The Layout (Standard Business Letter) Font: Times New Roman or Arial (Size 11 or 12). Margins: Standard 1-inch margins. Alignment: Left-aligned. 2. Copy/Paste This Template Replace the bracketed text with your specific info. [Your Full Name] [Your Street Address] [Akron, OH Zip Code] [Current Date: March 23, 2026] Weltman, Weinberg & Reis Co., LPA 5990 West Creek Rd, Suite 200 Independence, OH 44131 RE: NOTICE OF DISPUTE AND REQUEST FOR DEBT VALIDATION WWR Reference Number: [Insert Number from the Letter] Original Creditor: Discover Bank To Whom It May Concern, I am writing in response to your notice regarding the alleged debt referenced above. I am formally disputing the validity of this debt and requesting verification pursuant to the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), 15 U.S.C. § 1692g. Please provide me with the following: A complete breakdown of the alleged amount owed, including all principal, interest, and fees. Evidence of the "Chain of Title" (the legal assignment) showing the transfer of this specific account from the original creditor to your office. A copy of the original signed contract or agreement that created this debt. Notice to Cease and Desist: I am also requesting that you cease all telephone contact with me at my residence and my place of employment. All future communication regarding this matter must be in writing and sent via U.S. Mail. Sincerely, (Signature) [Your Printed Name] 3. How to "Certified Mail" it on Monday When you get to the Stow Post Office after court: Print two copies: One to mail, and one for your own physical records. Ask for a "Certified Mail with Return Receipt" form. It’s the green card (PS Form 3811). Fill it out: Put your address on the back so the post office mails it back to you once WWR signs for it. Keep the Receipt: The clerk will give you a white receipt with a tracking number. Staple this to your copy of the letter. Why the Google Doc matters: In "vibe coding" terms, this is your Version Control. If WWR ever claims they didn't get the letter or they keep calling you, you have the digital doc, the timestamped receipt, and the signed green card to prove they are violating federal law.
