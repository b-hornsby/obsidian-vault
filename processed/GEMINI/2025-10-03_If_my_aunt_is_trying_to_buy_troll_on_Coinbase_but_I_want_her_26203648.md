# If my aunt is trying to buy $troll on Coinbase but I want her to hold it on a self custodial wallet, what is the easiest way for her to do that? 

### USER
If my aunt is trying to buy $troll on Coinbase but I want her to hold it on a self custodial wallet, what is the easiest way for her to do that? 

### GEMINI
This is a great idea for your aunt! Holding crypto in a self-custodial wallet gives her full control over her assets. Since she's already using Coinbase, the easiest path for a beginner would be for her to use the Coinbase Wallet app. Here is the general easiest process, followed by the specific steps: The Easiest Overall Process Choose a Self-Custodial Wallet: For a beginner who is already using Coinbase, Coinbase Wallet (a separate app from the Coinbase exchange) is arguably the easiest option. It is non-custodial (meaning she controls the private keys/seed phrase) but has a very user-friendly interface and a simple linking process with the main Coinbase exchange. Other user-friendly options include Exodus or Trust Wallet. Set up the Wallet: She downloads the app and carefully writes down her Secret Recovery Phrase (often called a seed phrase). This is the most crucial step—if she loses this phrase, she loses access to her funds forever. Find the Wallet Address: In her new wallet, she selects the $TROLL token (which is likely an ERC-20 token on the Ethereum network) and finds the "Receive" address. Transfer from Coinbase: She goes to her main Coinbase exchange account, selects the $TROLL, chooses the "Send" or "Withdraw" option, and pastes her new self-custodial wallet's "Receive" address. Step-by-Step Guide using Coinbase Wallet (Recommended for Easiest Transfer) Download Coinbase Wallet: Your aunt needs to download the separate Coinbase Wallet app (not the main Coinbase exchange app). Set Up: She follows the prompts to set up a new wallet. Crucially, she must write down the 12 or 24-word Secret Recovery Phrase on paper and store it securely offline. This phrase is the key to her funds. Link to Coinbase: In the Coinbase Wallet, there's typically an option to "Add crypto with Coinbase Pay" or similar, which allows her to easily connect or link her Coinbase exchange account. Initiate Transfer: Once linked, she can initiate a transfer directly from her Coinbase exchange account to her Coinbase Wallet right within the app. This often streamlines the process compared to a manual external send. Step-by-Step Guide for Any Self-Custodial Wallet (e.g., Exodus, Trust Wallet) Download and Set Up: Download the chosen wallet (e.g., Exodus, Trust Wallet) and set it up. She must securely write down and store the Secret Recovery Phrase. Find the $TROLL Receive Address: Open the self-custodial wallet. Find $TROLL in the list of assets. Since $TROLL is often a meme coin on the Ethereum network, she should ensure the wallet supports it and is showing the Ethereum network address. Click "Receive" or "Deposit". Copy the long wallet address (it usually starts with 0x). Send from Coinbase Exchange: Log in to her main Coinbase account. Go to her portfolio/assets and find $TROLL. Click "Send" or "Withdraw". Paste the address she copied from her self-custodial wallet into the recipient field. Enter the amount to send. DOUBLE-CHECK THE ADDRESS and the NETWORK (it must be the correct blockchain, likely Ethereum). A mistake here can mean permanent loss of funds. Confirm and send. The transaction will incur a network fee (gas fee). Important Safety Note: Emphasize that in a self-custodial wallet, she is her own bank. If she loses her recovery phrase, no one—not you, not Coinbase, not the wallet company—can recover her funds. If she shares it, her funds can be stolen.
