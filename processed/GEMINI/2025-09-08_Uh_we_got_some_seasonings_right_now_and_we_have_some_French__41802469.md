# Uh, we got some seasonings right now and we have some French yellow mustard right now.

### USER
Uh, we got some seasonings right now and we have some French yellow mustard right now.

### GEMINI
Okay, you could mix some of that French's yellow mustard with your seasonings to make a paste, then rub it all over the ribs. Or, you could rub the ribs with just the mustard first, and then add the dry seasonings on top. Either way, you'll get a nice, even coating that will help the seasonings stick and add great flavor.
