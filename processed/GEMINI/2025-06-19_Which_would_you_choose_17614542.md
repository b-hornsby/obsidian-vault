# Which would you choose?

### USER
Which would you choose?

### GEMINI
If I had to choose solely based on the potential for the "hardest hit" (meaning highest THC content and typically strong effects based on general reputation), I would lean towards either Thin Mint Cookie or Granny Apple. Here's why: Thin Mint Cookie (or Thin Mint GSC): This strain consistently ranks as one of the most potent phenotypes of the Girl Scout Cookies lineage, with reported THC levels frequently reaching into the mid-20s and sometimes higher (20-28%). Its hybrid nature often provides a potent cerebral onset followed by deep relaxation. Granny Apple (or Granny Smith Apple): While perhaps less universally known for extreme potency than Thin Mint GSC, "Granny Apple" or "Granny Smith Apple" also shows reported THC levels in the low to mid-20s, with some sources even citing ranges up to 30%. This suggests it can be a very powerful strain. Sunset Sherbet is also a very potent strain (18-24% THC), and Skittles (Zkittlez) is certainly strong (15-23% THC). However, if the goal is the absolute "hardest hitting" based on common reported THC ranges, Thin Mint Cookie and Granny Apple seem to have a slight edge in their top-end potential. Important Caveat: As mentioned before, these are general expectations based on strain reputation. The actual potency of any given product depends entirely on the specific batch and its lab test results. Always check the THC percentage on the packaging if you want the most accurate information on potency.
