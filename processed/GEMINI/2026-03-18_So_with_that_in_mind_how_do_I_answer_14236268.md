# So with that in mind, how do I answer?

### USER
So with that in mind, how do I answer?

### GEMINI
Since the contract is attached, the law firm is trying to "lock you in." Your goal is to keep the door open by not admitting to their version of the math or their legal ownership of that specific contract. Here is exactly how to fill out that Defendant's Answer form you have. 1. The Header (Top of the Form) Plaintiff: [Copy the name from the Summons—e.g., American Express or Discover] Defendant: [Your Full Name] Case No.: [Copy the Case Number from the top right of your summons] 2. The Body (Your Response) For each numbered paragraph in their "Complaint," you must give a response. Since they attached a contract, you will use a specific legal phrase that forces them to prove the details. Write this exactly: "Defendant admits his name and address." "Defendant is without knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief as to the truth of the remaining allegations in the Complaint and therefore denies them." "Defendant specifically denies the accuracy of the alleged balance and demands strict proof of the chain of ownership of the subject debt." 3. The "Why" (Legal Logic) By using the phrase "without knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief," you are invoking Ohio Civil Rule 8(B). Even if the contract has your name on it, you don't "know" if their interest calculations are legal, if they added hidden fees, or if the law firm actually has the legal paperwork proving they bought your debt from the original bank. This keeps the case alive and prevents them from winning a "Judgment on the Pleadings." 4. "I ask the Court to:" Write: "Dismiss the Complaint at Plaintiff's cost and for any other relief the Court deems just." 5. The Bottom (The Signature & Service) Check the "Pro se" box (this means you're representing yourself). Certificate of Service: This is the most important part for the clerk. Served upon: [Name of Attorney from the Summons, e.g., James P. McGowan]. At: [The Attorney’s Address in Independence, OH]. By: Ordinary U.S. Mail. Date: [The date you drop it in the mail]. The Final Step-by-Step Logistics Print 3 copies of this Answer. Print the Form 20 (Fee Waiver) and fill it out with your $11/hr info, but do not sign it yet. Go to Stow Municipal Court (4400 Courthouse Blvd) tomorrow. At the Clerk's window: * Tell them you want to file an Answer and a Civil Fee Waiver (Form 20). Sign the Form 20 in front of the Clerk (they will notarize it for free). Have them Time-Stamp all three copies of your Answer. The Mail: Take one of those time-stamped copies, put it in an envelope, and mail it to the lawyer's office in Independence. Once this is filed, you are legally "safe" for several months. This buys you the time to get that better job and reach out to Community Legal Aid (330-535-4191) to help you negotiate that $8k down to a much smaller number.
