# Yo, so I was cleaning my PC, updating BIOS and all that, and now the PC won't turn back on. I just want to know why or what to look for so I can fix it.

### USER
Yo, so I was cleaning my PC, updating BIOS and all that, and now the PC won't turn back on. I just want to know why or what to look for so I can fix it.

### GEMINI
Huh, that's definitely not good. Let's start with the basics. First up, double-check that all the power cables are plugged in tight, both inside the computer and into the wall. If that's all good, let's look at the motherboard. See if there are any lights on it, especially near the CPU or RAM – those lights can tell you what's causing the problem.
