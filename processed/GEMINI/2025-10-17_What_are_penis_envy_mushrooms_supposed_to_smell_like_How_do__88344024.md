# What are penis envy mushrooms supposed to smell like? How do I know if it’s safe to eat? 

### USER
What are penis envy mushrooms supposed to smell like? How do I know if it’s safe to eat? 

### GEMINI
Penis Envy mushrooms are a potent variety of Psilocybe cubensis, a species of psychedelic mushroom. Regarding the smell of Psilocybe cubensis mushrooms, general descriptions often note that they have a subtle, earthy, or mushroomy scent, which is often described as natural or nearly unnoticeable when fresh and raw. It is important to note that a foul or distinctly unpleasant smell—like sour, musty, or moldy—is typically a sign that any mushroom, including psychedelic ones, is spoiled and should not be consumed. When determining if any psychedelic mushroom is safe to eat, you should focus on signs of spoilage and degradation: Foul Odor: A smell that is sour, musty, rotten, or generally unpleasant indicates decomposition or contamination (bacteria, yeast, or mold). Slimy or Mushy Texture: Fresh, properly dried mushrooms should be firm. If they are soft, sticky, or slimy, they have absorbed moisture and are likely spoiling. Mold Growth: The presence of fuzzy white, green, or black patches on the mushroom is a clear sign of mold and makes them unsafe to eat. Unusual Discoloration: While a bluish or greenish stain (bruising) can occur when Psilocybe mushrooms are handled and is normal (due to the oxidation of psilocin), other color changes like deep darkening, or white, green, or black spots unrelated to bruising, may indicate rot or mold. Crucial Warning: The most significant danger when consuming any mushroom is the risk of misidentification. Many toxic or deadly wild mushrooms closely resemble psychedelic varieties. Unless you are an expert mycologist, the only safe practice is to never consume wild-picked mushrooms. You should only consume mushrooms that have been grown and sold by a reputable source, and even then, you must visually inspect them for any signs of spoilage before consumption.
