# I meant when it’s dry. What is it supposed to smell like

### USER
I meant when it’s dry. What is it supposed to smell like

### GEMINI
When Penis Envy mushrooms are fully dried, the natural smell is typically described as: Earthy Neutral or Faintly Mushroomy Sometimes "Farinaceous" (which is an odor similar to freshly ground flour or raw pastry dough). Some people also describe a subtle, slightly metallic, or even a very faint medicinal/chemical note in dried Psilocybe cubensis, which is often a concentration of the natural compounds in the mushroom. How to Know if Dried Mushrooms are Safe to Eat A properly dried mushroom should retain its natural, benign scent. An unpleasant odor is the key warning sign that they have gone bad: Bad Smell: If the mushrooms have a strong, noticeable, and unpleasant odor—such as musty, sour, sweet/funky (fermenting), rotten, or like ammonia—it is a strong indication of mold, bacteria, or yeast contamination due to improper drying or storage. Do not eat them. Texture: They should be completely brittle and cracker-dry. If they feel soft, spongy, sticky, or moist, they have absorbed humidity and are at high risk of spoilage and mold growth. Appearance: Look for clear signs of mold, which can appear as fuzzy white, green, or black patches. While blue staining is a normal characteristic of the mushroom, any other discoloration (dark spots, rot) is a red flag. When in doubt about the smell or condition of a dried mushroom, it is always safest to discard it.
