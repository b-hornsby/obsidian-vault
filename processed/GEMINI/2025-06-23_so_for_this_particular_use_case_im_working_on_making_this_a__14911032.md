### GEMINI
You've got a fantastic goal! Making a stand-out help desk AI assistant with your local setup is definitely achievable. Let's break down how to optimize its behavior and discuss image generation. Part 1: Shaping Your Help Desk AI's Behavior This is all about crafting the "Context" and "Example Dialogue" in the "Chat" tab, combined with sensible generation parameters. A. Choose the Right Chat Mode: For a help desk, I recommend sticking with the "Chat" or "Chat-instruct" mode. "Chat-instruct" can be particularly powerful for giving direct, guiding instructions at the start of a session. B. Crafting the "Context" (The Core of its Personality): Go to the "Chat" tab, and locate the "Context" text box under the "Character" section. This is where you define who your AI is. Here's an example of a robust context for a help desk AI. Copy and paste this, then refine it for your specific needs: You are an advanced AI help desk assistant for [Your Company Name/Specific Domain, e.g., 'Tech Support for Home Networks']. Your primary goal is to provide clear, concise, and accurate solutions to user queries, troubleshoot common issues, and guide users through processes. **Key Principles:** 1. **Professionalism:** Maintain a polite, respectful, and helpful tone at all times. Avoid slang, jargon (unless explaining it), or overly casual language. 2. **Clarity:** Provide step-by-step instructions when applicable. Use simple language. 3. **Efficiency:** Aim for direct answers. If a problem requires more information, politely ask for it. Do not ramble. 4. **Problem-Solving Focus:** Understand the user's core issue and work towards a solution. 5. **Empathy:** Acknowledge user frustration if detected, but remain solution-oriented. 6. **Resourcefulness:** If you cannot directly solve an issue, guide the user on the next best steps (e.g., "This might require a specialist, I can help you find the right contact."). 7. **Knowledge Base:** Assume access to a vast knowledge base of [Your Company/Domain]'s products/services/policies. **Your Capabilities:** - Answering FAQs. - Troubleshooting common technical problems. - Providing setup instructions. - Explaining features and services. - Directing users to relevant documentation or human support when necessary. **Limitations (and how to handle them):** - You cannot perform actions outside of providing information (e.g., you cannot log into a user's account, remotely control a device, or process payments). - If asked for sensitive personal information, gently decline and explain why. - If a query is outside your defined scope, politely state that and offer to help with relevant topics. Begin by greeting the user and asking how you can assist them today. C. Setting the "Example Dialogue": Still in the "Chat" tab, scroll down to the "Example Dialogue" section. This is crucial for showing the model how to behave, not just telling it. Provide a few turns that perfectly illustrate the help desk persona. <USER>: My internet isn't working. <BOT>: I'm sorry to hear you're experiencing internet issues. To help me troubleshoot, could you please tell me: 1. Are you connected via Wi-Fi or Ethernet? 2. Are any other devices in your home experiencing the same problem? 3. Have you tried restarting your router and modem? <USER>: I need help setting up my new [Product Name] printer. <BOT>: Absolutely! I can guide you through the setup process for your [Product Name] printer. To start, please ensure the printer is plugged in and powered on. What operating system are you using (Windows, macOS, Linux)? <USER>: How do I reset my password for the customer portal? <BOT>: I can assist you with password reset instructions for the customer portal. Please visit [link to your password reset page/URL] and follow the on-screen prompts. If you encounter any difficulties, please let me know. D. Fine-tuning "Parameters" (in the "Parameters" tab): Go to the "Parameters" tab on the left. Adjust these for a reliable help desk: Temperature: 0.2 - 0.5 Why: Lower values make the AI's responses more deterministic, factual, and less prone to "hallucinating" creative but incorrect solutions. You want accuracy for a help desk. Top P: 0.8 - 0.9 Why: Keeps a good range of token choices but focuses on the most probable ones. Prevents the AI from going too far off-topic. Top K: 40 - 50 Why: Similar to Top P, limits the selection to the most probable tokens, maintaining relevance and accuracy. Repetition Penalty: 1.1 - 1.2 Why: Prevents the AI from repeating phrases or ideas, ensuring diverse and engaging explanations. Max new tokens: 256 - 512 (or higher if needed for very detailed instructions) Why: This sets the maximum length of the AI's response. You want it long enough to provide full solutions but not so long it rambles. Adjust based on how verbose you need it to be. Frequency Penalty / Presence Penalty: You can generally leave these at their defaults (often 0 or 0.1), or slightly increase them (e.g., 0.05-0.1) if you notice the AI frequently using the exact same words or phrases across multiple turns. E. Test and Iterate: After setting the context, example dialogue, and parameters, go back to the "Chat" tab. Click the "Clear history" button to ensure the new context is applied fully. Start interacting with your AI. Pay close attention to its responses. Is it: Too informal? Adjust the context. Too verbose or too brief? Adjust Max new tokens. Giving incorrect information? Lower Temperature or review your context/examples for clarity. Repeating itself? Increase Repetition Penalty. Save your character! In the "Chat" tab, under the "Character" section, you can "Save character" to a .json file. This lets you easily load your custom help desk persona later. Part 2: Adding Image Generation Later On You're thinking ahead, which is great! Oobabooga's primary strength is text generation, but it has an extension system that allows it to interact with other AI models, including image generators like Stable Diffusion. How it generally works: Separate Image Generation Software: You'll typically need a separate installation of an image generation UI/backend, most commonly Automatic1111's Stable Diffusion WebUI (A1111). This needs to be running in addition to Oobabooga. API Access: A1111 has an API mode (--api launch flag) that allows other applications to send it text prompts and receive images. Oobabooga Extension: Oobabooga has an extension (e.g., sd_api_pictures or stable_diffusion_image_gen) that, when enabled, connects to your running A1111 API. Triggering Generation: Within Oobabooga's chat, you or the AI can then use specific syntax (e.g., !draw a cat in a hat or [img]a robot playing chess[/img]) to send a text prompt to Stable Diffusion, which then generates an image. The image URL (or a local path if configured) can then be displayed in the Oobabooga chat. Your Setup Consideration: Running Automatic1111 and Oobabooga simultaneously will significantly increase your VRAM usage. Your RTX 3070 with 8GB VRAM might struggle to run both a 7B LLM (like Nous-Hermes-2) and a Stable Diffusion model (especially larger ones like SDXL, which needs 8GB+ itself) at the same time, in the same WSL2 instance. You might need to: Run A1111 in Windows directly (if it's not resource-contended by other things). Use smaller Stable Diffusion models (e.g., SD 1.5 based models). Temporarily unload the LLM in Oobabooga when generating images, and then reload it, or vice versa. Next Steps for Image Generation (Later): Install Automatic1111's Stable Diffusion WebUI: Follow a guide to get this running, ideally in your Windows environment for better resource separation, or in a separate WSL2 instance if you prefer that Linux environment. Ensure you enable its API. Explore Oobabooga Extensions: In Oobabooga's Web UI, go to the "Extensions" tab. Look for "sd_api_pictures" or similar Stable Diffusion integration extensions. Enable and configure them according to their instructions (usually just entering the A1111 API URL). Take it one step at a time! Get your help desk AI perfected first. Once you're happy with its text-based performance, then dive into the world of image generation. Good luck, Bryan – this is going to be a unique project!
