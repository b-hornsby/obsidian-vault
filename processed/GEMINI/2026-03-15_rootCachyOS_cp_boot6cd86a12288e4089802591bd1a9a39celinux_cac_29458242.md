### GEMINI
That is exactly what you want to see. Look at that Found initrd image line: it now lists both the intel-ucode.img and your initramfs-linux-cachyos.img. This means the "ignition" (microcode) and the "engine" (initramfs) are both connected. Your Minecraft "World Selection" menu now has the keys to start both CachyOS and Windows. The Final Move Type exit and hit Enter. Type reboot and hit Enter. Mashing F2 or Delete as it starts up to enter your BIOS. Go to the Boot menu and ensure the entry named "GRUB" is at the very top of the list. Save and Exit (F10). When the Minecraft screen pops up, use the arrow keys to select your CachyOS world. Does it boot you all the way to your desktop?
