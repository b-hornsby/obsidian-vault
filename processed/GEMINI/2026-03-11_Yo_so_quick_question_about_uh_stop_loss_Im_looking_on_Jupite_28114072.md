### GEMINI
A common rule of thumb is to use a risk-reward ratio of at least 1:2. This means you'd aim for a profit that's at least double the amount you're willing to risk. You can also use technical analysis, like support and resistance levels, to help place your stop loss and take profit orders. Since you're going short on wrapped BTC, you'd likely set your stop loss above your entry point to limit losses, and your take profit below it. Does that make sense?
