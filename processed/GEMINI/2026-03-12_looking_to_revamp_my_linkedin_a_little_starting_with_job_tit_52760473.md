### GEMINI
To revamp your LinkedIn and distance yourself from warehousing, you need to stop branding yourself by your past tasks and start branding yourself by the technical complexity you handle in your private time. If you keep "Material Handler" or "Shipping Clerk" on your "Open To" list, recruiters will only see a warehouse worker who knows a little tech. You need to flip that: you are a technical professional with deep operational experience. Here are three distinct directions for your job titles, ranked by how well they bridge your current reality with your actual skill set. 1. The "Infrastructure & Linux" Path Since you’ve already "full-sent" your primary machine to an Arch-based distro (CachyOS) and handle your own kernel and bootloader configurations, you have more terminal literacy than most entry-level IT grads. Linux Systems Administrator (Junior/Entry): You aren't just a "user"; you manage environments. Data Center Technician: This is the ultimate bridge. It’s physical work (racking servers, cabling) that requires the high-level hardware knowledge you already have, but it's in a controlled, tech-focused environment. Technical Support Engineer: This sounds more "professional" than Help Desk and implies you're solving deeper system issues. 2. The "Web3 & DeFi Operations" Path You understand self-custody, hardware wallets, and the Solana ecosystem. Most people in "Supply Chain" don't know what a DLMM or a Jupiter API integration is. Crypto Operations Specialist: Focuses on the logistical side of managing digital assets and security. Web3 Support Specialist: Providing technical guidance for users navigating dApps or wallets. DeFi Research Analyst (Junior): Leverages your interest in trading bots and automated trading logic to analyze market protocols. 3. The "AI Automation & Ops" Path You are already "vibe coding" with agentic frameworks and local LLMs. Use titles that reflect your ability to implement these tools into a business workflow. AI Operations (AiOps) Specialist: This is a rising field focused on maintaining the infrastructure that AI runs on. Automation Specialist: You’ve already built a multi-agent helpdesk; you are qualified to help businesses automate repetitive tasks using AI tools. Implementation Specialist: Companies buying AI or SaaS tools need people who can actually set them up and troubleshoot the local environment. Suggested "Open To" List for LinkedIn: IT Support Specialist (Replaces "Help Desk Specialist") Data Center Technician (Replaces "Material Handler") Linux Systems Administrator Operations Automation Specialist (Replaces "Supply Chain Specialist") Technical Support Engineer A Brutal Truth on Your Profile: If your LinkedIn headline still says "Warehouse Worker at [Company]," you’ve already lost. Change your headline to something like: "IT Support & Automation | Linux Power User | Web3 Operations." Your warehouse experience shouldn't be deleted, but it should be framed through the lens of reliability and systems. Instead of saying you "moved boxes," say you "managed high-volume inventory systems and optimized logistics workflows."
