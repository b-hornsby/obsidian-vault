# Give me a suggestion on a good silent sounding keyboard that’s either 65% or 75% I want a compact keyboard but I still want the important keys

### USER
Give me a suggestion on a good silent sounding keyboard that’s either 65% or 75% I want a compact keyboard but I still want the important keys

### GEMINI
Alright, let's find you a silent, compact keyboard with the essential keys! 65% and 75% are great choices for balancing space-saving with functionality. Here's my suggestion, along with some considerations: My Top Recommendation: Keychron Q1 Pro (75%) or Keychron Q2 Pro (65%) Why Keychron? High-Quality Construction: Keychron keyboards are known for their solid build quality, typically using aluminum cases. This adds weight and stability, reducing unwanted resonance and noise. Gasket Mount: This mounting style provides a softer, more cushioned typing experience, which also contributes to noise reduction. Hot-Swappable Sockets: Allows you to easily change switches without soldering. This is crucial for achieving a silent setup. Customization: Keychron keyboards are highly customizable. You can change keycaps, switches, and even the internal dampening material. Wireless and Wired Options: The Pro models offer both Bluetooth and wired connectivity, giving you flexibility. Pre-Lubed Switches: The Pro models come with pre-lubed switches which are a good starting point for a smooth and quiet typing experience. Why 75% or 65%? 75% (Q1 Pro): This layout keeps the function row and arrow keys, which are essential for many workflows. It's a great balance between compactness and functionality. 65% (Q2 Pro): This layout is even more compact, omitting the function row. However, it still retains the arrow keys and dedicated delete key, which are important for many users. Achieving Silence with Keychron: Silent Switches: This is the MOST important factor. Replace the stock switches with silent linear switches. Here are some excellent options: Gazzew Boba U4 Silent: These are considered some of the quietest and smoothest silent switches on the market. Durock Silent Linear Switches (Daybreak, Dolphin, etc.): Another excellent, high-quality silent linear option. Gateron Silent Inks: A popular choice for silent linears with a smooth feel. O-Rings: Install O-rings on the keycaps. These small rubber rings dampen the sound of the keycaps bottoming out. Lubrication: Lubricate the switches with a high-quality keyboard lubricant. This will further reduce friction and noise. (Though the Pro models are pre-lubed, you can still improve them.) Case Foam: Add case foam to the inside of the keyboard case. This will absorb sound and reduce resonance. Desk Mat: Use a thick desk mat to absorb sound vibrations. Alternatives (If Keychron Doesn't Fit Your Needs): NuPhy Halo65/Halo75: Similar to Keychron in terms of build quality and features. Also offers gasket mount and hot-swappable sockets. Akko MOD007v3 (75%): A more budget-friendly option with good build quality and gasket mount. Important Considerations: Budget: Keychron keyboards are generally in the mid-to-high price range. Customization Time: Achieving optimal silence requires some time and effort for switch replacement, lubrication, and potentially adding foam. Switch Feel: Silent switches can feel slightly different from regular switches. It's best to try a few different types to find your preference. Let me know if you have any more questions! I can help you find specific silent switches or other accessories.
