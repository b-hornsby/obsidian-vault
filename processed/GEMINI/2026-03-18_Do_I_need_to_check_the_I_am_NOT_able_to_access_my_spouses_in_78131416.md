# Do I need to check the I am NOT able to access my spouses income section?

### USER
Do I need to check the I am NOT able to access my spouses income section?

### GEMINI
No, you should leave that box unchecked. Since you are not married, you do not have a spouse. Checking that box implies that a spouse exists but you can't get to their money. Since there is no legal spouse in this equation, the box doesn't apply to you. Here is how to handle that specific section: The Checkbox: Leave it blank. The "Spouse" Column: In the table directly below that checkbox, write "N/A" or "0" in every box under the "Spouse" header. The Logic: By leaving it blank, you are telling the court: "I am a single individual filing this on my own merit." Final Review of the "Public Benefits" Section Since you aren't on benefits: Checkboxes (Medicaid, SNAP, etc.): Leave them all blank. The "187.5%" Line: You don't need to put an "X" there if you don't receive those specific benefits.
