Its job is to listen to your questions, find answers in your knowledge base, and then use Oobabooga's brain to give you a smart response. 1. Settings & Ingredients: CHROMA_DB_PATH, EMBEDDING_MODEL_NAME: These tell the chatbot where your "Super-Smart Filing Cabinet" (chroma_db) is and which "Meaning-Translator" (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) was used to create it (they must match!).
