How much is this worth with rainn Wilson signature on it? 

### GEMINI
The value of a Dwight Schrute Funko Pop #882 with Rainn Wilson's signature can vary significantly based on several factors: Authentication: The most crucial factor is whether the signature has been authenticated by a reputable third-party authentication service such as PSA/DNA, Beckett, or JSA. A Funko Pop with a Certificate of Authenticity (COA) from one of these services will be worth considerably more. Condition: The condition of both the Funko Pop box and the figure itself is important. Items in mint condition will command higher prices. Exclusivity: Funko Pop #882 is a 2019 Fall Convention exclusive, which generally makes it more valuable than common releases. Market Demand: The popularity of "The Office" and the character Dwight Schrute influences the demand and thus the price. Rarity of Signature: How often Rainn Wilson signs Funko Pops can affect the price. Based on available information, here's a general idea of the potential value: Unsigned Dwight Schrute Funko Pop #882: Typically sells for around $20 - $45 online, depending on the platform and condition. Signed Dwight Schrute Funko Pop with Authentication: Signed versions with authentication can range from $150 to $300+. I found listings for signed and authenticated versions in this price range, with some even higher depending on the specific authentication and condition. One listing showed a "Gem Mint 10 Auto" PSA certified version for $200. Another listing without specific grading was priced at $212.49. To get a more precise valuation for your specific item: Check for Authentication: See if the Funko Pop comes with a COA from a reputable authentication service. Assess Condition: Carefully examine the box and figure for any damage. Research Recent Sales: Look for recent sales of the exact Funko Pop #882 with Rainn Wilson's authenticated signature on platforms like eBay, StockX, or dedicated Funko Pop collecting groups. Filter for "sold" listings to see actual prices people have paid. Keep in mind that prices can fluctuate based on market trends.
