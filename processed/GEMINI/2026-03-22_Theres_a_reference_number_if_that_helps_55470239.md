There’s a reference number if that helps?

### GEMINI
Yes, the reference number (often called a "WWR File Number" by them) is the most important piece of information to include. It’s their internal tracking ID, and putting it on your letter ensures it gets routed to the right legal team immediately. Here is the updated Debt Validation Letter with that reference number integrated. Debt Validation Letter (For Weltman, Weinberg & Reis) Draft this today and mail it Monday along with your court filings. To: Weltman, Weinberg & Reis Co., LPA Address: 5990 West Creek Rd, Suite 200, Independence, OH 44131 Re: Reference Number: [Insert Your Reference Number Here] Original Creditor: Discover Bank / Capital One To Whom It May Concern, I am writing in response to your notice dated [Date on the notice] regarding the alleged debt referenced above. I am formally disputing the validity of this debt and requesting verification pursuant to the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), 15 U.S.C. § 1692g. Please provide me with the following: A complete breakdown of the alleged amount owed, including all principal, interest, and fees. The date of the last payment made on this account. Evidence of the "Chain of Title" (the legal assignment) showing how your client obtained the rights to this specific debt from the original creditor. Verification that this debt is within the Ohio statute of limitations for collection. I am also requesting that you cease all telephone contact with me at my place of employment and my residence. All future communication regarding this matter must be in writing and sent via U.S. Mail. Sincerely, Bryan [Your Last Name] [Your Address] Why this is the right move for Monday: The "Paper Trail": By including the reference number, you aren't just "some guy" complaining; you are a defendant who has formally identified their case. Stopping the Phones: Weltman is known for high-volume calling. That last paragraph in the letter legally forces them to stop calling you the moment they receive it. The "Poor Man's Defense": Since you’re filing a Form 20 (Fee Waiver) at the court for the other case on Monday, that is a public record. Weltman will see that you've been declared "Indigent" by the court, and they will realize that trying to sue you for a second debt is a waste of their time because you have nothing to garnish. Monday Schedule Recap: Court (Stow): File the Answer and Form 20. Post Office (Stow): Mail #1: Stamped copy of your Answer to the lawyer in the $8k case. Mail #2: This Validation Letter to Weltman (WWR) via Certified Mail. Do you have the envelope and a few bucks for the Certified Mail fee ready to go for Monday morning?
