# restream isnt free

### USER
restream isnt free

### GEMINI
You are correct that many multistreaming services, including Restream.io, have a paid component. However, it's a bit more nuanced than that. Restream's Free Tier Restream does, in fact, offer a free plan. This plan allows you to: Multistream to up to two channels simultaneously. Stream using third-party software like OBS Studio. Use their cross-platform chat feature. The main limitations of the free tier are a Restream watermark on your stream and the inability to stream to more than two platforms at once. Since you are specifically asking about streaming to Twitch and TikTok, Restream's free tier would work perfectly for your needs. Other Free and Low-Cost Options If you don't want to use Restream's free tier or you want more flexibility, here are some other options: 1. The OBS-Multiplatform Plugin This is the most popular completely free method. With your powerful PC, this is a very viable option. What it is: A plugin for OBS Studio that allows you to add multiple streaming destinations. How it works: You'll set up a separate stream for Twitch and a separate stream for TikTok within the plugin. OBS will then encode and upload two separate video streams simultaneously. Pros: It's completely free, and you have full control over your stream settings for each platform. You can set a higher bitrate for Twitch and a different one for TikTok if you want. Cons: This method uses more of your internet's upload bandwidth because you're sending two separate streams. It's also more resource-intensive on your CPU and GPU, but with your specs, this won't be a problem. Your main consideration will be your internet connection. 2. Streamlabs Desktop What it is: A free all-in-one streaming software based on OBS. How it works: Streamlabs offers a built-in multistreaming feature, but you typically need to be a Streamlabs Ultra subscriber to unlock it. However, they occasionally offer promotions or free trials. 3. Other free services There are a few other free services available, but they often come with significant limitations, like watermarks, lower video quality (720p), or a limited number of streaming hours per month. Final Recommendation Given your PC specs, you have two excellent choices for free multistreaming: Use Restream.io's free tier. This is the simplest and most stable method. It works perfectly for your goal of streaming to Twitch and TikTok, and your PC only has to upload one stream. Use the OBS-Multiplatform plugin. This is the best option if you want to avoid a third-party service and have full control. Your powerful hardware is perfectly suited for this, and the only potential bottleneck is your internet upload speed.
