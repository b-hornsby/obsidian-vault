Based on the information available for the token symbol "$TROLL" on major exchanges like Coinbase, it appears that Coinbase lists the Solana-based version of the TROLL token. This means you need a self-custodial wallet that is built for the Solana network (SPL tokens). The good news is that this is a very common use case now, and there are several excellent user-friendly options. Here is the easiest way for your aunt to do this: 1. Choose a User-Friendly Solana Wallet The best self-custodial wallet for a beginner in the Solana ecosystem is Phantom. It is widely recognized as the most user-friendly and well-supported Solana wallet. Step-by-Step Transfer Process Once you've chosen a wallet, the process is straightforward, but extreme care must be taken with the address and network. Step A: Set Up the Self-Custodial Wallet (The Critical Step) Download: Your aunt downloads the chosen app (e.g., Phantom or Exodus) on her phone or computer. Create Wallet: She follows the instructions to create a new wallet. Secure the Phrase (MOST IMPORTANT): She must write down the 12- or 24-word Secret Recovery Phrase on paper and store it in a secure, private location. She must never store it digitally or share it with anyone. This phrase is the only way to recover her funds if she loses her device. Step B: Get the Solana Wallet Address Open the Wallet: Open the self-custodial wallet (Phantom, Exodus, etc.). Select the Token: She should look for a Solana-specific address. Usually, you can click on the Solana (SOL) asset, or the $TROLL (SPL) token itself. Click "Receive": Click the "Receive" or "Deposit" button. Copy the Address: A long string of characters (her public wallet address) will appear. She needs to copy this entire address (do not try to type it). Step C: Send from Coinbase Exchange Log In: Log in to her main Coinbase account. Find $TROLL: Go to the portfolio/assets and find the $TROLL balance. Click "Send" or "Withdraw": Initiate a withdrawal. Paste the Address: Paste the long address she copied from her Phantom/Exodus wallet into the recipient field. Confirm the Network: This is the second most critical check: She must ensure the transaction is on the Solana network. Coinbase will usually select this automatically when it recognizes the Solana address, but she must verify it before sending. Send a Test Amount (Highly Recommended): For a first-time transfer, it is always best to send a very small amount of $TROLL first (e.g., $5 worth). Once that small amount arrives in the self-custodial wallet, she can confidently send the rest. Finalize: Review the transaction details (address, amount, network) and confirm the send. The $TROLL (SPL token) should arrive in her self-custodial Solana wallet within a few minutes.
