### GEMINI
Here's the tl;dr for solving the "0 B FREE OF 0 B" issue for your D: drive in Steam: Try "Add Drive" in Steam settings, then select your D: drive. If that fails, create a new folder on your D: drive (e.g., D:\SteamLibrary), then try "Add Drive" and select that specific folder. Run Steam as Administrator (right-click Steam icon -> "Run as administrator") and retry step 1 or 2. Check folder permissions: Make sure your user account has "Full control" or full read/write access to your D: drive or the D:\SteamLibrary folder (right-click folder -> Properties -> Security tab). Restart Steam and your computer. (Less common) Temporarily disable antivirus and try adding the drive.
