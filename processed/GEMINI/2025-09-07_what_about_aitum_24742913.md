# what about aitum? 

### USER
what about aitum? 

### GEMINI
Aitum is an excellent option for you, especially for multistreaming. It's not a service in the same vein as Restream.io, but rather a suite of OBS Studio plugins and a separate application. Aitum has two key products that are relevant to your needs: Aitum Multistream: This is a free OBS plugin that allows you to stream to multiple platforms simultaneously, directly from OBS. It eliminates the need for a third-party service like Restream.io. Because your PC is powerful, it can handle the extra load of encoding and uploading multiple streams at once. Aitum Vertical: This is a free plugin that's a game-changer for streaming to TikTok and YouTube Shorts. It adds a second, vertical canvas to your OBS, allowing you to create a dedicated layout for vertical platforms without affecting your main horizontal stream. It can even link scenes so that when you change scenes on your main horizontal stream, the vertical stream automatically changes as well. This is crucial for giving your TikTok viewers a high-quality, native experience. The main Aitum App, which provides stream automation and viewer interaction tools, is a paid subscription ($4.99/month), but the Multistream and Vertical plugins are both free. For you, using Aitum is a very good choice. Your RTX 3070 can leverage its NVENC encoder to handle the multiple video streams with minimal performance impact. Aitum Multistream will save you the subscription cost of a service like Restream while providing a very similar function. The Aitum Vertical plugin, in particular, will give your TikTok viewers a much better viewing experience than a simple cropped horizontal stream. You can learn more about how Aitum works in this Aitum basics overview.
