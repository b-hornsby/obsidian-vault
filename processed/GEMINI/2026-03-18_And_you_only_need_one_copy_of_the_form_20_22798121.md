And you only need one copy of the form 20?

### GEMINI
Correct. You only need one original copy of the Form 20 (Fee Waiver). The court is the only entity that needs that form because it’s a request for the Judge to waive your costs. You do not need to send a copy of your financial secrets to the bank's lawyer.
