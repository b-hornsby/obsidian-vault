# how do i move my switch games from my sd card to the steam deck hard drive without messing emulation station up? i also have games in the sd card that i want emulation station to be able to pick up on as wel 

### USER
how do i move my switch games from my sd card to the steam deck hard drive without messing emulation station up? i also have games in the sd card that i want emulation station to be able to pick up on as wel 

### GEMINI
Moving your Switch games while keeping everything functional requires precision. EmulationStation (ES-DE) and EmuDeck are designed to look at one specific directory for ROMs. If you simply move folders, you break the path, and ES-DE will see nothing. To have games on both the internal SSD and the SD card simultaneously, you must use Symbolic Links (Symlinks). These are "virtual folders" that trick the system into thinking a folder is in its original spot when the files are actually elsewhere. The Strategy: Split Storage via Symlinks You will move your heavy Switch games to the internal drive, but leave a "shortcut" in the original SD card folder so EmulationStation doesn't get confused. Step 1: Move the Switch Games Enter Desktop Mode on your Steam Deck. Open Dolphin (the file browser). Go to your internal hard drive (/home/deck/). Create a new folder named Internal_Games (or whatever you like). Inside that, create a folder named switch. Open a second Dolphin window and go to your SD Card (/run/media/mmcblk0p1/Emulation/roms/switch/). Move (don't copy, or you'll run out of space) your Switch game files from the SD card to the new switch folder on your internal drive. Step 2: Create the Link (The "Mirror" Trick) Go to the switch folder on your Internal Drive. Right-click and drag that switch folder back into the Emulation/roms/ folder on your SD card. When you let go of the mouse button, a menu will pop up. Select "Link Here". You should now see a folder on your SD card named switch with a small arrow icon on it. Step 3: Handling Other Games on the SD Card Since you haven't touched the other system folders (like snes, ps2, etc.) on the SD card, EmulationStation will continue to find them as it always has. By using the symlink for only the switch folder, you’ve effectively split your library across two drives without changing ES-DE's settings. Critical Warnings Pathing: If you use Steam ROM Manager to add games directly to your Steam Library, you must re-run the parser. The old shortcuts point to the SD card path; even with a symlink, it’s cleaner to let the manager refresh the paths to the new internal location. Don't Move the Base "Emulation" Folder: Moving the entire root Emulation folder is a massive headache. Only move the specific system subfolders (like switch) that are eating up your space. Save Files: Your save files are usually stored in /home/deck/.var/app/ (for Flatpaks) or within the Emulation/saves folder. Moving the ROMs via symlink does not break saves, as the emulator still "sees" the game in the expected location. Precise Action Plan Audit: Identify exactly which Switch games are too big for the SD card. Execute: Use the "Link Here" method described above for the roms/switch folder. Verify: Open EmulationStation. If the games show up and launch, the link is active. Cleanup: Re-run Steam ROM Manager only if you want individual game icons in your Steam library to update their direct target paths.
