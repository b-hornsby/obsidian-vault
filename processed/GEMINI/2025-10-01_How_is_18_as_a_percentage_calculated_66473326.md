# How is 1/8 as a percentage calculated? 

### USER
How is 1/8 as a percentage calculated? 

### GEMINI
# Calculate 1/8 as a percentage fraction = 1 / 8 percentage = fraction * 100 print(f"Decimal: {fraction}") print(f"Percentage: {percentage}") Decimal: 0.125 Percentage: 12.5 Calculating $\frac{1}{8}$ as a percentage involves two simple steps: Convert the fraction to a decimal. Multiply the decimal by $100$ and add the percent symbol ($%$). 1. Convert Fraction to Decimal To convert the fraction $\frac{1}{8}$ to a decimal, you perform the division: $$\frac{1}{8} = 1 \div 8 = 0.125$$ 2. Convert Decimal to Percentage Next, you multiply the resulting decimal by $100$ to get the percentage: $$0.125 \times 100 = 12.5$$ Therefore, $\frac{1}{8}$ as a percentage is $12.5%$. 🎉
