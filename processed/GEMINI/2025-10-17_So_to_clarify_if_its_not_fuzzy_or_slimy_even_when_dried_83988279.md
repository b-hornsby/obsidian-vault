# So to clarify, if it's not fuzzy or slimy even when dried

### USER
So to clarify, if it's not fuzzy or slimy even when dried

### GEMINI
Right, if it's not fuzzy or slimy, even when dry, and it doesn't have a foul or musty smell, it's likely just bruising. The dark color is normal and doesn't necessarily indicate that the mushroom has gone bad.
