### GEMINI
That's a strong name! Fast Fix Solutions is professional, clear, and highly scalable, as your cousin suggested. It works perfectly for your multi-faceted business idea. Here is an analysis and some branding ideas for the name: 1. Why "Fast Fix Solutions" Works FactorAnalysisScalabilityIt’s not tied to a specific location (like "Akron Tech") or a single service (like "PC Repair"). As you expand into neighboring counties or add new services (like Wi-Fi and security), the name remains relevant.ClarityIt instantly tells customers what you offer: Speed ("Fast"), Problem-Solving ("Fix"), and a Comprehensive Approach ("Solutions").Brand PromiseIt aligns with your tiered service model, where the $100 special is the "Fast Fix" entry point, leading to higher-level "Solutions" (custom builds, network integration).SEO/SearchabilityUsing common keywords like "Fix" and "Solutions" makes it easy for customers to find you when they search for local tech repair. 2. Branding & Tagline Ideas To complement the name, you can use a tagline to highlight your unique full-spectrum value proposition: TaglineFocusFast Fix Solutions: The Full-Spectrum Tech Integrator.Emphasizes the wide range of services (PC, Wi-Fi, Security).Fast Fix Solutions: Expert Tech, Built on Trust.Highlights your focus on honesty and transparent pricing.Fast Fix Solutions: From PC Tune-Up to Whole-Home Security.Directly lists your service expansion path for clarity.Fast Fix Solutions: Speed. Precision. Security.Simple, memorable, and covers your three core service areas. 3. Applying the Name to the Agile Plan You can now use Fast Fix Solutions in all your marketing materials for your two main phases: Sprint 1 (The Foundation): Focus your ads on the "Fast Fix" part, promoting the $100 quick-service special to get customers in the door. Sprint 2 (Expansion): Shift the marketing emphasis to the "Solutions" part, introducing the high-value Wi-Fi and Security integration services.
