### GEMINI
Since you are not on public benefits, you leave that specific section (the "Public Benefits" list with checkboxes) blank. However, you must be very thorough with the Monthly Income and Monthly Expenses tables that follow. Because you aren't automatically qualified by a benefit like SNAP, the court will look at your math to see if your expenses leave you with no money to pay the court fees. 1. How to fill out the Monthly Income Table (Page 2) In the "Applicant" column, write your $11/hr wages. Monthly Employment Income: Write roughly $1,906 (this is $11 x 40 hours x 4.33 weeks). All other rows: Write $0 or draw a line through them if you have no other income. TOTAL MONTHLY INCOME: Write $1,906. Spouse Column: Leave this completely blank. 2. How to fill out Monthly Expenses (The most important part) Since you aren't on benefits, this table is what proves you are "indigent" (low income). Don't underestimate your costs. Rent / Mortgage: List what you pay your girlfriend’s dad for your room. Food / Paper Products: Be realistic. Even if you share meals, list what you personally spend on groceries/hygiene. Utilities: List your portion of the electric/water/trash if you chip in. Transportation / Gas: List what you spend on gas to get to work. Phone: List your monthly cell bill. Insurance: If you pay car insurance or health insurance, list it here. Credit Card / Other Loans: If you are making payments on other debts, list the monthly minimums. The Goal: Your Total Monthly Expenses should be very close to (or even higher than) your Total Monthly Income. This shows the Clerk that you literally have $0 left over to pay a $100+ filing fee. 3. Liquid Assets Cash on Hand: Be honest. If you have $20 in your wallet, write $20. Checking/Savings: Write the current balance of your bank account. If it’s $5, write $5. Final Check Before the Courthouse Print Name: Write your full legal name on the line that starts with "I, [Print Name]..." STOP: Do not sign the "Signature" line or fill in the "Notary" section. The Clerk: Take this form and your 3 copies of the Answer to the Stow Courthouse tomorrow. The Signature: You will sign it at the window while the Clerk watches you. Once you finish those tables, you’re ready. Would you like me to help you double-check the final address of the attorney one last time so your Certificate of Service is 100% correct?
