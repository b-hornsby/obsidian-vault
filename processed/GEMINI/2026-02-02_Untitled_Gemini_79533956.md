Thanks for those screenshots—they clarify exactly what you're seeing. Since searching "vesktop" in the Plugins list only showed web-related fixes, we need to go to the app's core configuration. The screen sharing flicker on GNOME 49 Wayland is most likely due to the Electron Capturer fighting the system's PipeWire stream. 1. Find the Hidden Capture Toggle Since it didn't show up in your search, look at the sidebar on the far left of your second and third screenshots. Scroll down to the Vencord Settings section. Click on Vesktop Settings (it's the very last item in that block, just above "Billing Settings"). In the main window that opens, look for a toggle labeled "Use Electron Capturer". Turn this OFF. This forces the app to use the PipeWire portal, which is native to GNOME and should eliminate the buffer-swap flicker. 2. Disable Hardware Acceleration Permanently Since the terminal flag didn't fully resolve it, ensure the app isn't trying to force GPU scheduling in the background. In that same Vesktop Settings menu, find "Hardware Acceleration". Toggle it OFF. Relaunch the app. This is a common workaround for flickering on NVIDIA/Wayland setups where "Explicit Sync" isn't perfectly handled by the application. 3. Verification of System Portals If the flickering persists after toggling the capturer, your CachyOS installation might have multiple portals interfering with each other. Run this command: pacman -Qs xdg-desktop-portal If you see xdg-desktop-portal-wlr or xdg-desktop-portal-kde installed, they may be conflicting with xdg-desktop-portal-gnome. For a clean GNOME 49 experience, you should generally only have the gnome and gtk versions active. Would you like me to walk through how to clean up those portal conflicts if the settings toggle doesn't fix it?
