My bad—I forgot you're using Micro as your editor, not Nano. In Micro, the search shortcut is different. Here is how to do it in Micro: Open the file again: sudo micro /etc/grub.d/30_os-prober Search: Press Ctrl+F (F for Find). Type LONGNAME and hit Enter. The Edit: It will highlight the line. Look for: LONGNAME="${LABEL} (on ${DEVICE})" Change it to: LONGNAME="Tiny11" Save & Exit: Press Ctrl+S to save. Press Ctrl+Q to quit. Apply it: sudo grub-mkconfig -o /boot/grub/grub.cfg Check the terminal output after that last command—does it say "Found Tiny11 on /dev/nvme0n1p1" now?
