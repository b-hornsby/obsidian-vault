# What did BP (gas station) get in trouble for in the earlier 2000s 

### USER
What did BP (gas station) get in trouble for in the earlier 2000s 

### GEMINI
BP was involved in several major accidents and controversies in the early 2000s, primarily related to safety failures and environmental incidents. The most notable incidents include: Texas City Refinery Explosion (March 2005): A massive explosion at BP's refinery in Texas City, Texas, killed 15 workers and injured 170 others. Investigations found that the company had ignored its own safety protocols and that a warning system was disabled. BP was ultimately fined and paid billions in compensation to victims. Prudhoe Bay Oil Spills (2006): BP was forced to temporarily shut down operations at its Prudhoe Bay field in Alaska due to severe corrosion in a pipeline, which led to the largest oil spill on the Alaskan North Slope, dumping over 200,000 gallons of crude oil into the tundra. Reports indicated BP had been warned about the need to check the pipeline years earlier. Market Manipulation Charges: BP settled with the U.S. Department of Justice over charges of manipulating the market price of propane, agreeing to pay hundreds of millions of dollars in fines. These incidents, stemming from failures in safety and maintenance practices, led to large fines and a significant amount of legal and public relations fallout for the company in the years leading up to the 2010 Deepwater Horizon oil spill (which is outside the "earlier 2000s" but became BP's most costly and disastrous accident).
