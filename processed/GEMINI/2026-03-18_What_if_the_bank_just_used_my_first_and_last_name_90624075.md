# What if the bank just used my first and last name

### USER
What if the bank just used my first and last name

### GEMINI
If the bank only used your first and last name, you should list yourself exactly that same way in the "Defendant" section at the top of the form. Consistency is the goal here. The court system is built on matching records. If the bank (the Plaintiff) says they are suing "John Doe," and you file an answer as "John Michael Doe," it can sometimes create a small "hiccup" in their filing system. Here is the rule of thumb for your name: At the Top (The Caption): Write your name exactly as it appears on the Summons/Complaint you received. If they left out your middle name, you leave it out there too. In the "Pro Se" Section: Write your full legal name (including middle). This is where you are providing your official contact info to the court. On the "Form 20" (Fee Waiver): Use your full legal name as it appears on your ID. Since the Clerk has to look at your ID to notarize this, it needs to be your 100% official name. One Final "Brutally Honest" Logistics Check: You mentioned you were served around March 2nd or 4th. Today is March 18th. Day 21: You are officially in the "home stretch." The Goal: Do not let this hit the 28-day mark. The Move: Get to 4400 Courthouse Blvd in Stow by Friday morning. Wait! One last thing: When you get to the Clerk's window, they might ask you for a "filing fee." Remember: There is no fee to file an Answer in Stow. If they mention a fee for something else, that is where your Form 20 Fee Waiver comes in. Hand them that form first.
