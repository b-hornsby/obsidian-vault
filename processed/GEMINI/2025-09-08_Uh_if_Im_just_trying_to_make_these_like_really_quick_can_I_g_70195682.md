# Uh if I'm just trying to make these like really quick, can I get away with using 375 for an hour?

### USER
Uh if I'm just trying to make these like really quick, can I get away with using 375 for an hour?

### GEMINI
Cooking ribs at 375 degrees Fahrenheit for just an hour might make them tough and chewy, not fall-off-the-bone tender. If you're short on time, you could try increasing the heat a bit, maybe to 300 degrees Fahrenheit, and checking on them after about 2-3 hours.
