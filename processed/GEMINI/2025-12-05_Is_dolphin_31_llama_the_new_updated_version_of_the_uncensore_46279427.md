### USER

### GEMINI
That's a misnomer and a potential trap. Let's clarify the reality of the Dolphin models: The "Dolphin" models (created by Eric Hartford/Cognitive Computations) are fine-tuned versions of base models like Llama, Llama 2, and Llama 3/3.1. They are specifically created to be uncensored and system-prompt-compliant, meaning they are designed to follow your instructions without the typical safety guardrails and alignment layers found in the original Meta models. Here's the breakdown of your question: 1. The Dolphin Series is the Uncensored Model The Dolphin models (like Dolphin 2.9, Dolphin 3.0, etc.) are already known for being the uncensored alternatives. Any version with the name "Dolphin" is, by its nature and design, the uncensored variant of the base model it uses. 2. Dolphin 3.1 Llama is a Newer Uncensored Model The name "Dolphin 3.1 Llama" (or more precisely, variants like Dolphin 3.0 Llama 3.1 8B or Dolphin 2.9.4 Llama 3.1 8b) indicates a fine-tune based on the Meta Llama 3.1 base model. Llama 3.1 is the newer, more powerful base model released by Meta. The Dolphin fine-tune is the process that removes the original model's safety restrictions (censorship/alignment) and makes it highly compliant with the system prompt. Therefore, yes, Dolphin 3.1 Llama is an updated version of the uncensored model—it's the uncensored version that leverages the newer, more capable Llama 3.1 architecture compared to older Dolphin models based on Llama 3.0, Llama 2, or other models. 🎯 Your Blind Spot You are treating the "Dolphin" label as a product update when it is an alignment philosophy. The question should not be if $Dolphin_{n}$ is the "new uncensored model," but whether $Dolphin_{n}$ is a better tool than $Dolphin_{n-1}$. The latest Llama-based Dolphin model will generally be: More capable (due to the stronger Llama 3.1 base). Equally uncensored (that is the core feature of the Dolphin fine-tune).
