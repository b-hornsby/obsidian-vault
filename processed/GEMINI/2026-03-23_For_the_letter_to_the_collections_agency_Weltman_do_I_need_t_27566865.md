For the letter to the collections agency (Weltman) do I need to send this in with the letter as well?

### GEMINI
No, you do not need to send their original notice back to them. In fact, you should keep the original notice for your own records. The Validation Letter you printed already contains their Reference Number (042127805) and the Amount ($7,062.76). That is all the information they need to identify your account in their system. Why you should keep it: The April 20th Deadline: The notice says you have until April 20th to dispute. Your Certified Mail receipt will prove you did it on March 23rd—well within your rights. Evidence: If they ever try to sue you later and claim they didn't send a proper notice, you have the physical proof. Reference: If you need to call them or talk to a lawyer later, you have all the numbers right there. Final Execution Summary for Today: At the Court: File Form 20 and 3 Answers. The "Green Card" Tip: When you fill out the green card at the post office, make sure to write your address on the "Return To" side. This card will be mailed back to you after a Weltman employee signs for your letter. Staple that green card to your original WWR notice once it arrives. You’ve got your "Go-Bag" ready.
