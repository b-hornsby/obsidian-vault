# So currently I am not on any public benefits. What do I do under that section?

### USER
So currently I am not on any public benefits. What do I do under that section?

### GEMINI
If you aren't on any public benefits, simply leave that section blank or write "None" or "N/A" next to the list of benefits. Not being on benefits does not disqualify you. In Ohio, you qualify for the mandatory fee waiver based on two things: Income: Your gross income is at or below 187.5% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines. Expenses: Your monthly expenses are equal to or greater than your income/assets. The Math for 2026 For a household of one in Ohio, the 187.5% threshold is approximately $2,430 per month. Since you make $11/hour (roughly $1,906/month gross), you are well within the limit to qualify even without SNAP or Medicaid. How to fill out the rest of the form to ensure approval: Since you aren't checking a "Public Benefits" box (which is an automatic qualifier), the court will look closely at your Monthly Income and Monthly Expenses tables on the next page. Income Table: Be precise. List your $11/hr wages. If you have no other income (no side hustles, no dividends, etc.), put $0 in those other boxes. Expenses Table: This is where you prove you truly can't afford the fee. List everything: Rent/Room payment to your girlfriend's dad. Gas for your car to get to work. Your phone bill. Groceries. Average hygiene/cleaning supplies. Assets Section: If you have less than a few hundred dollars in your checking account, list that amount honestly. If you have no stocks or bonds, put $0. The "Discretionary" Safety Net Even if someone's income was slightly higher, Ohio law (R.C. 2323.311) allows judges to grant a "discretionary waiver" if they see that paying the court fee would cause a hardship. By showing that your $11/hr is entirely eaten up by basic living costs, you are making a very strong case for that waiver. Your Final Plan for Tomorrow Complete the Form: Fill in the income/expenses but do not sign. The Window: Go to the Stow Clerk. The Hand-off: Hand them your Answer and the Form 20. The Oath: They will ask you to swear the information is true, you'll sign, they'll stamp it, and you're done.
