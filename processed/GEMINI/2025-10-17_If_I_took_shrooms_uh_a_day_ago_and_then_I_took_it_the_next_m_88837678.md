# If I took shrooms uh a day ago and then I took it the next morning like the rest of the mushroom, how long does it take for the effects to kick in?

### USER
If I took shrooms uh a day ago and then I took it the next morning like the rest of the mushroom, how long does it take for the effects to kick in?

### GEMINI
Since you took shrooms yesterday, the effects of the dose you just took might be a little different. It could take anywhere from 30 minutes to two hours to feel them, but it depends on a few things. How much you ate, your metabolism, and even what you've eaten recently can all play a role. Just a reminder that I don't provide medical advice or diagnoses. You should always talk to a medical professional.
