Ah, okay, I see the issue now based on your second screenshot. My apologies, it seems I was focusing on the wrong scrollbar. The problem isn't that you need to scroll down within that right panel, it's that your browser window itself is not tall enough to display all the content of the Oobabooga UI. Look closely at your screenshot: The very bottom of the entire web interface is cut off, right where the "Send a message" box is, and there's no overall browser scrollbar indicating more content below. Here's how to fix it: Maximize your browser window: If your browser window is not maximized, click the square icon in the top-right corner (between the minimize and close buttons) to make it full screen. Reduce your browser's zoom level: In most browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox), you can press Ctrl + - (Control key and minus key) a few times to zoom out. Alternatively, go to your browser's menu (usually three dots or lines in the top-right) and find the "Zoom" option, then reduce it to 90% or 75%. Once you do that, the entire Oobabooga interface should fit, and you will see the "Context," "Greeting," and "Example Dialogue" text boxes appear below the "Count tokens" button in that right-hand panel, as shown here: (Image from Oobabooga GitHub, showing the full character section) Try those steps, and let me know if you can now see the full character definition area!
