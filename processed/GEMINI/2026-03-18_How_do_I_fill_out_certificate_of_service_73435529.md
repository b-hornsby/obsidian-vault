# How do I fill out certificate of service?

### USER
How do I fill out certificate of service?

### GEMINI
The Certificate of Service is your proof to the court that you aren't playing games and have officially sent a copy of your Answer to the bank's lawyer. If this isn't filled out correctly, the court can "strike" your Answer, which leads back to that default judgment we’re avoiding. Based on the summons you uploaded, here is exactly how to fill in those lines at the bottom of your form: 1. Identify the Recipient On the line that says "A copy of this document was served upon Plaintiff or upon Plaintiff’s attorney at the following address:", write: James P. McGowan, Zwicker & Associates P.C., 4511 Rockside Road, Suite 210, Independence, OH 44131 2. Method and Date Method: On the line after "by", write "Ordinary U.S. Mail". The Date: On the lines for the date, write the current day you are dropping it in the mail. Example: 19th day of March, 2026. 3. The Final Signature (Filer’s Signature): Sign your name here. This must be the same signature you used in the middle of the form. Pro-Tip: The "Notary" Trap You'll notice your Answer form doesn't have a specific spot for a Notary, but your Form 20 (Fee Waiver) definitely does. The Answer: You can sign this at home right now. The Fee Waiver: DO NOT sign that until you are standing at the Clerk’s window in Stow. They will act as the Notary. Your Final Logistics Checklist 3 Copies: Print/copy the filled-out Answer three times. The Filing: Take all 3 to the Stow Clerk (4400 Courthouse Blvd). They keep one and "Time-Stamp" two for you. The Mail: Take one of those time-stamped copies, put it in an envelope, and mail it to James McGowan's office in Independence. Do this the same day you file. The Receipt: Keep the 3rd time-stamped copy for your own records. If they ever claim you didn't respond, that stamped paper is your shield. You've got this. By Friday, you can have this entirely off your plate and be legally protected while you continue your IT job search.
