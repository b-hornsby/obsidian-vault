Part 1: Filling out the "Defendant's Answer" Looking at the photo of your form, here is exactly what to write in each section: The Top Header: Plaintiff: Copy the name exactly as it appears on your summons (likely a bank like Discover or Amex). Defendant: Your full name. Case No: Copy the number from your summons (e.g., 2026 CV 0XXXX). The Body (The lines in the middle): Write these exact words. They are designed to protect your rights without you having to prove anything yet: "Defendant admits his name and address as stated in the Complaint." (This is a standard "safe" admission). "Defendant denies the allegations regarding the debt amount and ownership for lack of knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief as to the truth of the allegations." (This is the most important line. It forces them to produce original contracts and proof of the $8k math). "Defendant denies all other allegations in the Complaint and demands strict proof thereof." "I ask the Court to:" Write: "To dismiss the Plaintiff's Complaint at Plaintiff's cost, and for any other relief the Court deems just."
