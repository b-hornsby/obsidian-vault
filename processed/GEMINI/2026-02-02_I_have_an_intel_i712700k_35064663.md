Since you are on an Intel i7-12700K, the "Global C-state" setting won't be under an AMD menu. It will be under your CPU Power Management settings. Where to find it on Intel: Enter BIOS (use systemctl reboot --firmware-setup again if you need to). Go to Advanced Mode (usually F7). Navigate to Advanced -> CPU Configuration. Scroll down to CPU - Power Management Control. Look for CPU C-states. Set this to Disabled.
