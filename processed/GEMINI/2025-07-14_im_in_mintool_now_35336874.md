Excellent! MiniTool Partition Wizard is the right tool for this. From your screenshot of MiniTool Partition Wizard, I can see: Disk 1 is your internal drive (Samsung SSD 970 EVO Plus, 1.82 TB). Disk 2 is your external 4TB Seagate drive (3.64 TB total). It correctly shows "E:4tb drive" (2048.0 GB, NTFS, Primary) and then a "1678.0 GB Unallocated" space next to it. The key here is that the unallocated space is immediately to the right of your E: drive, which makes extending it straightforward with MiniTool Partition Wizard. Here's how to extend your E: drive to its full 4TB capacity using MiniTool Partition Wizard: Select the "E:4tb drive" Partition: In MiniTool Partition Wizard, click once on the blue bar representing your "E:4tb drive" partition under Disk 2. It should highlight. Choose "Extend Partition": On the left sidebar, under "Change Partition," click on "Extend Partition." Extend Partition Window: A new "Extend Partition" window will appear. Take Free Space from: This dropdown should show "Unallocated Space" (or similar, corresponding to the 1678.0 GB). Select it if it's not already. New Partition Size: You'll see a slider. Drag the slider all the way to the right to utilize the entire 1678.0 GB of unallocated space. The "Partition Size" should update to show the combined total (around 3726 GB). Click "OK." Apply the Changes: Back in the main MiniTool Partition Wizard window, you'll see the E: drive now visually extended, and the "Unallocated" space should be gone. IMPORTANT: These changes are only pending. To apply them, you must click the "Apply" button in the bottom-left corner of the MiniTool Partition Wizard window (it looks like a blue checkmark). A confirmation window will appear. Click "Yes" to start the operation. MiniTool Partition Wizard will then perform the extension. This process might take a few moments. Once it's complete, your E: drive will be formatted to its full 4TB capacity. You can then close MiniTool Partition Wizard and verify in Windows Disk Management or File Explorer.
