### GEMINI
Since you were served around March 4th, your 28-day window closes on April 1st. You have everything you need to hit the "pause" button on this lawsuit and protect your income. Here is exactly what to write on your form to keep your defenses up and force them to prove their $8k math. 1. The Header (Top of the Form) Plaintiff: [Copy the name from your summons—e.g., American Express or Discover] Case No.: [Copy the Case Number from your summons—likely starts with "2026 CV"] Defendant: Your Full Name 2. The Body (Your Response) Write these exact lines. Even if the contract is attached, these phrases protect you by requiring them to prove the specifics of the debt and their legal right to sue you. "Defendant admits his name and address as stated in the Complaint." "Defendant is without knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief as to the truth of the remaining allegations in the Complaint and therefore denies them." "Defendant specifically denies the accuracy of the alleged balance and demands strict proof of the chain of ownership of the subject debt." 3. The Request I ask the Court to: Write: "Dismiss the Plaintiff’s Complaint at Plaintiff's cost, and for any other relief the Court deems just." 4. The Bottom (Personal Info) Check the box for (Pro se). Fill in your current Address, City/State/Zip, and Telephone Number. 5. Certificate of Service (Crucial) This part proves you told the other side you are fighting the case. Served upon: Write "James P. McGowan, 4511 Rockside Road, Suite 210, Independence, OH 44131". By: Write "Ordinary U.S. Mail". Date: The date you drop it in the mailbox. Sign: Your signature. Your Checklist to Finish The Form 20 (Fee Waiver): Take the printed Form 20 with you to the court. Do not sign it yet. The Trip to Stow: Go to 4400 Courthouse Blvd, Stow, OH. Tell the clerk: "I am here to file an Answer and a Civil Fee Waiver (Form 20)." Sign the Form 20 in front of them (they will notarize it for free). Hand them 3 copies of the Answer. They will "Time-Stamp" them. The Mail: Take one of those stamped copies, put it in a stamped envelope, and mail it to the lawyer in Independence. Once you do this, you are legally safe from an automatic loss. This buys you the time you need to find that better job and reach out to Community Legal Aid (330-535-4191) to help you settle for a fraction of the $8,000 later.
