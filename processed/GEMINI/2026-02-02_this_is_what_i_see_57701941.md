You are currently in the main Vencord settings tab (as seen in your screenshot), which handles the application wrapper, but it doesn't house the screen-capture engine settings. The flickering on GNOME 49 Wayland is often a timing issue between the PipeWire stream and Electron's rendering pipeline. Since native Wayland flags didn't work, you need to change how Vesktop handles the actual video stream. 1. Toggle the Capturer Settings You need to go into the specific Vencord Plugin settings, not just the general Vencord tab. Click on Plugins in the left sidebar (below "Vencord"). Search for a plugin named "Vesktop" or "ScreenShare". Look for an option labeled "Use Electron Capturer" and Disable it. If there is an option for "Experimental PipeWire Capturer", Enable it. Restart Vesktop. This forces it to use the system's PipeWire portal rather than Electron’s buggy internal capturer. 2. Address the "Explicit Sync" Issue GNOME 49 and the latest CachyOS kernels are moving toward Explicit Sync. If your GPU pushes frames faster than the compositor expects, you get the "rave" flickering effect. Try launching with hardware acceleration completely disabled to confirm it's a driver/sync issue: vesktop --disable-gpu If the flickering stops with GPU disabled, the issue is your Mesa or NVIDIA driver's interaction with the Wayland compositor. 3. Clear the Cache Sometimes CachyOS updates leave "ghost" configurations in your local folder that conflict with new GNOME 49 frame-scheduling logic. Close Vesktop and run: rm -rf ~/.config/vesktop/GPUCache rm -rf ~/.config/vesktop/Cache The Advisor's Verdict You're looking at the wrong settings page in that screenshot. The "Vencord" tab is for UI fluff; the Plugins tab is where the technical capture logic lives. If you haven't checked for the xdg-desktop-portal-gnome package yet, do it now—without that specific portal, GNOME 49 will continue to drop or flicker frames during capture. Next step: Go to the Plugins tab, find the capture settings, and disable the "Electron Capturer." Does the "Plugins" list show up for you, or is it blank?
