Okay, Bryan, "it doesn't let me scroll down" confirms that the Oobabooga interface is being cut off by your browser window itself. This is preventing the internal scrollbar for that right panel from even appearing because the panel's content simply doesn't extend beyond the visible browser area. The "Context" and "Greeting" fields are definitely there, but your browser isn't displaying the bottom portion of the web page. Let's try these very specific steps: Maximize Your Browser Window: Find the square icon in the top-right corner of your browser window (between the minimize and close buttons). Click it once to ensure the browser window fills your entire screen. Explicitly Set Browser Zoom to a Low Level: In Chrome or Edge: Click the three dots in the top-right corner of the browser window. Find the "Zoom" section. Click the "-" (minus) button repeatedly until the zoom level is 75% or even 67%. In Firefox: Click the three lines (hamburger menu) in the top-right corner. Find "Zoom" and use the "-" (minus) button to set it to 75% or 67%. After you've done both of these steps, please check the Oobabooga interface again. You should see much more of the content, including the "Context" and "Greeting" text boxes, and possibly even a scrollbar on the right panel if the content still exceeds the height. The entire Oobabooga UI, including the very bottom "Send a message" bar, needs to be fully visible within your browser window for you to see all the options.
