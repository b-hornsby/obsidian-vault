# With iOS 26.3 I’m noticing crazy battery drain. Using an iPhone 15 plus with 90% maximum capacity. Any settings I can turn off to help maintain better battery life and fix this? 

### USER
With iOS 26.3 I’m noticing crazy battery drain. Using an iPhone 15 plus with 90% maximum capacity. Any settings I can turn off to help maintain better battery life and fix this? 

### GEMINI
Look, let’s be direct: your iPhone 15 Plus is entering its "middle-age" phase with that 90% battery capacity, and iOS 26.3 is a resource-heavy beast. You’re likely dealing with a combination of post-update background indexing and a software version that is pushing your aging hardware harder than before. If you want to stop "playing small" with your settings and actually see a difference, here is the objective, high-level audit of what you need to change. 1. The High-Impact "Trim" These are the settings that actively eat cycles and data in the background. Stop making excuses for "convenience" and turn them off. Adaptive Power Mode: This is the big one in iOS 26. While it's designed to be smart, users are reporting it causes massive idle drain on older hardware like the 15 series. Turn it off and see if stability returns. Settings > Battery > Power Mode > Toggle off Adaptive Power. Background App Refresh: You don't need 50 apps checking for updates while you're sleeping. Settings > General > Background App Refresh > Off (or select only 2-3 essential apps). Apple Intelligence (Siri & Summarization): If you aren't actively using the new AI writing tools or notification summaries, they are a literal weight on your processor. Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri > Toggle Off. 2. The Strategic "Hardware" Adjustments Your 15 Plus has a great screen, but that screen is your biggest power draw. Location Services Audit: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. If an app is set to "Always," it is a parasite. Set everything to "While Using" and turn off Precise Location for anything that isn't a map or weather app. Motion & Transparency: These "Liquid Glass" UI effects look nice but require GPU cycles. Settings > Accessibility > Motion > Reduce Motion (On). Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Reduce Transparency (On). The Brutal Reality Check Your battery health is at 90%. In Apple-speak, that’s "Peak Performance Capability," but in reality, you’ve lost 10% of your fuel tank. Every software update (especially 26.3) adds weight to the car. The Opportunity Cost: If you spend your whole day worrying about the percentage, you’re losing mental bandwidth. If these changes don't work within 48 hours (the time it takes for indexing to finish), your install might be "dirty." The Prioritized Plan Immediate Action: Perform a Force Restart (Volume Up, Volume Down, then hold the Side Button until the Apple logo appears). This kills hung background processes that a standard "slide to power off" might miss. The 48-Hour Rule: Wait two full days. If the drain is still "crazy," go to Settings > Battery and look at the "Activity by App" list. If "Home & Lock Screen" or "Siri" is over 20%, you have a software loop. The Nuclear Option: If the drain persists, Reset All Settings (not a factory reset, just settings). It’s a pain to set up your wallpaper and Wi-Fi again, but it often clears the cache conflicts that cause post-update drain.
