This involves bringing assets like treasury bonds and other real-world financial instruments onto the blockchain. The development of the "Ondo Chain" is a key part of this, as it's a layer 1 blockchain specifically designed for institutional-grade RWAs. Hardware Wallet Considerations with RWAs: While standard ERC-20 token compatibility is a base, RWAs can have unique characteristics. These can include: Regulatory requirements. Corporate actions (like stock splits). Specific data and compliance needs. Therefore, full support for RWAs may require more than just basic token storage. It might involve: Wallet software updates to handle specific RWA data. Integration with compliance tools. Potentially, specialized wallet features. It is important to understand that the Ondo Chain is still relatively new, and the wallet support for the specific functions that the Ondo chain provides will be evolving. Key Takeaways: Hardware wallets that support Ethereum (like Ledger devices) can likely hold the Ondo (ONDO) token itself, as it's an ERC-20 token. However, the full range of RWA-related functionalities that Ondo is developing might require ongoing software updates and integrations from wallet providers. It is very important to keep up to date with the latest information from both Ondo finance, and the hardware wallet providers themselves. I hope this clarifies the situation.
