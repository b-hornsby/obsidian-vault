Thanks for sharing the screenshot! Ah, I see what's happening. You're currently on the "Settings" tab, which is showing "Extensions & flags." This tab is for general UI settings and some command-line flags, not the main "Extensions" page where you install and manage individual extensions. To get to the place where you can properly enable and configure the RAG extension: Look at the left-hand navigation menu. Click on the "Extensions" tab. It's usually listed directly above "Model" and below "Training" (or near the bottom of the list). It looks like a puzzle piece icon. Once you click on the "Extensions" tab, you will see a much longer list of available extensions, each with an "Install" button and, once installed, an "Enable" checkbox and a "Configure" button (like a gear icon). That's where you'll find the rag_agent (or similar) extension to enable it and begin the ingestion process. Let me know when you've clicked that "Extensions" tab and are on the correct page!
