# is it not under character in the top left part? 

### USER
is it not under character in the top left part? 

### GEMINI
You're absolutely right to point that out, Bryan! Yes, clicking on "Character" in the top-left navigation panel (the list that also has "Chat", "Parameters", "Model", etc.) will take you directly to the dedicated "Character" configuration page. However, the "Character" section you are currently seeing on the right-hand side panel while in the "Chat" tab is actually the exact same set of settings. Oobabooga often puts commonly used settings from other tabs directly into the "Chat" tab's right panel for convenience. The core issue remains that the entire Oobabooga web interface is not fully visible in your browser window. Whether you access the character settings via the "Chat" tab's right panel or by clicking the "Character" tab on the left, the bottom part of those settings (where "Context," "Greeting," and "Example Dialogue" are located) will still be cut off if your browser window is too small or zoomed in too much. So, please try again: Maximize your web browser window. Reduce your browser's zoom level (e.g., to 90% or 75%) by pressing Ctrl + - a few times. This should make the entire Oobabooga interface fit into your screen, revealing those crucial text boxes you're looking for!
