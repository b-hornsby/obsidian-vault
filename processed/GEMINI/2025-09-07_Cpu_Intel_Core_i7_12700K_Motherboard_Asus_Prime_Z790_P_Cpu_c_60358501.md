Based on the specifications you've provided, your PC is more than capable of streaming stably on both Twitch and TikTok, even while playing demanding games. Here's a breakdown of why: CPU: Intel Core i7-12700K This is an excellent processor with a high core and thread count. It can easily handle running a demanding game, streaming software (like OBS), and other background applications simultaneously without a significant drop in performance. The CPU's power means you can even use a CPU-based encoder (x264) for higher-quality streams if you prefer, though your GPU's encoder is likely a better choice. GPU: PNY GeForce RTX™ 3070 8GB This is a fantastic graphics card for gaming and streaming. The NVIDIA RTX series has a dedicated hardware encoder called NVENC. This encoder handles the video encoding process for your stream, which significantly reduces the performance impact on your CPU and allows you to maintain high in-game frame rates. You can stream at 1080p60fps with high quality and still have plenty of power left over for a smooth gaming experience. Memory: Corsair Vengeance DDR5 32GB (2x16GB) 4800MHz CL40 32GB of RAM is more than enough for gaming and streaming. Storage: 970 Evo Plus M.2 SSD 2TB An M.2 SSD is perfect for fast game load times and is also ideal for recording local VODs of your streams. You won't have any issues with data transfer speeds while streaming. Overall Assessment: Your PC is a high-end streaming machine. You will be able to stream at a high quality (e.g., 1080p, 60fps) on both Twitch and TikTok without issue. The bottleneck will not be your hardware, but rather your internet upload speed. The most important factor now is your internet connection's upload speed. For 1080p 60fps streaming on Twitch, a minimum stable upload speed of around 6-8 Mbps is recommended, but 10 Mbps or higher is even better to account for any fluctuations. For TikTok, the requirements are often less demanding, but a good connection is still key for a stable broadcast. Your PC is powerful enough that you can use the same high-quality settings as you would for Twitch. Before you start, run a speed test (e.g., on Speedtest.net) to find your actual upload speed. As long as it meets the recommended speeds for the quality you want to stream at, your PC will perform exceptionally well.
