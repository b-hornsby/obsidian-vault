If it's still grayed out, you're likely facing a specific browser-to-hardware communication block. Let’s stop guessing and fix the actual bottleneck. Here is the exact checklist. Do not skip any, or it won't work. 1. Kill the "Ledger Live" App This is the #1 reason for the grayed-out button. Why: The Ledger Live desktop app "hijacks" the USB port. Only one program can talk to your Ledger at a time. Action: Completely close Ledger Live on your computer. Don't just minimize it; kill it in your Task Manager/Activity Monitor. 2. The Browser "Permission" Fix Phantom needs explicit permission to see your USB devices. Action: Go to your browser address bar and type chrome://settings/content/usbDevices (or brave://settings/content/usbDevices). Check: Look for Phantom in the list. If it’s there, remove it and try again to trigger a fresh "Allow" pop-up. Crucial: Ensure you are using Chrome or Brave. Firefox does not support Ledger on Phantom. 3. Hardware State (The "Ready" Check) If your Ledger isn't in the exact right state, Phantom ignores it. Action: 1. Connect via USB (Bluetooth doesn't work for the desktop extension). 2. Enter your PIN. 3. Open the Solana App on the device. 4. Screen must say: "Solana is ready". (If it says "Bolos" or you're on the dashboard, it will stay grayed out). 4. Enable "Blind Signing" Even if you get it to connect, you won't be able to move funds without this. Action: While inside the Solana app on your Ledger, go to Settings → Blind Signing → Change to "Yes" or "Enabled". The "Hail Mary" Strategy If you have done all of the above and it is still grayed out, your Phantom extension might be "stuck" in a bad state. Switch Browsers: If you’re on Chrome, install Brave. If you’re on Brave, try Chrome. Install a fresh Phantom extension there. Solflare Alternative: Solflare is the other major Solana wallet. It often has a more robust Ledger connection than Phantom. Go to Solflare.com, click Access Wallet, and select Ledger. If Solflare can see your Ledger but Phantom can't, you know the issue is specifically the Phantom extension's software. Are you currently on a Mac or Windows, and which browser are you using? Knowing this lets me tell you the exact USB driver/privacy setting that is likely blocking you.
