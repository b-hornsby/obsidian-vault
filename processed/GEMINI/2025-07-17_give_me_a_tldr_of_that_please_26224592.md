Given your Noctua NH-U12S cooler, your i7-12700K overclocking will be limited by heat. TL;DR Overclocking Goal: P-cores: Aim for 5.0 GHz all-core initially. You might reach 5.1 GHz if temps allow, but 5.2 GHz is unlikely with your cooler. E-cores: Target 4.0 GHz all-core. Vcore (Voltage): Start with 1.25V Adaptive Mode. Keep it below 1.35V for daily use and to manage heat. Temperatures: Crucially, keep temps below 90°C during stress tests. If they hit 95°C+, reduce clocks/voltage. Process: Make small changes in BIOS, then stress test thoroughly (Cinebench, OCCT, Prime95) while monitoring temperatures with HWInfo64. Adjust and repeat until stable and cool. Your cooler is the main constraint, so be realistic with your expectations for "maximum performance."
