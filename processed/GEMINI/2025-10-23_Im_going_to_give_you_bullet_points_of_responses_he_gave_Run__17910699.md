The Game Plan: Phased Approach Based on this analysis, the best strategy is to phase in your cousin's suggestions, starting with your core competency and leveraging the best upsides. Phase 1: Launch & PC Domination (1-3 Months) Launch Service: Finalize your $\mathbf{$100}$ Special (e.g., "Full System Tune-up + Virus Check") to get initial customers and reviews. Tiered Pricing: Immediately define and market your two higher tiers (e.g., "Data Recovery" and "Custom Build Consultation") to maximize ATV. Core Focus: Focus 90% of effort on PC Fixes, Custom Builds, and Emulation—your highest-expertise areas. Marketing: Use Facebook/Google Business Profile to advertise your PC service and start incorporating affiliate links for recommended software/gear. Phase 2: Tech Expansion (3-6 Months) Networking Launch: Introduce "Whole-Home Wi-Fi Optimization" as your first expanded service. This leverages existing PC/Networking knowledge and has minimal new tool/inventory cost. Sourcing Setup: Establish a relationship with one or two wholesale/distributor suppliers for parts (RAM, SSDs, etc.). Implement the retail/wholesale pricing model. Test Security: Take on one or two simple home security installation jobs (e.g., a simple Wi-Fi camera setup) for friends/family to gauge the time/skill requirement before formally marketing it. Phase 3: Scaling & Security Integration (6+ Months) Full Security Launch: If Phase 2 tests were successful, formally launch the Security Camera Installation service. Purchase proper liability insurance and a basic inventory (e.g., a couple of Costco security kits). Hire/Partner: As demand grows, consider hiring a low-voltage partner or apprentice to handle the physical installation aspects of networking and security while you focus on high-value PC repair and diagnostics.
