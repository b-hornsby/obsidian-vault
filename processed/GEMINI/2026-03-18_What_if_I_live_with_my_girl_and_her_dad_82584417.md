# What if I live with my girl and her dad?

### USER
What if I live with my girl and her dad?

### GEMINI
Living with your girlfriend and her dad is actually common, but you need to handle it correctly on Form 20 so you don't accidentally get disqualified for the fee waiver. In the eyes of the court, "Household" usually means people who share income and expenses, not just people who share a roof. Here is the move: 1. How to list them on Form 20 There is a section for "Other Persons Living in Your Household." The Rule: If you are not married to her and her dad doesn't support you financially (or vice versa), they are technically roommates, not a "household unit." The Action: List their names and your relationship (e.g., "Girlfriend" and "Roommate/Landlord"), but in the Income section, only list your $11/hr. Do not list their paychecks. You are an independent adult responsible for your own $8,000 debt. 2. Handling the "Expenses" This is where living with them actually helps your case: Rent: Even if you aren't on an official lease, list the amount you pay them each month for your "room." If it’s $400, write $400. Utilities: If you chip in $50 for the electric or internet, list that. Food: List what you personally spend on groceries. 3. The "Judgment Proof" Strategy The court wants to see if you can afford the court fees. By showing that you make $1,900/month but spend $1,800 on rent, food, and gas while living in a shared house, you are proving that you have zero "disposable income" for a law firm to take. Why this matters for the $8k debt: By filing this Form 20 while living in a "roommate" situation, you are sending a clear signal to the bank's lawyer: "I don't own this house, I don't own these assets, and I barely make enough to pay my roommates rent." This makes you a very unattractive target for them. Final Check for Tomorrow: Place: Stow Municipal Court (4400 Courthouse Blvd). Time: Aim for the morning (8:00 AM – 11:00 AM) so you aren't rushed. ID: Do not forget your State ID. The Answer: 3 copies. The Form 20: 1 copy (unsigned).
