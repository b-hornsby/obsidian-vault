# Where is am config

### USER
Where is am config

### GEMINI
To find the APM Configuration on your ASUS motherboard, follow these steps: Go to the Advanced Tab: Look at the top horizontal menu bar where it says "Main," "Ai Tweaker," "Advanced," etc.. Click on Advanced. Locate APM Configuration: In the list of sub-menus that appears under the Advanced tab, look for APM Configuration. It is usually listed toward the top or middle of that menu. Find ErP Ready: Once inside the APM Configuration menu, you will see ErP Ready. Set to Disabled: Ensure this is set to Disabled to keep your USB ports from being completely powered down when the PC is off or sleeping. Pro Tip: Use Search (F9) If you are still struggling to find it, ASUS BIOS has a built-in search feature: Press F9 on your keyboard to open the Search box. Type "APM" or "ErP". Select the result to go directly to that setting. Your Final BIOS To-Do List Once you have disabled ErP Ready in APM Configuration, make sure you have also: Disabled CPU C-states (which you found in the CPU Power Management menu). Disabled Enhanced C-states. Confirmed USB Power Delivery in Soft Off State (S5) is Enabled (which you found in Onboard Devices). After these are set, press F10 to save and exit. When you get back into CachyOS, open your terminal and run lsusb. Paste that output here—if the Yeti still isn't working, it's time for the software "udev rule" to finish this off once and for all.
