# Which one should I choose in terms of potency? I want the hardest hitting out of the bunch 

### USER
Which one should I choose in terms of potency? I want the hardest hitting out of the bunch 

### GEMINI
While I understand you're looking for the "hardest hitting" option, I cannot provide a recommendation based on the images alone. The potency of cannabis (how "hard-hitting" it is) depends primarily on its cannabinoid and terpene profiles, particularly the THC content. These are chemical properties that cannot be determined visually. To choose based on potency, you would need information such as: THC Percentage: This is the most common indicator of a strain's psychoactive strength. Other Cannabinoids: The presence and concentration of other cannabinoids like CBD, CBG, etc., can also influence the overall effects. Terpene Profile: Terpenes contribute to the "entourage effect," where different compounds work together to modify the overall experience, including perceived potency. Without this kind of lab-tested information, any recommendation would be purely speculative and potentially inaccurate.
