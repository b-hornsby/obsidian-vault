# Business name for PC repair and custom builds shop

### HUMAN
Working through a business idea and I need help with coming up with a name. Generally speaking the idea is to be able to do pc/some electronic repair, custom pc builds (if the client brings me the parts), emulation help, pc optimization and maybe even scale into security equipment installation (cameras, alarms, etc). As far as naming goes I don’t want it to sound like a store front (bestbuy, micro center, etc) and I also don’t want it to sound like some futuristic thing. I want it to roll off the tongue well and I want it to be a place that people can be like “I took it to ____ and they got my stuff fixed quick”

### ASSISTANT
I like where you're headed with this - you want something that feels personal, trustworthy, and service-oriented rather than corporate or gimmicky.

Here are some directions that might work:

**Name-based (if you're comfortable using yours):**
- "[Your name]'s Tech Services"
- "[Last name] Technical"
- "[Your name] Tech Solutions"

These work really well for the "I took it to Miller's and they fixed it right up" vibe you're after.

**Place/Community feel:**
- "Main Street Tech Repair"
- "Corner Tech" 
- "Local Tech Services"
- "Neighborhood Tech Repair"

**Straightforward and trustworthy:**
- "Reliable Tech Services"
- "Trusted Tech Repair"
- "Right Tech Solutions"
- "Solid State Services" (plays on the tech angle subtly)

**Action/service-focused:**
- "Quick Fix Tech"
- "Tech Right Services"
- "Done Right Technical"

My personal favorites from these would be something name-based if you're open to it, or "Solid State Services" - it's got that subtle tech reference without being over the top, it sounds professional, and "I took it to Solid State" rolls off the tongue pretty naturally.

What kind of vibe feels most like you? Are you thinking more personal/local feel, or completely professional and neutral?

### HUMAN
Definitely looking for a neutral vibe here. I want it to be something I can scale into an LLC down the road but like I said it shouldn’t sound too much like a store front sort of name

### ASSISTANT
Got it - neutral and scalable but still service-focused. Here are some options that thread that needle:

**Clean and professional:**
- "Apex Technical Services"
- "Summit Tech Solutions"
- "Cornerstone Technical"
- "Foundation Tech Services"
- "Precision Tech Solutions"

**Slightly more distinctive:**
- "Benchmark Technical" (tech term, sounds professional)
- "Clearpath Tech Services"
- "Tether Technical" (suggests connection/reliability)
- "Anchor Tech Solutions"
- "Keystone Technical Services"

**Simple and direct:**
- "Merit Tech Services"
- "True North Technical"
- "Level Tech Solutions"
- "Prime Technical Services"

I think **"Benchmark Technical"** or **"Cornerstone Technical"** hit that sweet spot - they're professional enough to scale into an LLC, they don't sound like retail, and phrases like "I took it to Benchmark" or "Cornerstone got it sorted out" flow naturally in conversation.

The word "Technical" or "Tech Services" as the second part keeps it clear what you do without sounding like a store, while the first word gives you that professional edge.

Any of these resonating with you, or want me to explore a different angle?

### HUMAN
i like the idea of it ending in tech services or technical more than solutions to be completely honest but i'm not sure about any of these. i want it to be creative/eye catching to the average person. the name should convey trust, speed and accuracy if that helps any

Any of these hitting closer to what you're imagining?
