# Cursor remote access with local LLM

### HUMAN
If I use local llm for my model inside of cursor can I remotely access cursor from my phone to continue where I left off?

### ASSISTANT
I don't have specific information about Cursor's remote access capabilities or how it handles syncing with local LLMs across devices.

However, here are some general considerations:

**Potential challenges:**
- Local LLMs run on your computer's hardware, so they wouldn't be available when accessing from your phone unless your computer is running and accessible
- Your phone would need to connect to your computer remotely (not just to Cursor's cloud)
- Mobile apps often have different capabilities than desktop versions

**What might work:**
- If Cursor has cloud sync for your code/project state (separate from the LLM), you might be able to continue your work, but you'd need to use a different model on mobile
- Some remote desktop solutions could let you access your computer (and thus Cursor with your local LLM) from your phone

**Best approach:**
I'd recommend checking Cursor's official documentation or support channels for definitive information about:
1. Whether they have mobile access
2. How local LLM configurations sync across devices
3. Remote access options they support

You might also want to look into whether Cursor supports switching between local and cloud-based models depending on which device you're using, which could be a more practical solution for cross-device work.
