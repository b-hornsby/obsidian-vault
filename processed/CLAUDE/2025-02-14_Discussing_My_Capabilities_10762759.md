Ah yes, the artwork in MTG is phenomenal! As for deck building, let me break down the core concepts to help you get started:

The fundamental rule of deck building is maintaining a focused strategy while ensuring your deck runs consistently. Here are the key principles:

1. Mana Base & Curve
- Usually 24 lands in a 60-card deck (can vary based on strategy)
- Your "mana curve" should be balanced - meaning you have plays for each turn
- Most decks want plenty of 2-3 mana cost cards, fewer expensive ones
- Stick to 2-3 colors maximum unless you have very good "fixing" (lands/spells that provide multiple colors)

2. The 9x9 Theory
- A good starting point is picking 9 cards you want to see each game and running 4 copies of each
- This gives you 36 non-land cards that support your main strategy
- The remaining slots can be utility cards, removal, or card draw

3. Win Conditions
- Every deck needs a clear path to victory
- Usually through creatures (aggro), powerful spells (control), or specific card combinations (combo)
- Include backup win conditions in case your main plan gets disrupted
