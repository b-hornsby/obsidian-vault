# Frequency of AI solution posts on LinkedIn

### HUMAN
is it too much having privatized ai solutions on my linkedin too many times?

### ASSISTANT
That's a fair thing to question. The short answer: it depends on *how* it shows up, not just *how often*.

A few things to consider:

**When repetition is fine**
If "AI solutions" appears in different contexts — a project, a skill, a summary line — that's natural. Recruiters and ATS systems actually reward consistent keyword usage to a point.

**When it becomes a problem**
- If it reads like keyword stuffing ("leveraged AI solutions to build AI-powered AI solutions...")
- If it's vague filler without substance behind it — *what* AI, *what* outcome?
- If it crowds out more concrete, technical language that would actually land better for IT support/helpdesk roles

**The bigger risk for your situation**
For the roles you're targeting (IT Support Specialist, Data Center Tech, etc.), "privatized AI solutions" is a bit abstract and might actually *dilute* your profile rather than strengthen it. Hiring managers for those roles respond more to things like: troubleshooting, ticketing systems, hardware support, networking, endpoints — not AI strategy language.

If you're referencing your local AI helpdesk setup (Ollama, Oobabooga, etc.), I'd frame it as a specific *project* with technical details rather than a repeated buzzword. That's way more credible and interesting to a technical recruiter.

Want me to take a look at specific lines and suggest tighter wording?
