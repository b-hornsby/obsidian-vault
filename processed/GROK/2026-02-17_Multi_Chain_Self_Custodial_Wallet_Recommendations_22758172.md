No single self-custodial (non-custodial) wallet supports **every** one natively in a seamless way, as some chains (like ICP, NEAR, XRP Ledger) have unique requirements and limited wallet ecosystem overlap. However, several strong **multi-chain self-custodial wallets** cover most or all with good support in 2026. Here are the best practical options:

### Top Recommendations
1. **Trust Wallet** (Mobile-first, highly recommended for broad coverage)
   - Fully self-custodial (you control your seed phrase/private keys).
   - Supports: ETH (and ERC-20 like LINK), SOL, XRP (including XRPL tokens), NEAR, and many others.
   - For ICP: Limited or indirect (may require custom/add-on in some cases, but not native core support).
   - For HYPE/PUMP: If on supported chains (e.g., Hyperliquid or Solana), add via custom token if needed.
   - Great for buying directly in-app (via integrated ramps/partners), swapping, staking, and dApp access.
   - Free, user-friendly, available on iOS/Android + browser extension.
   - Why it fits: Covers the majority of your list (ETH, SOL, XRP, NEAR) and is popular for multi-chain use.

2. **Bitget Wallet** (Formerly BitKeep; strong multi-chain option)
   - Self-custodial with broad support (~160 blockchains in recent reviews).
   - Excellent for cross-chain swaps and managing diverse assets.
   - Likely covers ETH, SOL, XRP, NEAR, and more; good candidate for HYPE (Hyperliquid) and Solana-based PUMP.
   - Mobile + extension; includes in-app features for buying/swapping.

3. **Exodus Wallet** (Desktop + Mobile)
   - Self-custodial, beautiful interface, built-in swaps/staking.
   - Supports: ETH (LINK), SOL, NEAR (in many cases), and custom tokens on various networks.
   - XRP: Supported on some platforms (mobile/desktop).
   - ICP: Custom token support available on certain networks.
   - Doesn't cover every niche chain perfectly, but handles most of your list well.

4. **Hardware + Software Combo for Max Security** (e.g., Ledger or Tangem with app)
   - Ledger (with Ledger Live or integrated apps like Exodus/Phantom): Supports ETH, SOL, XRP, LINK, and more. NEAR/ICP may have partial or app-specific support.
   - Tangem (card-based cold wallet): Broad multi-chain (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, etc.), self-custodial, very secure for long-term holding.
   - Pair with software wallets (e.g., Phantom for SOL/PUMP, MetaMask for ETH/LINK) for full coverage.

### Quick Tips
- **For buying**: Most of these (Trust, Exodus, Bitget) have in-app fiat on-ramps (credit card, bank transfer) or DEX swaps to purchase directly into the wallet. Avoid keeping large amounts on exchanges—transfer to your self-custodial wallet ASAP.
- **Challenges**: ICP and NEAR often need their official wallets (e.g., NNS for ICP, NEAR Wallet) for full features like governance/staking. HYPE (Hyperliquid) may require its native interface or compatible wallets. PUMP (Solana memecoin-related) works best in Solana wallets like Phantom or Trust.
- **Best starting point**: Download **Trust Wallet**—it hits ETH, SOL, XRP, NEAR solidly and is easy for beginners. Add custom tokens/networks as needed for the rest.
- Always back up your seed phrase securely (offline), enable biometrics/passcodes, and double-check addresses when transferring.
