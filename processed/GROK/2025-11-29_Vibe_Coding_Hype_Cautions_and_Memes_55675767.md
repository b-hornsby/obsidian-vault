### What is Vibe Coding?
Vibe coding is an AI-assisted software development approach where you describe your app or feature in natural language (like "build a cozy rustic website with a login form"), and an AI tool (e.g., Gemini, Claude, or Replit's Agent) generates, refines, and debugs the code for you. Coined by AI researcher Andrej Karpathy in early 2025, it's exploded in popularity for rapid prototyping, especially among non-coders, indie hackers, and even big tech teams. It's not about replacing traditional coding but shifting focus from syntax to ideas—think "pair programming with a tireless robot."
