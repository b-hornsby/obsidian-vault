# NEAR Protocol: Blockchain for AI Agents

### HUMAN
What is NEAR crypto

### ASSISTANT
**NEAR** (often called NEAR crypto) refers to the native cryptocurrency and token of the **NEAR Protocol**, a high-performance Layer 1 blockchain platform.

NEAR Protocol is a scalable, smart contract-enabled blockchain designed for building decentralized applications (dApps). It uses a Proof-of-Stake (PoS) consensus mechanism and a unique sharding technology called **Nightshade** to achieve high throughput, low fees, and fast transactions — positioning it as a more developer- and user-friendly alternative to platforms like Ethereum.

Originally launched around 2020 by founders Illia Polosukhin (ex-Google) and Alexander Skidanov, it started as a community-run cloud computing platform for dApps, with a strong focus on usability (e.g., human-readable account names instead of complex addresses).

As of 2026, NEAR Protocol has heavily pivoted and branded itself as **"the blockchain for AI"**. It's built to power AI-native applications, autonomous agents, and the "agentic future":

- **User-Owned AI** — Ensuring AI agents act in users' best interests with ownership and control.
- **Intents & Chain Abstraction** — Allowing seamless, goal-based transactions across multiple chains without users dealing with bridges or gas complexities.
- **AI infrastructure** — Supporting encrypted model execution, verifiable compute, multichain actions, and agents that can own assets, transact, coordinate, and evolve on-chain.

The **NEAR token** itself is used for:
- Paying transaction fees
- Staking to secure the network and earn rewards
- Governance
- Storage and other network operations

As of mid-February 2026, the live price of NEAR is hovering around **$1.04–$1.06 USD**, with a market cap of approximately **$1.34–$1.36 billion** (ranking it around #48–#56 among cryptocurrencies). It has a circulating supply of roughly 1.28–1.29 billion tokens.

Official website: near.org

It's traded on major exchanges like Binance, KuCoin, Coinbase, Kraken, etc. If you're looking into it for investment, development, or AI/blockchain use cases, it's currently emphasizing the intersection of AI agents and Web3 rather than just traditional DeFi or general-purpose dApps.

### HUMAN
What is it in simple terms? Why is it important?

### ASSISTANT
**NEAR** (in simple terms) is a super-fast, user-friendly blockchain — basically a digital network that lets people build and run apps without big companies controlling everything.

Think of it like this:
- Regular blockchains (like Ethereum) can be slow and expensive, like a crowded highway.
- NEAR uses smart tech (called **sharding**) to split the work across many "lanes," so it handles tons of actions quickly and cheaply — often in under a second, with very low fees.

It started as a place for developers to easily create decentralized apps (like games, finance tools, or social stuff), with nice features like easy-to-remember usernames instead of long codes.

**Right now in 2026**, NEAR has shifted big-time to become **"the blockchain for AI"**.

**In super simple terms**: It's building a system where AI "agents" (smart programs that act on their own) can:
- Own money or stuff (like crypto or digital items).
- Make decisions and do tasks for you.
- Buy, sell, or move things across different blockchains automatically.
- Do all this privately and securely, without big tech companies spying or controlling your data/AI.

You tell the AI what you want (your "intent," like "book me the cheapest flight"), and it handles the complicated blockchain parts behind the scenes — no need to mess with wallets, gas fees, or switching chains yourself.

**Why is it important?**
- AI is exploding everywhere, but today most powerful AI is locked inside companies like Google or OpenAI — they own your data, control the models, and could misuse it.
- NEAR wants to flip that: Make AI **user-owned** — private, transparent, and actually working for **you** (not advertisers or corporations).
- It could power the next big wave: AI agents that live on the blockchain, coordinate with each other, handle real money/tasks, and create an open "agent economy" where value goes back to people.
- By solving hard problems like privacy for AI models, verifiable actions, and seamless multi-chain use, NEAR positions itself as key infrastructure for the "agentic future" — where AI does a lot of work autonomously and safely in Web3.

In short: NEAR isn't just another crypto — it's trying to make sure the future of AI + internet stays open, private, and in **your** control, not trapped in closed big-tech silos. That's why it's getting a lot of attention in 2026 as AI and blockchain merge.
