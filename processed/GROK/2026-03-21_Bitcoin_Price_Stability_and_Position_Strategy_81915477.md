Here's how it works based on Jupiter's mechanics (confirmed in their support articles on Collateral Management, Leverage Management, and position combining):

### To Increase Effective Leverage on Your Existing Position
- The key is **adding collateral while opening "more" of the same position** (long WBTC in your case). Jupiter combines multiple entries in the same market/direction into **one single position**.
- When you add more collateral to increase size:
  - The new portion uses the **current leverage you select** (via slider when adding).
  - The overall position's leverage becomes a **weighted average** of the old and new parts.
  - Example: If your current ~$21 collateral is at 10x, and you add new collateral with higher leverage selected (e.g., set slider to 40x for the addition), the blended leverage rises toward 40x (depending on how much you add relative to existing).
- This effectively "ramps up" leverage without closing/reopening fully.
- To add:
  1. In the mobile app, go to your open position (tap on the WBTC long card).
  2. Look for options like **"Add to Position"**, **"Increase Size"**, or **"Deposit Collateral"** (it might be under the position details, near "Close" or the TP/SL section—often a "+" or "Add" button/icon).
  3. Select/add more collateral amount (e.g., more USDC or WBTC equivalent).
  4. When prompted, adjust the **Leverage Slider** for this addition (it should appear similar to opening a new trade—slide to 40x or your target).
  5. Confirm the tx— it combines automatically, updating your overall leverage, size, entry (averaged), liq price (moves closer), etc.
- Adding collateral directly (without increasing size) **decreases** leverage (widens liq buffer), which is the opposite of what you want—so make sure you're adding to **increase the position size**.

### To Add More Money/Collateral Generally

- Then use it in the "add to position" step above.
