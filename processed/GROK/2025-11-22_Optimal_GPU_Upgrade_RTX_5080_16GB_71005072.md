# Optimal GPU Upgrade: RTX 5080 16GB

### HUMAN
Given my pc specs give me a gpu that I should upgrade to that doesn’t bottleneck the rest of my system, allows me to play 1440p gaming comfortably when I want to, and allows me to run bigger models than my current 3070 8gb. Give me the optimal choice. These are those specs: Cpu: Intel Core i7-12700K
Motherboard: Asus Prime Z790-P
Cpu cooler: Noctua NH-U12S
Memory: Corsair Vengeance Ddr5 ram 32GB (2x16GB)
4800MHz CL40
Storage: 970 Evo Plus M.2 SSD 2TB
Graphics card: PNY GeForce RTX™ 3070 8GB XLR8 Gaming
REVEL EPIC-X RGB
Case: Corsair 4000D Airflow
PSU: Corsair CX750M
OS: Windows 11

### ASSISTANT
### Optimal GPU Upgrade Recommendation: **RTX 5080 16GB**

Your current RTX 3070 8GB is heavily limited by its VRAM for both modern 1440p gaming (stutters in VRAM-hungry titles like Alan Wake 2, Cyberpunk with path tracing, or upcoming 2025+ games) and local AI/LLM work (can't comfortably fit >13B-30B models at reasonable quantization/context without offloading to slow system RAM).

Your i7-12700K (strong 12-core/20-thread CPU) + 32GB DDR5 + fast SSD is an excellent foundation and will **not meaningfully bottleneck** a high-end GPU at 1440p in the vast majority of games — it's still a top-tier CPU in late 2025. Any minor CPU limitation in a few esports/CPU-heavy titles is far outweighed by the GPU gains you'll see.

- **Gaming**: The 5080 crushes 1440p — expect 144+ FPS ultra in demanding 2025 titles with full RT/DLSS 4/MFG. It's essentially a "4090-level" card at 1440p but with newer architecture (Blackwell) for better efficiency and features.
- **AI/LLMs**: 16GB GDDR7 is double your current VRAM and lets you run significantly larger/faster models (e.g., Llama 3.1/4 70B Q4, Mistral Large, or 34B+ at Q6/Q8) with long contexts. The 5080's extra CUDA/Tensor cores and higher memory bandwidth give noticeably faster token generation than the 5070 Ti for the same VRAM.
- **Your PSU**: Real-world tests show 12700K + 5080 systems peak around 650-720W total. Your Corsair CX750M (semi-modular 80+ Bronze) can handle it reliably if you use a proper 12V-2x6 cable (native or quality adapter). Avoid heavy overclocking on both CPU/GPU simultaneously.
- **Why not 5070 Ti?** Same VRAM but slower inference and slightly lower gaming headroom — you specifically want "bigger models" comfortably/fast.
- **Why not 5090?** 32GB is fantastic for massive 100B+ models or multi-model workflows, but it's extreme overkill, draws too much power for your PSU, and costs nearly double with minimal extra benefit at 1440p gaming.

If budget is tighter and you're okay with slightly slower inference, the RTX 5070 Ti 16GB is still a huge upgrade and 100% safe on your PSU. But the **RTX 5080** hits the absolute best balance of performance, future-proofing, and compatibility with your existing system.

You'll also need the new 12V-2x6 (12VHPWR) power cable — most 5080 AIB models include an adapter, but grab a native CableMod or Seasonic one for peace of mind.

Enjoy the upgrade — your rig will feel brand new!

### HUMAN
What about on the budget side of things?

If the RTX 5080 (~$950–1100) stretches your budget too far, the **RTX 5070 Ti 16GB** is the clear **optimal budget choice** that still fully meets (and exceeds) everything you asked for:
- Comfortable 1440p gaming (ultra settings, RT + DLSS 4 → 100–180+ FPS in 2025 titles)
- Runs **much bigger models** than your 3070 8GB (70B Q4/Q5 or 34B Q8 comfortably, long contexts)
- No bottleneck from your i7-12700K at 1440p
- Safe on your 750W PSU (TGP ~300W, total system ~550–650W peak)

Black Friday 2025 deals have pushed it to all-time lows — you can grab one for **$699–730** right now (PNY/M SI models at Walmart/Newegg/Amazon), sometimes even below the $749 MSRP.

#### Why the RTX 5070 Ti 16GB is the sweet spot on budget
- **Gaming**: Roughly matches or beats a 4080 Super at 1440p — your 3070 feels ancient in comparison, especially with path tracing or heavy RT.
- **Local AI/LLMs**: 16GB + newer Tensor cores = you can finally run modern 70B-class models at usable quantization/speed without constant off-loading hassles. Token rates are 2–3× your 3070.
- **Deals right now**: Black Friday pricing is the lowest we’ve seen all year — grab one quick before they bounce back up.
- **Your system**: Zero bottlenecks, works perfectly with your 750W Corsair (use a good 12V-2x6 cable/adapter).

If your absolute max budget is ~$550–600 and you’re okay with slightly slower inference on the very largest models, the RTX 5070 12GB at $479–529 is still a massive upgrade and totally fine for 1440p. But for only ~$150–200 more, the 5070 Ti gives you the full 16GB + noticeable speed headroom that will age far better for both gaming and AI.

Snag a 5070 Ti while the $699 deals last — your rig will thank you!
