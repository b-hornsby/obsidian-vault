# Axiom vs Hyperliquid: Which is Better?

### HUMAN
Which is better? Axiom or hyperliquid?

### ASSISTANT
**Axiom** and **Hyperliquid** are both prominent players in the decentralized trading space (especially perpetual futures/perps), but they're not exactly direct apples-to-apples competitors.

- **Hyperliquid** is a decentralized Layer-1 blockchain and exchange built specifically for high-performance on-chain trading. It features fully on-chain order books, perpetual futures (with up to 40x leverage), spot trading, very low fees, deep liquidity, and a battle-tested system that handles massive volume (often billions daily) with tight spreads and reliable execution even in volatile markets. It's widely regarded as one of the top on-chain perps venues, with strong self-custody, no KYC for most users, and revenue flowing back to users via mechanisms like HLP vaults.

- **Axiom** (axiom.trade) is a Solana-based all-in-one trading terminal/interface (not a standalone blockchain or DEX). It focuses on memecoin sniping, spot trading on Solana, wallet/Twitter monitoring, yield opportunities, and—importantly—integrated perpetuals trading powered by **Hyperliquid** itself (you trade Hyperliquid perps directly through Axiom's UI, often with up to 50x leverage mentioned in reviews).

In short: Axiom uses Hyperliquid as its backend for perps. Many users access Hyperliquid via frontends like Axiom, Phantom Perps, pvp.trade, or others because these offer nicer UIs, extra tools (e.g., real-time whale tracking, social sentiment, faster Solana memecoin flows), points/rebates, or bundled features.

### Which is "better"?
It depends on your needs:

- **If you want pure perps trading (deepest liquidity, most markets, best execution for leveraged trades)** → **Hyperliquid** is generally superior as the core protocol. It dominates on-chain perps volume and open interest, has proven resilience (e.g., during volatility), and is the venue many pros use directly or via integrations. It's often called the benchmark for decentralized perps.

- **If you want an all-in-one Solana-focused experience** (memecoin sniping + perps + yield + analytics in one slick web interface) → **Axiom** often wins for convenience. It bundles Hyperliquid perps with Solana-native tools, has lower overall friction for multi-tasking traders, and has exploded in popularity (sometimes surpassing Hyperliquid in daily revenue during memecoin surges, e.g., hitting top-3 DeFi revenue spots). Many traders prefer Axiom's UI and extras over the native Hyperliquid app.
