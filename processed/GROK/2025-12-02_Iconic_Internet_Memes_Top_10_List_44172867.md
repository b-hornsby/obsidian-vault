# Iconic Internet Memes: Top 10 List

### HUMAN
what are the top 10 dankest memes of all time. working on a project and i need specific, recognizable memes/characters for this project

### ASSISTANT
Here are the **top 10 most iconic, recognizable, and perpetually "dank" memes/characters of all time** (in roughly chronological order of peak virality). These are the ones that have transcended generations, still get referenced daily in 2025, and are instantly understood by anyone who’s ever touched the internet.

1. **Rickroll (Rick Astley – Never Gonna Give You Up)**  
   The undisputed king of internet bait-and-switch. Still the ultimate troll move in 2025.

2. **Trollface / Problem? (Rage Comics)**  
   The original rage comic face from 2008–2012 era. Pure concentrated chaos energy. Instantly screams “trolling.”

3. **Pepe the Frog (Feels Good Man / Sad Pepe / Smug Pepe)**  
   The most versatile and controversial meme character ever. Every emotion has a Pepe variant. Still everywhere despite everything.

4. **Doge (Shiba Inu with broken English captions)**  
   “Wow. Such meme. Very historic.” One of the few memes that became a cryptocurrency and an official dog breed nickname.

5. **SpongeBob Mocking / Alternating Caps Text**  
   The “mOcKiNg SpOnGeBoB” text and the associated SpongeBob sarcasm images. Used in literally every comment section ever.

6. **Distracted Boyfriend (Stock photo couple + girl walking by)**  
   The ultimate template for “person ignoring obvious good choice for shiny new thing.” Still the most relatable stock-photo meme.

7. **This is Fine (KC Green’s dog in burning room)**  
   The universal symbol of existential dread and denial. Used for everything from personal life to global collapse.

8. **Drake Hotline Bling (Drake rejecting / approving)**  
   The cleanest two-panel “dislike / like” template in history. Still dominates reaction images.

9. **Woman Yelling at a Cat (Taylor Armstrong + Smudge the Cat)**  
   Perfect absurd two-panel format. The cat’s dead-eyed stare is pure meme perfection.

10. **Chad / GigaChad (Ernest Khalimov / Yes Chad / Virgin vs Chad)**  
    The ultimate alpha male archetype. “Average Fan vs. Average Enjoyer” and “Gigachad approving” templates are still everywhere in 2025.

### Honorable Mentions (extremely close to top 10):

- Expanding Brain

- Bernie Sanders Mittens (inauguration photo)

These 10 (plus the honorable mentions) are the safest bets for maximum instant recognition in any project—whether it’s a game, video, shirt design, or whatever you’re cooking. They’re the meme equivalents of Mickey Mouse at this point.

### HUMAN
specifically what were the top 10 dankest memes around the dank meme era? think the dank meme era and how it gained mainstream prominence around 2014

### ASSISTANT
Here are the **absolute peak dank memes of the 2014–2016 "Dank Memes" golden era** – the exact stuff that flooded r/dankmemes, iFunny, 9GAG, and Instagram meme pages when the word “dank” became the universal seal of quality. This is the era when “dank” went fully mainstream.

### Top 10 Dankest Memes of the 2014–2016 Era (in rough order of dominance)

1. **MLG / Montage Parody Memes** (2014–2015 peak)  
   Airhorns, illuminati triangles, “360 noscope,” “Get rekt,” Hitmarkers, Doritos + Mountain Dew, Sanic, “Wow, such skill,” “Dank Kush 420.” The entire aesthetic.

2. **Doge** (late 2013 → 2014–2015 explosion)  
   This was literally the face of the entire era. “Much dank. Very meme. Wow.”

3. **Pepe the Frog (Rare Pepes, 2014–2016)**  
   The birth of “Rare Pepe” trading, Pepe photoshopped into everything, “Feels Bad Man / Feels Good Man,” Smug Pepe. This was the #1 currency of dankness.

4. **SpongeBob Mocking / “Mocking SpongeBob” text** (2014–2016)  
   The alternating caps text (e.g., “mY nAmE jEfF”) started here, plus the chicken-wing SpongeBob image mocking everything.

5. **John Cena “Are you sure about that?”** (2015)  
   Random trumpet blast + sudden Cena face. Still the ultimate surprise prank meme of the era.

6. **Dat Boi (Unicycle frog)** (2016)  
   “Here come dat boi!!!!” “O shit waddup!” One of the purest, most absurd dank memes ever. Peak 2016 surrealism.

7. **Arthur Fist / Dwayne “The Rock” Johnson smelling** (2015–2016)  
   Arthur’s clenched fist when you’re mad but holding it in, and The Rock sniffing the air for bullshit.

8. **Loss.jpg (minimalist version)** (2014–2016)  
   The Ctrl+Alt+Del comic stripped down to four panels. The ultimate “Is this Loss?” meta-meme. Extremely niche but insanely dank to those in the know.

9. **Harambe** (May 2016 – end of 2016)  
   “Dicks out for Harambe.” Took over the entire internet in summer 2016. The last truly unifying dank meme before everything got political.

10. **Savage Patrick / Evil Patrick** (2015–2016)  
    Patrick with red eyes saying the most sociopathic shit imaginable. The original “savage” meme template.
