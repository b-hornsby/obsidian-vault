# Where to Buy ICP Officially

### HUMAN
Where can I buy ICP officially besides ledger live?

### ASSISTANT
The **Internet Computer (ICP)** token, developed by the **DFINITY Foundation**, doesn't have a single "official" direct sales point from DFINITY itself for general purchases (beyond specific mechanisms like the NNS dapp). Instead, it's widely available on reputable centralized cryptocurrency exchanges and through integrated tools.

Besides **Ledger Live** (which supports buying ICP via third-party providers), here are some reliable and commonly used options to buy ICP:

### Official/Integrated Option via the Network
- **NNS dapp** (nns.ic0.app): This is the official Network Nervous System frontend from DFINITY. You can log in with Internet Identity, go to Tokens > Internet Computer > Buy ICP. It redirects to a third-party provider (like Banxa) for credit card purchases directly into your NNS wallet/account. This is one of the most "official" ways tied to the Internet Computer ecosystem itself.

### Popular Centralized Exchanges
ICP is listed on many major, well-established exchanges where you can buy it with fiat (e.g., USD via bank transfer, credit/debit card) or by trading other cryptos:

- **Kraken** — Often recommended for security and ease; supports fiat deposits and direct ICP buys.
- **Coinbase** — Available in the US (including Ohio); user-friendly for beginners with direct fiat purchases.
- **Crypto.com** — Supports buying ICP in the app with various payment methods (bank transfer, card, etc.).
- **OKX** — Good liquidity and options for ICP trading.
- **Binance** (where available; note: not in all regions like the US) — High volume.
- Others frequently mentioned: KuCoin, Bybit, Gate.io, etc.

These are not "official" in the sense of being run by DFINITY, but they are legitimate platforms where ICP is officially listed and traded.

### Tips
- Always use official exchange websites/apps to avoid scams (e.g., check kraken.com, coinbase.com).
- For security, after buying, consider transferring to a personal wallet like the NNS dapp, Oisy, Plug, or hardware like Ledger.
- Availability and payment methods can vary by location (e.g., US regulations), so check the platform for your region.
- Prices fluctuate; as of recent data, ICP trades around $2–$3 range, but verify live prices.

If you're looking for the most decentralized/on-chain approach, start with the NNS dapp. For fiat on-ramps, exchanges like Kraken or Coinbase are solid choices. Let me know if you need steps for a specific one!
