# Self-Hosted AI Assistant: Clawdbot Overview

### HUMAN
What is clawdbot?

### ASSISTANT
**Clawdbot** (often stylized as ClawdBot or @clawdbot) is an **open-source, self-hosted personal AI assistant** — basically a powerful, customizable AI agent that runs on your own hardware (like a Mac Mini, Linux machine, old laptop, or cloud server) and acts like a 24/7 digital helper/employee.

It's designed to go far beyond just chatting: it can actually *do things* in your digital life, such as:

- Clear your inbox and send emails
- Manage your calendar (schedule, resolve conflicts, time-block tasks)
- Handle iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, Signal, etc. (lives in the messaging apps you already use)
- Browse the web, run shell commands, manage files on your computer
- Read/understand videos, voice messages, PDFs
- Integrate with tools like Gmail, Google Calendar/Drive, 1Password, home automation, Notion, Obsidian, etc.
- Remember everything long-term (persistent memory, no forgetting like typical chatbots)
- Accept custom "skills" (tools you write or download) to automate almost anything
- Run proactively (send you reminders, summaries, daily briefs, research when you say "idea:", etc.)

Key points that make it stand out in 2026:

- **Self-hosted & private** — Everything stays on your machine/server; no data goes to a company cloud unless you choose a hosted LLM (it usually uses Claude models from Anthropic via API, but supports others)
- **Local-first agent** — Gives the AI real access to your system (with sandboxing/security options to keep it safe)
- **"The lobster way"** — Playful branding (🦞), created by Peter Steinberger (@steipete) who came out of semi-retirement to build it
- **Very active development** — Frequent updates (e.g. video understanding, cross-channel memory, new integrations)
- **Popular setup** — Lots of people buy cheap Mac Minis just to run it 24/7; there are easy install guides via npm + a setup wizard

Official site: https://clawd.bot/  
GitHub: https://github.com/clawdbot/clawdbot  
Docs: https://docs.clawd.bot/

It's currently one of the most talked-about "AI that actually does stuff" projects in the agent/self-hosted AI community — people describe it as "the personal AI Siri/ assistant we were promised years ago, but actually delivered (and open-source)". If you're into local LLMs, agents, or automating your personal life, it's worth checking out. 🦞
