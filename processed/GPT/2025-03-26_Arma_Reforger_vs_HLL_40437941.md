# Arma Reforger vs HLL

### USER
Between arma reforged and hell let loose which is more worth it to buy?

### ASSISTANT
It depends on what you're looking for in a tactical shooter. Here’s a breakdown:  

### **Arma Reforger**  
✅ **Pros:**  
- More of a sandbox military simulator with modding support.  
- Built on the new Enfusion engine, setting the stage for *Arma 4*.  
- More flexibility in mission design and community-created content.  

❌ **Cons:**  
- Feels like an early-access tech demo rather than a full game.  
- Lacks the depth of *Arma 3* in content and features.  
- Smaller player base compared to *Hell Let Loose*.  

### **Hell Let Loose**  
✅ **Pros:**  
- More structured, immersive WWII large-scale battles.  
- Intense teamwork-oriented gameplay with voice comms and roles.  
- Active community with consistent updates.  

❌ **Cons:**  
- No single-player or AI skirmish options.  
- More linear gameplay compared to *Arma Reforger’s* sandbox nature.  
- Learning curve can be frustrating if your squad lacks coordination.  

### **Which One Is Worth It?**  
- If you want **a mil-sim sandbox experience with modding**, go for *Arma Reforger*—but be aware that it's still a work in progress.  
- If you prefer **a polished, teamwork-heavy WWII shooter with intense battles**, *Hell Let Loose* is the better choice right now.  

If you're new to tactical shooters, *Hell Let Loose* is probably the safer bet for an engaging experience. If you're more into modding and sandbox warfare, *Arma Reforger* might be worth it for its potential (but it's not fully fleshed out yet).
