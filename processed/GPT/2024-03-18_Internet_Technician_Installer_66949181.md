### ASSISTANT
The person who installs internet services is typically referred to as an "internet technician" or an "internet installer." These professionals are responsible for setting up and configuring internet connections in homes, businesses, and other locations. They may work for internet service providers (ISPs) or be employed by independent contracting companies.

There are several other jobs similar to that of an internet technician or installer, which involve installing, maintaining, or troubleshooting various types of technology or infrastructure. Here are a few examples:

1. **Cable Technician**: These professionals install and maintain cable television, internet, and phone services for residential or commercial customers. They may work for cable companies or contracting firms.

2. **Telecommunications Technician**: Telecommunications technicians install, maintain, and repair equipment used for telecommunications services, including landline phones, cellular networks, and internet infrastructure.

3. **Network Technician**: Network technicians are responsible for installing, configuring, and maintaining computer networks, including local area networks (LANs) and wide area networks (WANs). They troubleshoot network issues and ensure that data can be transmitted efficiently and securely.

4. **Satellite Technician**: Satellite technicians install and align satellite dishes for television, internet, or other communication services. They may also troubleshoot and repair satellite equipment.

5. **Fiber Optic Technician**: Fiber optic technicians install, splice, and test fiber optic cables used for high-speed internet and telecommunications services. They ensure that fiber optic networks operate effectively and meet performance standards.

6. **Field Service Technician**: Field service technicians travel to customer locations to install, maintain, or repair various types of equipment or systems, including technology-related services like internet, telecommunications, or computer hardware.

7. **IT Support Technician**: IT support technicians provide technical assistance and support to computer users within an organization. They may help with setting up internet connections, troubleshooting network issues, and resolving other IT-related problems.

These are just a few examples, and there are many other roles within the technology and telecommunications industries that involve installing, maintaining, or supporting various types of technology infrastructure.
