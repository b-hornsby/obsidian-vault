The difference between **QHD (Quad High Definition)** and **UHD (Ultra High Definition)** comes down to resolution:  

- **QHD (2560 × 1440)** – Also known as **1440p**, this resolution is four times the pixels of **720p HD**. It’s commonly used in high-end monitors, gaming laptops, and smartphones.  
- **UHD (3840 × 2160)** – Also known as **4K UHD**, this resolution is four times the pixels of **1080p Full HD**, providing much sharper details, often used in TVs, professional monitors, and gaming setups.  

### Key Differences:  
- **Pixel Count**: UHD has significantly more pixels (8.3 million) than QHD (3.7 million).  
- **Sharpness**: UHD is sharper, especially noticeable on larger screens.  
- **Performance**: QHD is less demanding on GPUs, making it better for gaming at higher frame rates.  
- **Common Use Cases**: QHD is popular for gaming and productivity monitors, while UHD is common in TVs, content creation, and high-end gaming.  

If you're gaming or need high refresh rates, QHD is often a better choice unless you have a powerful GPU. For media consumption and high-detail work, UHD is preferable.
