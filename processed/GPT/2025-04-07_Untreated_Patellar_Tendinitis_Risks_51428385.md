If patellar tendinitis (also known as jumper's knee) is left untreated, it can lead to several complications:

1. **Worsening of Pain**: The pain may become more persistent and intense, particularly during physical activities that involve jumping or running.

2. **Chronic Tendon Damage**: Over time, the tendon may undergo degenerative changes, leading to a condition called tendinosis, where the tendon weakens and loses its ability to properly function.

3. **Reduced Mobility**: Untreated tendinitis can limit your range of motion and affect your ability to move freely, especially during sports or physical activities.

4. **Risk of Tendon Rupture**: In severe cases, the tendon may weaken to the point of tearing, which can require surgical intervention and a lengthy recovery process.

5. **Longer Recovery Time**: If left untreated, the healing process can take much longer, and the need for more aggressive treatments, like physical therapy or surgery, might increase.

It's important to address patellar tendinitis early on with rest, ice, and proper rehabilitation to prevent long-term issues.
