Alright, let’s walk through exactly **how you’d options trade NVIDIA (NVDA)** on Fidelity—step-by-step. You can do this even with beginner-level options approval (Level 1 or 2).

## **Step-by-Step: Buying a Call on NVDA in Fidelity**

### **1. Make Sure You’re Approved**
- If you haven’t yet, apply for **Options Trading Approval** (you need at least Level 2 to buy calls/puts).
  - Log in > Accounts & Trade > Options > Apply

### **3. Browse the Options Chain**
You’ll now see a big table (the **options chain**) with:

- **Expiration dates** (choose 1–4 weeks out if you want short-term)
- **Strike prices** (where you think NVDA will go)
- **Calls** on the left, **Puts** on the right

**Example Trade Setup:**
- **Call Option**
- **Strike Price:** $950
- **Expiration:** 3 weeks out
- **Premium (cost):** ~$15.00 (x 100 = $1,500 total)

> You’re saying: *“I believe NVDA will go above $950 within 3 weeks.”*

### **5. Monitor & Manage Your Position**
- Use **Fidelity’s options tools** or **Active Trader Pro** to:
  - Track the option’s price
  - Set alerts
  - Decide when to sell or exercise the option

If NVDA rises above your strike + premium paid, your call gains value.

### **Alternative Option: Sell a Covered Call on NVDA**
If you already own at least **100 shares of NVDA**, you can sell a call against it and **get paid premium income** (lower risk strategy).
