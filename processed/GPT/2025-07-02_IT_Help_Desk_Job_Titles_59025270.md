### 🎯 Pro Tips for Searching:
- Add "entry-level," "junior," or "Tier 1" to your search to filter out senior roles.
  - Example: `entry level IT support`, `Tier 1 help desk`, `junior desktop support`
- Use filters like "0–1 years experience" or "training provided" on job boards like:
  - **Indeed**
  - **LinkedIn Jobs**
  - **ZipRecruiter**
  - **Dice**
  - **Jobcase**
- Look at staffing agencies too (e.g. TEKsystems, Robert Half, Insight Global) — they often have short-term or contract IT support roles perfect for beginners.
