## 💶 2. EU’s MiCA Regulation (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation)

- A comprehensive EU framework effective as of **Dec 30, 2024**. 
  - Regulates **asset‑referenced tokens**, stablecoins, and other crypto-assets.
  - Applies to issuers and service providers (exchanges, wallets, custodians) across all EU countries citeturn0search24.

## 🧭 4. SEC and Legislative Guidance

- **SEC commentary** and legal analyses by law firms (Lexology, Fenwick, Braumiller) offer frameworks:
  - RWA tokens are subject to securities or commodities laws depending on structure and rights.
  - Compliance must consider the Howey Test, registration routes, custody rules, and fractionalization citeturn0search0turn0search13turn0search6.
- **Fed-level insight**: The FDIC commented on how tokenization interacts with deposit insurance and governance structures citeturn0search10.
