# Explain market cap crypto

### USER
What is the simplest way to explain market cap in terms of crypto?

### ASSISTANT
The simplest way to explain **market cap** in crypto is:  

> **Market cap = Price of one coin × Total number of coins in circulation.**  

It tells you the *total value of that cryptocurrency* if you added up every single coin at the current price.  

For example:  
- If 1 coin costs $10 and there are 1 million coins, the market cap = $10 × 1,000,000 = **$10 million**.  

Think of it like the *“size”* of the cryptocurrency compared to others. Bigger market cap usually means a more established coin, smaller market cap usually means a riskier or newer coin.
