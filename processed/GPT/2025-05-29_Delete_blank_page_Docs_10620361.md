### ASSISTANT
Deleting a blank page in Google Docs is usually straightforward. Here are a few ways to do it:

### Method 1: Use the Backspace or Delete key
1. Place your cursor at the end of the content on the page **before** the blank page.
2. Press **Delete** (if your cursor is at the end of the previous page) or **Backspace** (if your cursor is at the start of the blank page).
3. Keep pressing until the blank page disappears.

### Method 2: Check for extra line breaks or page breaks
Sometimes blank pages happen because of extra empty paragraphs or manual page breaks.

- Click at the top of the blank page and press **Backspace** until it’s gone.
- Or, if there’s a page break:
  1. Show **Show/Hide** formatting marks by going to **View > Show > Show non-printing characters** (if available).
  2. Delete the page break line.

### Method 3: Adjust margins or content
If the blank page is caused by margins or large empty space:

- Go to **File > Page Setup** and check your margins.
- Reduce the bottom margin if it pushes content to a new page.
