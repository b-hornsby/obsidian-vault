# What is Market Cap

### ASSISTANT
**Market cap** (short for **market capitalization**) is the total value of a cryptocurrency or company's circulating supply.

### Simple Formula:
**Market Cap = Price × Circulating Supply**

### Example:
If a coin is worth **$10** and there are **1 million coins** in circulation:
- **Market Cap = $10 × 1,000,000 = $10 million**

It shows how "big" or "valuable" the asset is in the market.
