# Best Apps for Jailbroken Fire Stick

### ASSISTANT
If you've jailbroken your Fire Stick, you can install third-party apps to expand its functionality. Here are some of the best apps right now:

### **Streaming & Media Apps**  
1. **Kodi** – The go-to app for streaming everything, including live TV, movies, and shows via add-ons.  
2. **Cinema HD** – One of the best apps for watching movies and TV shows with high-quality streams.  
3. **BeeTV** – Another solid alternative for on-demand movies and TV shows.  
4. **CyberFlix TV** – A clone of the old Terrarium TV with a large library of content.  
5. **TeaTV** – Offers a great selection of movies and TV shows with smooth playback.  
6. **Stremio** – A good alternative to Kodi that uses add-ons to stream content from various sources.  
7. **Plex** – Great for streaming your own local media library.  

### **Live TV & Sports**  
8. **Live NetTV** – Provides hundreds of live TV channels, including sports, news, and entertainment.  
9. **TVTap Pro** – Streams live TV channels from different countries, including the US and UK.  
10. **HD Streamz** – Offers live TV and radio from various international sources.  
11. **Swift Streamz** – Another live TV app with a variety of global channels.  

### **Utility & Performance**  
12. **Downloader** – A must-have app for sideloading APKs onto your Fire Stick.  
13. **FileLinked (or its alternatives like Applinked or Unlinked)** – Used for bulk downloading APKs from custom stores.  
14. **Mouse Toggle** – Helps navigate apps that aren’t optimized for Fire Stick’s remote.  
15. **Aptoide TV** – An alternative app store with apps that aren’t on the Amazon App Store.  
16. **VPN (ExpressVPN, NordVPN, Surfshark, etc.)** – Essential for privacy, security, and accessing geo-blocked content.  

### **Game Emulation & Retro Gaming**  
17. **RetroArch** – A multi-emulator app for running retro games on Fire Stick.  
18. **PPSSPP** – Play PSP games with a Bluetooth controller.
