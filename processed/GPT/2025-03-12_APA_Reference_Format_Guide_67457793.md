# APA Reference Format Guide

### USER
How do I put my references in APA format?

### ASSISTANT
In **APA (7th edition)** format, references should follow this structure:

### **Basic Format for a Book**  
**Author, A. A.** (Year). *Title of book in sentence case and italics*. Publisher.  
**Example:**  
Smith, J. (2020). *Understanding technology trends*. TechPress.

### **Basic Format for a Journal Article**  
**Author, A. A.** (Year). Title of article in sentence case. *Title of Journal in Title Case and Italics, Volume*(Issue), page range. https://doi.org/xxxxx  
**Example:**  
Brown, L. M. (2021). The rise of artificial intelligence in cybersecurity. *Journal of Tech Innovations, 15*(3), 45–60. https://doi.org/10.xxxx

### **Basic Format for a Website**  
**Author, A. A.** (Year, Month Day). Title of webpage in sentence case. *Website Name*. URL  
**Example:**  
Jones, R. (2022, May 10). How blockchain is changing finance. *Crypto Insights*. https://www.cryptoinisights.com/blockchain-finance

### **Basic Format for a YouTube Video**  
**Uploader Name.** (Year, Month Day). *Title of video in sentence case* [Video]. YouTube. URL  
**Example:**  
Tech Explained. (2023, August 5). *How AI is transforming gaming* [Video]. YouTube. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xxxxxxx
