# RS11 vs Zoap comparison

### USER
Rs11 or zoap when it comes to concentrate?

### ASSISTANT
When it comes to **concentrates**, here's a quick comparison of **RS11** vs **Zoap** to help you decide, depending on your vibe and preferences:

### 🧼 **Zoap**
- **Genetics**: Rainbow Sherb x Pink Guava x OZK (related to RS11 family)
- **Profile**: Soapy, floral, candy-gas with a *unique nose* — very aromatic
- **Effects**: Clear-headed, uplifting with a *weirdly clean* finish
- **In Concentrate Form**: Can hit a little lighter than RS11 but has **super exotic terp appeal**, especially in live resin or rosin
- **Best For**: Daytime dabs, social situations, vibing without crashing
---
### Verdict:
- Want **flavor + heavy effects**? → **RS11**
- Want **exotic terps + functional high**? → **Zoap**

💭 If you’re a terp-hunting dabber who likes **rare profiles**, Zoap is wild.  
But if you’re after a **potent**, reliable **taste + hit combo**, RS11 is hard to beat.
