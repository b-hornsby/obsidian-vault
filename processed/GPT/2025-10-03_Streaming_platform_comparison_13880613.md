## What I’d lean towards *for discoverability*

If I were you, and your priority is growth & getting discovered more than immediate monetization, I’d probably:

1. Keep streaming on TikTok if you're doing that already, because of the high reach + potential for viral exposure.  
2. Add and/or emphasize YouTube (live + VOD + clips). YouTube gives you both live exposure *and* long-term discoverability via its search/recommendation engines.  
3. Only lean into Twitch (or similar) if you see a particular community or audience there that matches your content well, and you’re ready to invest time. Twitch is better for community, audience loyalty, interaction, more “sticky” viewers, but harder to grow from zero sometimes.  
4. Use multi-streaming tools if you have the bandwidth & technical resources, but focus your energy where you can do the best versions of your content. Multi-streaming dilutes some efforts but can help exposure.

If I had to pair **just one** with TikTok for discoverability, I’d go with **YouTube**.  

Here’s why:  

- **TikTok = instant reach** → great for short-form virality and pushing people into your ecosystem fast.  
- **YouTube = evergreen discoverability** → people can find your content weeks, months, even years later through search and recommendations. Unlike Twitch or Kick, your streams and clips don’t vanish once you’re offline.  
- **Perfect synergy** → you can chop TikTok streams or highlights into Shorts (boosting algorithmic reach), while your longer streams sit as VODs on YouTube, building a searchable archive.  
- **Algorithm favors live + VOD crossover** → if someone watches your Shorts, YouTube is more likely to recommend your live streams to them.  

Basically:  
👉 TikTok pulls people in fast.  
👉 YouTube keeps them around long-term.  

If your main goal is **growth and visibility**, TikTok + YouTube is the strongest pairing.  
If your main goal was **community & interactivity**, TikTok + Twitch could work — but it’s slower growth.
