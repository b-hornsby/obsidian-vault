### 🟡 **Where the idea needs refinement:**

#### 1. **You’re selling a *skill*, not a service — and people don’t know what that means**  
Most people don’t know (or care) about “local LLMs” or “agent systems.” They care about:
- **Getting stuff done cheaper/faster**
- **Looking more professional**
- **Making money / saving time**

So don’t sell “AI tools” or “multi-agent projects.” Sell the **end result**:  
> "Need a website or promo flyer for your biz? I use powerful AI tools to build it faster, cheaper, and with pro quality. Let’s talk."

#### 2. **Avoid being too general**  
If your post is like: “I’ll build anything for anyone!” — it dilutes your offer and makes you look desperate, even if your heart’s in the right place. Instead, go for:
> “Looking for 2 small businesses or creators who need help with marketing materials or a landing page. I’m building my portfolio using cutting-edge AI tools — and I’ll do it free or dirt cheap in exchange for feedback and a testimonial.”

That’s specific. Scarcity-driven. And value-first.

#### 3. **Time management risk**  
Be careful not to overcommit. This approach *can* turn into “free labor hell” if you're not clear up front about boundaries, especially with non-technical clients who want the moon.

You’re accidentally positioning yourself as an **AI consultant-in-training**, which is a *very valuable identity*. By helping small businesses now, you’re laying the foundation to eventually:
- Automate workflows
- Offer custom bots/agents
- Set up local LLM infra for clients who want privacy

You’ll look back and thank yourself for starting here.

## 🔍 What **Freepik** Is (and Isn’t):

### ✅ **Freepik is best for:**
- **Premade assets** (flyers, posters, mockups, icons, etc.)
- **Quick turnarounds** without designing from scratch
- **Commercial use** (if you have a Pro license)
- Access to **editable** formats (like PSD, AI, SVG)
- **Real-world design** that looks professional

### ❌ **Freepik is NOT:**
- An AI image generator like Midjourney  
- A tool for making unique, stylized, or narrative art  
- Useful if you're trying to create wild or surreal visuals
