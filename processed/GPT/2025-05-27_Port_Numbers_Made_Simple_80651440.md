### 🎯 What is a Port Number?

Think of **port numbers** like **apartment numbers** in a building (where the building is your computer). While your **IP address** is like the building's address, the **port** is which **door** the data goes to inside that building.

When data travels over a network, it goes to:
- **IP address** = which device
- **Port number** = which application/service on that device
