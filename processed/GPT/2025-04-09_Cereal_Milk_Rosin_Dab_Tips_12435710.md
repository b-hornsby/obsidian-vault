### **Now as Rosin—what changes?**

**Rosin** is solventless, meaning no chemicals are used to extract it—just heat and pressure. So you’re getting:
- **Pure flavor**: Rosin preserves terpenes beautifully, so that cereal-milk taste is gonna hit strong
- **Potent effects**: Expect a more concentrated high than flower—rosin is usually ~70–85% THC
- **Smooth hit**: No solvents = less throat burn than other concentrates
- **Fast onset**: The high comes on quicker and can be more intense upfront

### **Tips for Your First Rosin Experience:**
- **Use a low temp dab (500–600°F)** to taste all those terps and avoid burning them
- **Start small**—like *really small*. A grain-of-rice size is plenty
- **Stay hydrated** and chill somewhere comfy
- **Avoid overdoing it**—rosin hits *harder* and *faster* than flower
