But with options:
- You’re only risking the **premium** you paid (not unlimited loss like shorting)
- You don’t **own** or **borrow** the stock unless you choose to exercise the contract
- You can **trade options themselves** (they have value and can be sold before expiration)

- **Calls = like long**, **Puts = like short**, but with **less upfront risk**
