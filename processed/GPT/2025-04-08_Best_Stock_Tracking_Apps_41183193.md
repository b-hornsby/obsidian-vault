Here are some of the **best apps for tracking stock charts**, depending on your needs:

### **2. Thinkorswim by TD Ameritrade** – *Pro-level tools, Free to Use*
- **Pros:** Powerful desktop + mobile platform, paper trading included.
- **Features:** Advanced charting, tons of studies, real-time data.
- **Free even without a funded account.**
- **Great for:** Serious traders who want deep analysis.

### **4. Yahoo Finance** – *Casual Tracking & Simpler Charts*
- **Pros:** Easy to use, news integration.
- **Features:** Simple line and candlestick charts, alerts, portfolio tracking.
- **Great for:** Long-term investors or casual users.

**If you’re into crypto as well**, you might also like:
- **CoinMarketCap or CoinGecko** (simple charting)
- **DEXTools or Poocoin** for DeFi/token trading charts

### **Fidelity (TradFi platform)**
- **Custodial:** Fidelity holds your assets and your private keys (if applicable).
- **Security:** Very secure on a *centralized level*. Your account is protected by passwords, 2FA, and insured by SIPC (up to certain limits).
- **Control:** You don’t own the private keys—Fidelity controls access to your funds.
- **Risk:** Subject to account freezes, KYC rules, and potential service disruptions or government orders.

### TL;DR:
- **Fidelity = Safer for the average user** (but you don’t truly own your assets).
- **Phantom = True ownership** (but you’re the only line of defense).

### **1. Coinbase Wallet (not Coinbase.com)**
- **Type:** Non-custodial DeFi wallet
- **Assets:** Crypto only (not stocks), but you own the keys
- **Pros:** Easy to use, backed by Coinbase's infrastructure
- **Cons:** Still crypto-only—not a true TradFi stock broker

### **3. **Swan Bitcoin** or **River**
- **Type:** Bitcoin-only platforms
- **Ownership:** Let you auto-withdraw to your own wallet
- **Pros:** Encourage true ownership of BTC
- **Cons:** No stock access, just Bitcoin

### **Real Ownership + Regulated?**
Check out **Bitwise**, **INX**, or **Prometheum**—they're trying to bridge compliance + ownership using tokenized securities with actual regulation.
