# CompTIA A+ Operational Procedures Summary

### USER
Can you summarize the operational procedures part of the ComptiA+ test for me? Make it convenient to look over but also concise and easy to understand

### ASSISTANT
For the **Operational Procedures** section of the CompTIA A+ exam, focus on the following:  

### **1. Safety & Best Practices (High Priority)**
- **ESD Protection**: Know how to prevent static damage (wrist straps, grounding).  
- **Electrical Safety**: Understand power protection (UPS, surge protectors).  
- **Proper Disposal**: Learn how to handle hazardous materials (batteries, toner, old hardware).  
- **Personal Safety**: Lockout/tagout (LOTO), lifting techniques, and PPE usage.  

💡 **Tip:** Expect scenario-based questions on safety procedures (e.g., “What should you do before replacing a PSU?”).  

### **3. Documentation & Policies (Medium Priority)**
- **Change Management**: Always get approval before major system changes.  
- **Incident Documentation**: Know why logs, ticketing systems, and documentation matter.  
- **Backup Strategies**: Be familiar with the **3-2-1 Rule** (3 copies, 2 different media, 1 offsite).  
- **Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)**: Understand company policy enforcement.  

💡 **Tip:** You might get a question like, **“Which backup method allows quick recovery from ransomware?”**  

### **Key Focus Areas for Exam Day**
✅ **Safety First** – Always choose the answer that follows safety procedures.  
✅ **Professionalism Matters** – Stay polite and avoid blaming users.  
✅ **Backups & Documentation** – Expect a few questions on disaster recovery.  
✅ **Follow Troubleshooting Steps** – The test will try to trip you up with missing steps.  

### **1. Safety & Environmental Procedures**  
- **ESD (Electrostatic Discharge) Protection**: Use anti-static wrist straps, mats, and proper grounding techniques.  
- **Electrical Safety**: Avoid working on live circuits; use proper power protection (UPS, surge protectors).  
- **Hazardous Materials**: Dispose of batteries, toner, and electronics properly (follow **MSDS/SDS** guidelines).  
- **Personal Safety**: Wear proper PPE (gloves, goggles), follow lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures, and avoid lifting heavy objects improperly.  

### **3. Documentation & Policies**  
- **Incident Documentation**: Record errors, resolutions, and changes (ticketing system, logs).  
- **Change Management**: Get approval before making significant system modifications.  
- **Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)**: Understand and follow company policies on device and internet use.  
- **Licensing & Compliance**: Use only legally licensed software (avoid piracy).  
- **Disaster Recovery & Backup**:  
  - Use **image-based**, **incremental**, and **full backups**.  
  - Follow the **3-2-1 Rule** (3 backups, 2 different media, 1 offsite).
