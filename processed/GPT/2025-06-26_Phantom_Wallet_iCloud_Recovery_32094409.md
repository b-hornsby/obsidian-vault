# Phantom Wallet iCloud Recovery

### ASSISTANT
Yes, **you may be able to regain access to your Phantom wallet from another device using your iCloud backup**, **but it depends on a few key factors.** Here's the breakdown:

### ⚠️ **If You Did NOT Enable Phantom's iCloud Backup:**

Then the iCloud backup of your phone **does not contain the seed phrase** in a usable form. Even if you restore the full phone backup on another device, **apps like Phantom do not store private keys in plaintext in iCloud by default** (for security).

In this case:

- You'll need your **12-word secret recovery phrase** (that you were prompted to save when setting up the wallet).
- Without it or the encrypted iCloud backup, **there's no way to access your wallet**. Not even Phantom support can help.

### 💡 Tips for Now:
- **Try accessing an iPad or Mac** if you have one tied to your iCloud.
- You could **temporarily borrow a trusted iPhone** to restore your wallet and move funds to a new address or hardware wallet.
- **Check iCloud Drive > Phantom** on a Mac or iCloud.com to see if Phantom’s encrypted backup is present.
