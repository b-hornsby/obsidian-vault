Sure! Here's the simplest explanation:

- **Bullish** means you think **prices will go up**.
- **Bearish** means you think **prices will go down**.

In crypto or any market:

- A **bullish market** is when people are buying, prices are rising, and there's optimism.
- A **bearish market** is when people are selling, prices are falling, and there's fear or pessimism.

If someone says, “I’m bullish on Bitcoin,” it means they believe Bitcoin’s price will increase.  
If they say, “I’m bearish on Ethereum,” they believe Ethereum’s price will drop.
