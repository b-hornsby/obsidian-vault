### **2. Set USDC as Your Spending Source**
Now that your USDC is on Coinbase, just tell the card to use USDC when you spend:

#### Here's how:
1. Open the **Coinbase app**.
2. Tap on the **Coinbase Card** section (you’ll find it under "Pay" or in the main dashboard).
3. Tap **“Spending settings”** or **“Spending asset”**.
4. Select **USDC**.

That’s it—your Coinbase Card will now **automatically pull USDC** from your balance when you use it, and **convert it to USD at the time of purchase.**

### 1. **Send USDC to Coinbase**
- In Trust Wallet, go to your USDC asset, hit **Send**, and enter your **Coinbase USDC wallet address** (double-check the network! If you're using Ethereum-based USDC, make sure you're sending to an ETH-compatible address on Coinbase).
- Wait for the transaction to confirm.

### 3. **Set USDC as Your Spending Asset (if needed)**
- Open the **Coinbase app**.
- Tap your **Coinbase Card** (you may find it under “Pay” or in the main menu).
- Hit **“Manage Card”**.
- Tap **"Spending Settings"** or **"Asset used for spending"**.
- Choose **USDC**.

*This tells Coinbase to automatically pull from your USDC balance when you swipe your card.*

Ah, gotcha—thanks for clarifying!

Here’s the key thing:  
**With the Coinbase Visa Card, there's no separate “wallet” or balance *on* the card itself**—it always pulls funds directly from your **Coinbase account** (i.e. the exchange). So:

> **You can’t preload the card like a prepaid debit card.**  
> The card just acts like a bridge to your crypto that lives on Coinbase.

### Workaround Ideas:
If you’re trying to:
- **Minimize how much you leave on exchanges**, or
- **Only move over funds when needed**,

Then here's a solid flow:

1. Keep your USDC in Trust Wallet or another self-custody wallet.
2. When you’re ready to spend:
   - Quickly send only what you want to use to your **Coinbase account**.
   - Set **USDC as your spending asset** in the app.
   - Spend using the card.
3. If you’re extra cautious, withdraw any leftover USDC back to your wallet after you’re done spending.
