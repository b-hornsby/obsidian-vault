A **retrace** in crypto (or any financial market) refers to a temporary reversal in the price of an asset before continuing in the original trend direction. It’s a short-term pullback that doesn’t necessarily indicate a full trend reversal.  

For example, if Bitcoin is in an uptrend and moves from $40,000 to $45,000, then drops back to $42,500 before continuing upward, that drop is considered a retrace. Traders use **Fibonacci retracement levels**, support zones, and volume analysis to determine whether a retrace is just a dip or the start of a bigger trend reversal.

The **REINS Act** (**Regulations from the Executive in Need of Scrutiny Act**) is a proposed U.S. law that aims to increase congressional oversight of major federal regulations. It would require any new **"major rule"** (typically those with an economic impact of **$100 million or more**) to be approved by Congress before taking effect.  

### **Key Points:**
- Currently, executive agencies (like the EPA or SEC) can implement major regulations without direct congressional approval.  
- The REINS Act would **shift power from executive agencies to Congress**, requiring lawmakers to **approve or reject** major regulations.  
- Supporters argue it **reduces regulatory overreach** and **ensures accountability**.  
- Critics say it could **slow down necessary regulations** and **politicize the process**.
