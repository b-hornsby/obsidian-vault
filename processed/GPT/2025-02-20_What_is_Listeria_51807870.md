# What is Listeria

### USER
What is listeria in the simplest way you can explain it to me?

### ASSISTANT
Listeria is a type of bacteria that can make you sick if you eat contaminated food. It’s mainly found in raw foods, unpasteurized dairy, and processed meats like deli meats and hot dogs. It can cause food poisoning, and in serious cases, it’s especially dangerous for pregnant women, newborns, and people with weak immune systems.
