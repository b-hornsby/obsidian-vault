# Mushroom safety check

### ASSISTANT
Yeah — if you ate it and you’re still feeling fine (especially several hours later), then it was **very likely okay**. Most moldy or spoiled mushrooms cause **GI symptoms** (like nausea, stomach pain, vomiting, or diarrhea) within a few hours at most.  

Here’s a quick rundown:  
- 🍄 **If it was just slightly discolored or had some dried spots** → that’s usually just oxidation or age, not mold.  
- 🦠 **If it had fuzzy white/green growth or smelled sour** → that’s mold or bacterial growth. Even then, most foodborne molds aren’t life-threatening, just unpleasant.  
- ⏱️ **If 6–12 hours have passed and you feel normal** → it’s a good sign the mushroom wasn’t toxic or seriously spoiled.  

That said, keep an eye out for:  
- Persistent nausea or stomach cramps  
- Dizziness, sweating, confusion  
- Vomiting or diarrhea  

If none of those show up, you’re almost certainly in the clear.
