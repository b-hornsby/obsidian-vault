# Flower vs Concentrates Strategy

### ASSISTANT
If you’re a daily smoker with some heavy days, your best bet for cost-effectiveness and longevity depends on your tolerance and smoking habits. Here’s the breakdown:  

### **Cost-Effectiveness:**  
- **Flower** is usually cheaper per gram, especially if you buy in bulk (like a half ounce). It also gives you a more balanced high due to the full spectrum of cannabinoids and terpenes.  
- **Concentrates** (wax, shatter, rosin, etc.) are more potent, so you use much less per session, but they cost more upfront. If you have a high tolerance, dabs might stretch your stash longer since a little goes a long way.  

### **Longevity:**  
- **If you stick to flower**, a half-ounce (14g) could last anywhere from a week to two weeks, depending on how much you smoke.  
- **If you mix in concentrates**, your flower will last longer since you’ll be supplementing it with high-potency hits. A gram of concentrate is roughly equivalent to about 3.5g–7g of flower in potency, depending on quality.  

### **Best Strategy for Variety & Longevity:**  
- Grab a **half-ounce of flower** for casual smoking and variety.  
- Add **1-2 grams of concentrate** for days you want something stronger or need to conserve flower.  
- Consider different strains (sativa/hybrid/indica) in both flower and concentrate to switch up effects.  

This way, you get cost-effectiveness from flower and potency from concentrates without blowing through either too quickly. If you’re going through a lot daily, adding some edibles could also help stretch your supply.
