# Flower vs Concentrates Cost-Effectiveness

### ASSISTANT
If cost-effectiveness and longevity are your main concerns, a mix of flower and concentrates might be the better option, but it depends on how you consume them. Here’s a breakdown:  

### **Cost-Effectiveness & Longevity:**  
1. **Flower** – If you’re smoking joints or blunts often, you’ll go through flower quickly. A half ounce could last a week or two depending on your consumption. Using a dry herb vaporizer can stretch it further since it’s more efficient.  
2. **Concentrates** – These are more potent, meaning you need less per session. A gram of concentrate (~70%+ THC) can be equivalent to about an eighth of flower (~20% THC). If you dab moderately, 3 grams could last as long as a half ounce of flower, maybe longer.  

### **Best Approach for Variety & Efficiency:**  
- **Split it up** – Get **a quarter of flower and a few grams of concentrate.** This way, you can smoke casually but rely on dabs when you need a stronger effect with less consumption.  
- **Choose the right concentrate** – Live resin, rosin, or diamonds will be strong but different in effect. A mix (e.g., a sativa, an indica, and a hybrid) keeps things interesting.  
- **Vaping or dabbing saves more** – If you use a vape/dab rig instead of a torch, it’s more efficient and less wasteful.  

If your tolerance is high, having both options will let you control your intake while keeping costs down. Plus, dabbing can be more discreet and less harsh on your lungs compared to smoking constantly.
