# Hormones Influence Bone Structure

### USER
Does taking hormones change your bone structure?

### ASSISTANT
Taking hormones, such as estrogen or testosterone, during puberty can influence the development of secondary sexual characteristics, including bone structure. Estrogen typically promotes the growth of wider hips and narrower waists in individuals assigned female at birth, while testosterone promotes broader shoulders and a more angular jawline in those assigned male at birth. However, the extent of these changes can vary depending on factors such as age, genetics, and the duration of hormone therapy.
