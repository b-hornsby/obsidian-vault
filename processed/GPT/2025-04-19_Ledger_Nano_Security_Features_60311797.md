# Ledger Nano Security Features

### ASSISTANT
A **Ledger Nano** (like the Nano S or Nano X) is a **hardware wallet**, and it’s designed with serious security in mind to protect your crypto assets. Here's how it works under the hood:

### **Real-World Example of How It Works**
1. You want to send Bitcoin.
2. You connect your Ledger to your computer.
3. The transaction info is shown on your Ledger screen.
4. You physically confirm it using the device buttons.
5. Ledger signs the transaction internally, **without exposing your private key**.
6. Your computer sends the signed transaction to the network.
