# Swap SOL for BTC

### ASSISTANT
You **can't directly swap Solana (SOL) for Bitcoin (BTC)** inside Phantom Wallet because Phantom is a **Solana-only wallet**, and Bitcoin exists on a completely different blockchain (BTC ≠ SOL chain). However, here are **safe and easy ways to make that swap** using your Phantom wallet:

### ✅ **Method 2: Use a Centralized Exchange (easiest for beginners)**
If you're not comfortable with bridges or cross-chain swaps, do this:

1. **Send SOL from Phantom to a CEX** like Coinbase, Binance, or Kraken.
2. On the exchange:
   - Convert SOL → BTC.
3. Withdraw BTC to a Bitcoin wallet.

> This is the **most beginner-friendly** and safest route, though it requires KYC.

### 🔐 Pro Tips
- **Never send SOL directly to a BTC address. You’ll lose it.**
- If you're using a bridge, verify **chain compatibility and fees**.
- Confirm you're swapping for **native BTC**, not a wrapped or fake version unless that’s your goal.
