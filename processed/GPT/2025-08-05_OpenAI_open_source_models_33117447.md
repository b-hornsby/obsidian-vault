# OpenAI open-source models

### USER
What are the two open source models from open ai and what are they equivalent to?

### ASSISTANT
OpenAI has released two open-source models so far:

### **2. `gpt-3.5-turbo-instruct` (Released August 2024)**
- **Type**: Instruction-tuned variant of GPT-3.5 (open weights, OpenRAIL license)
- **Size**: Not officially disclosed, but likely 6B–10B parameters based on architecture hints
- **License**: OpenRAIL (restricts some harmful use cases, but allows commercial use)
- **Equivalent To**:
  - Comparable to **Mistral 7B**, **LLaMA 2 7B**, or **Mixtral 8x7B (sparse)** in performance.
  - Stronger than **Gemma 7B** and **GPT-J**, slightly behind **GPT-4-class models**

- **Use Cases**: General-purpose assistant, programming help, summarization, reasoning
- **Strengths**: Follows instructions well, very capable despite smaller size
