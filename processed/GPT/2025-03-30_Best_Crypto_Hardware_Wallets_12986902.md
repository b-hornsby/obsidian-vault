### ASSISTANT
For securely storing your crypto, you’ll want a **hardware wallet** that supports **XRP, XLM, HBAR, XDC, ETH, SOL, ADA, and various memecoins.** The best options are:

### **1. Ledger Nano X** (Best Overall)  
   - ✅ Supports **all the coins you listed** + memecoins on **Ethereum (ERC-20), Solana (SPL), and BSC (BEP-20)**  
   - ✅ Bluetooth support (mobile-friendly)  
   - ✅ Strong security with custom OS  
   - ❌ Requires Ledger Live for some coins, but third-party wallets can expand support  

### **2. Trezor Model T** (Best Open-Source)  
   - ✅ Supports **ETH, SOL, ADA, XRP, XLM, and most ERC-20/BEP-20 tokens**  
   - ✅ Open-source firmware (no proprietary OS like Ledger)  
   - ❌ No **native support for HBAR or XDC** (requires third-party wallets)  
   - ❌ No Bluetooth  

### **3. Ellipal Titan 2.0** (Best Air-Gapped Wallet)  
   - ✅ Supports **all the coins you listed**  
   - ✅ Completely **air-gapped** (QR-code only, no USB or Bluetooth attack surface)  
   - ✅ Mobile-first approach  
   - ❌ Bulkier than Ledger/Trezor  

### **4. Keystone Pro** (Best for Security)  
   - ✅ Supports **ETH, SOL, ADA, XRP, XLM, XDC, HBAR, and memecoins**  
   - ✅ Air-gapped with **QR-code signing**  
   - ✅ Open-source firmware  
   - ❌ Expensive ($300+)  

If you **prioritize security** and want **full cold storage**, Ellipal Titan or Keystone Pro are solid choices. If you want **ease of use and broad token support**, Ledger Nano X is your best bet.
