# Sweating and anxiety causes

### ASSISTANT
What you're describing—sweating from your hands and intense anxiety—can come from a few different causes, even if you've eaten. Some possibilities include:

### 🔹 **2. Generalized Anxiety or Panic Response**
- Sweaty palms and restlessness are classic signs of **sympathetic nervous system** activation—aka fight or flight.
- This can happen from **chronic stress**, overthinking, or even **no clear trigger**.
- Anxiety doesn't always "make sense" in the moment. Your body can go into alert mode even if nothing feels wrong.

### 🔹 **4. Hormonal or Nervous System Imbalance**
- If this is happening more often, or comes in waves unrelated to food or stress, it could be:
  - **Thyroid issues (like hyperthyroidism)**
  - **Autonomic nervous system dysregulation**
  - **Low blood pressure or heart rate variability issues**

### ✅ What You Can Try Right Now:
1. **Box breathing** (4-in, 4-hold, 4-out, 4-hold) for 2–3 minutes.
2. Drink a glass of water with a pinch of salt if you suspect dehydration or lightheadedness.
3. Eat a small **protein-rich snack** if you feel like food isn’t sticking.
4. Get outside and move a little—light motion helps burn off excess adrenaline.

### 🔄 **Your Body Doesn’t Stay the Same Over Time**
Just because you’ve *tolerated* it before doesn’t mean your body will forever. A few things can shift the balance:

#### 🧠 1. **THC Tolerance ≠ Nervous System Resilience**
- You can build a **tolerance to the *high***… but not to the **stress** it puts on your nervous system.
- Over time, your endocannabinoid system may get out of sync—leading to:
  - Baseline anxiety
  - Restlessness when not high
  - Sweaty palms
  - Sudden “why do I feel like this?” moments

#### 🕓 3. **Sudden Onset Can Be Triggered by Something Small**
- Even something like **one rough night of sleep**, **dehydration**, **a stressful week**, or **mixing caffeine** can tip you over the edge *after weeks or months of "feeling fine."*

### 🌿 **How Daily Weed Use Can Cause Anxiety and Sweating**

#### 1. **THC Can Activate the Fight-or-Flight System**
- THC increases activity in the **amygdala**, the brain's fear center.
- Even if you’re used to it, your **nervous system still gets stressed**, especially if the dose is high or if you use it frequently.
- This can lead to **sweating, racing heart, and uneasiness**—sometimes even when you're *not* high.

#### 2. **Withdrawal & Rebound Effects**
- When used daily, your body adapts to having THC around. So when you go **even a few hours without it**, you might feel:
  - Restless
  - Anxious
  - Sweaty
  - Irritable
- That can happen **even if you don’t feel “addicted.”**

#### 3. **Impact on Sleep, Mood, and Blood Sugar**
- Long-term use can subtly affect:
  - **Blood sugar stability** (can lead to crashes = anxiety)
  - **REM sleep quality** (you might not feel rested)
  - **Cortisol levels** (stress hormone)

All of these can make your system more reactive during the day.

#### 4. **Cumulative Stress on the Nervous System**
- Daily THC may dull your feelings *in the moment*, but long term, it can **weaken your stress resilience**.
- So when you’re hit with even a little stress (hunger, caffeine, loud noise, random thought), your body may **overreact.**
