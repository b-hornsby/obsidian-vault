# Phantom wallet token removal

### ASSISTANT
Great question — and it’s actually very common, **even with a Ledger**.

Here’s how it happens:

### 🔐 Why Your Ledger Is Still Safe
As long as:
- You **didn’t approve any transaction** related to that token,
- You **haven’t clicked any scam links** or tried to "remove" the token through a third-party site,

> 🔒 Your wallet is secure and your Ledger protected your private keys.

### ✅ What You Should Do Now
1. **Hide or block the token** in Phantom.
2. **Ignore it forever.**
3. **Never follow links related to it.**
4. (Optional) Use [https://revokecash.app/solana](https://revokecash.app/solana) to double-check you didn’t accidentally approve anything.

### 🚫 **DO NOT**
- **Do not interact with the token at all** — especially don’t try to swap, stake, or click links claiming to help remove it.
- **Do not visit any websites** associated with it.
- **Do not approve transactions** related to it in Phantom.

#### Option 2: **Use Phantom’s “Block Token” Feature**
1. Tap the token.
2. Tap the “...” in the upper right.
3. Choose **“Block this token”**.
   - This tells Phantom to block it completely — not just hide it.

### 🧠 Why This Happens
Scammers will often:
- Send fake coins to wallets.
- List a fake website in the token name or description.
- Trick users into connecting their wallet to a malicious site to “claim rewards” or “remove” the token.
- Then drain the wallet once permissions are granted.
