# Orange Juice Bong Risks

### ASSISTANT
Using orange juice in a bong instead of water might sound interesting (maybe even tasty), but here's what actually happens:

### Pros (kind of):
- **Slight flavor twist**: You might get a citrusy hint in the smoke, though it’s usually not that noticeable.
- **Novelty**: It’s fun to experiment once for the sake of curiosity.

### Cons (more important):
- **Stickiness & residue**: Orange juice has sugar, pulp, and acids. These quickly gunk up your bong, making it way harder to clean than with water.
- **Harsher smoke**: Despite sounding smooth, OJ can make hits feel **harsher** due to the **acidity** in the juice. The citric acid can irritate your throat and lungs when heated.
- **Bacterial growth**: The sugars make a perfect breeding ground for mold and bacteria if you leave it sitting — even overnight.
- **No filtration benefits**: Water helps cool and filter the smoke. OJ doesn’t really do either better, and in fact can mess with the cooling effect.

### Verdict:
It's not dangerous per se, but it’s not recommended for regular use. If you're experimenting, just **clean your bong immediately after** to avoid a sticky, sour-smelling mess.
