### 🧠 1. **Original Project Stargate (Military Use)**

**What it was:**  
Project Stargate was a **U.S. military and CIA program** that ran from the 1970s to the 1990s. It focused on **“psychic spying”**, also called **remote viewing**.

**Goal:**  
They wanted to see if people could use psychic abilities to gather intelligence — like seeing inside enemy bases or locating hostages without physically being there.

**What they did:**  
They trained people to try and "see" distant or hidden objects, events, or people using only their minds.

**Did it work?**  
Mostly no. While there were a few odd successes, the program was eventually shut down because the results were inconsistent and not reliable enough for military use.
