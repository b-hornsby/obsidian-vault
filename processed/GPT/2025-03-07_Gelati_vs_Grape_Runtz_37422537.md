# Gelati vs Grape Runtz

### USER
Between the two strains gelati and grape runtz, which one is better?

### ASSISTANT
It depends on what you're looking for.  

- **Gelati** (a cross between Gelato and Biscotti) is known for its balanced effects, offering a mix of relaxation and mental clarity. It has a sweet, creamy, and slightly spicy flavor. It’s a solid choice if you want something that won’t completely knock you out but still provides a strong, euphoric high.  

- **Grape Runtz** (a cross of Grape Ape and Runtz) leans more into a fruity, candy-like flavor with deep relaxation effects. It’s typically more sedative, making it great for unwinding or helping with sleep.  

If you prefer a more balanced, functional high, **Gelati** is the better pick. If you want something more relaxing and flavorful, **Grape Runtz** is the way to go.
