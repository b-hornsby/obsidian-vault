### ASSISTANT
The Helium Network's token, **HNT (Helium Network Token)**, is **mintable by design** — this isn’t a flaw or oversight, but an intentional part of its **tokenomics and incentive structure**. Here's why:

### ⛏️ **2. It Follows a Deflationary Emission Schedule**
Even though HNT is mintable, it’s not infinite in supply at a constant rate. The protocol follows a **predefined minting curve** with **halvings**:

- It started with **5 million HNT minted per month** in 2020.
- The supply is halved **every 2 years** (next halving in August 2025).
- Eventually, no new HNT will be minted.

So, it’s **mintable but capped** — similar to how Bitcoin is mined but has a max supply.

### 🔐 **4. Governance & Upgrades**
Minting is controlled by the **Helium blockchain protocol**, and changes to the minting logic (if any) must be **approved through governance proposals**.
