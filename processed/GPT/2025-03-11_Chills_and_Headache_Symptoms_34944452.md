# Chills and Headache Symptoms

### USER
What does it mean when my whole body has chills and my head hurts?

### ASSISTANT
It sounds like you might be experiencing symptoms of an illness, such as:  

- **Viral Infection (Cold, Flu, COVID-19, etc.)** – Chills and headaches are common early signs.  
- **Fever** – Chills often accompany a fever as your body tries to regulate temperature.  
- **Dehydration** – Lack of fluids can cause headaches and chills.  
- **Migraine or Tension Headache** – Some headaches can cause chills or body discomfort.  
- **Other Infections (Sinusitis, Strep Throat, etc.)** – Some bacterial infections can cause similar symptoms.  

If your symptoms worsen (high fever, severe pain, confusion, trouble breathing), it’s best to seek medical attention. Make sure to rest, drink fluids, and monitor for other signs.
