How good is perplexity?

### ASSISTANT
Pretty much, yeah — **Perplexity works a lot like a web browser that’s AI-first.**  

Here’s how it stacks up against a normal browser:  

- **Search-first experience** → You type a query, and instead of giving you a list of links like Google, it summarizes answers directly and then shows the sources underneath. You can still click out to the original websites, so it doubles as a browser.  
- **Follow-up questions** → Instead of opening new tabs or rephrasing a search, you just keep chatting. It remembers the thread like ChatGPT.  
- **Built-in browsing** → When you click links inside Perplexity, they open in its own reading view, so you don’t have to leave the app/site.  
- **Mobile app** → On phones, it feels *very* much like a standalone browser, since you can navigate through results, sources, and pages without touching Safari/Chrome.  

That said, it’s **not a full browser replacement** (yet):  
- No bookmarks, history management, extensions, or advanced privacy features like you’d get in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.  
- It’s more of a **“research navigator”** than a general-purpose browser for, say, logging into banking sites or running web apps.  

👉 The way to think of it: **Google → Browser**. **Perplexity → AI Browser.**

### ASSISTANT
Perplexity is actually one of the stronger new players in the AI space right now, but *how good it is* really depends on what you’re comparing it to and what you want out of it.  

Here’s a breakdown:  

### 👍 Strengths
- **Real-time search + AI**: Unlike ChatGPT (which mostly relies on training data + occasional web browsing), Perplexity is designed from the ground up to pull live web results and summarize them. That makes it very strong for *research, fact-checking, and staying current*.  
- **Citation-focused**: Every answer comes with sources, so you can verify where info came from. Great for credibility.  
- **Concise and fast**: It usually gives direct, to-the-point answers instead of long-winded explanations.  
- **Strong free tier**: Even without paying, you get web search + summaries with decent quality.  
- **Pro models**: With Perplexity Pro, you can access GPT-4, Claude, Mistral, and others in one place, similar to a meta-AI hub.  

### 👎 Weaknesses
- **Not as creative**: It’s more of a “research assistant” than a “brainstorming partner.” If you want deep ideation, ChatGPT or Claude tend to be better.  
- **Less personality**: Its tone is very neutral, sometimes robotic compared to ChatGPT.  
- **Limited depth in some areas**: Because it prioritizes summarization, it can oversimplify or miss nuance when you want a long, detailed breakdown.  
- **Pro is pricey**: $20/month like ChatGPT Plus, but since it leans heavily on external models, you might not always need it if you already subscribe elsewhere.  

### Bottom line  
- **For research and fact-based queries → Perplexity is excellent** (often better than ChatGPT because of the live sources).  
- **For creativity, coding, and in-depth “walk me through this” style explanations → ChatGPT or Claude usually win.**  
- **As a general tool → It’s a great complement, not necessarily a replacement.**
