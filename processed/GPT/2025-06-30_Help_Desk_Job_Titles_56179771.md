# Help Desk Job Titles

### ASSISTANT
Great question — help desk jobs often go by a variety of names, depending on the company or specific duties. To broaden your search and avoid missing out on opportunities, try looking for these related job titles:

### 🔧 **Alternative Titles for Help Desk Roles**
Use these in job boards or LinkedIn:

#### Entry-Level/General:
- **IT Support Specialist**
- **Technical Support Specialist**
- **IT Technician**
- **Support Technician**
- **Desktop Support Technician**
- **Service Desk Analyst**
- **End User Support Specialist**
- **Technical Support Representative**
- **Computer Support Specialist**
- **IT Service Desk Analyst**

#### Tier-Specific:
- **Tier 1 / Level 1 Support**
- **Tier 2 / Level 2 Support** *(if you have more experience or certs)*

#### Remote/Hybrid Emphasis:
- **Remote IT Support**
- **Virtual IT Technician**
- **Remote Help Desk Technician**

#### Specialized Variants:
- **IT Support Analyst**
- **IT Operations Support**
- **IT Customer Support**
- **Technical Customer Support**
- **Application Support Analyst** *(focuses on software)*

#### For MSPs (Managed Service Providers):
- **IT Support Engineer**
- **NOC Technician** (Network Operations Center – entry-level roles often available)
- **Field Technician** *(some travel required, but usually hands-on)*
---
### 💡 Pro Tips:
- On job boards like Indeed, LinkedIn, or Glassdoor, try using broad search terms like:
  - `IT Support`
  - `Technical Support`
  - `Service Desk`
  - `Entry-Level IT`
  - `Support Technician`
  - Then apply filters like **entry-level**, **remote**, or **no degree required** to hone results.

- Try skills-based searches too:
  - `CompTIA A+`
  - `Troubleshooting`
  - `Windows Support`
  - `Active Directory`
  - `Ticketing Systems (e.g. Jira, Zendesk, ServiceNow)`
