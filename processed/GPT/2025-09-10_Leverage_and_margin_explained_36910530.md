When you open a leveraged position on Troll in Quanto:  

1. **You don’t actually “own” the Troll coins** like in spot trading.  
   - Instead, you’re entering a **contract** that tracks the price of Troll.  
   - The platform shows you a **synthetic coin amount** so it’s easier to visualize.  

2. That coin amount is based on your **position size ÷ Troll’s price**.  
   - Example: If Troll = **$1.78**, your $1,780 position would show as **1,000 Troll** exposure.  
   - If Troll = **$0.89**, that same $1,780 position would show as **2,000 Troll** exposure.  

3. As Troll’s price moves, the **USD value of those synthetic coins changes**.  
   - If the value goes up, your PnL goes positive (your account balance grows).  
   - If it goes down, your PnL goes negative (your margin shrinks).  

⚡ The key thing:  
That Troll coin number on Quanto isn’t the same as spot coins in your Phantom wallet.  
- In spot → coins are yours, fixed number, price changes affect total value.  
- In Quanto → coins are just a representation of your **exposure size**, which is powered by leverage.  

👉 When you close the position:  
- You don’t get “the coins.”  
- You get back **USDC/USDT equivalent** = margin + profits (or minus losses).  

So the extra “coins” you see are just the platform saying:  
*“If this were real spot, here’s how many Troll coins you’d effectively be holding with $1,780 worth.”*
