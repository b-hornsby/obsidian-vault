# Discount Percentage Calculation

### USER
What percentage discount would it be if the original total was 42.99 and the sale price was 34.40

### ASSISTANT
To calculate the percentage discount, you subtract the sale price from the original price, then divide that difference by the original price, and finally multiply by 100 to get the percentage. So, the discount percentage would be: 

\[
\frac{{42.99 - 34.40}}{{42.99}} \times 100
\]

\[
\frac{{8.59}}{{42.99}} \times 100 \approx 19.99\%
\]

So, the discount would be approximately 19.99%.
