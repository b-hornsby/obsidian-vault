### ASSISTANT
Hall effect switches can be worth it if you prioritize durability, smooth actuation, and adjustable actuation points. Since they rely on magnetic sensors instead of physical contacts, they last longer (often rated for 100M+ keystrokes) and allow for features like rapid trigger and customizable actuation. They’re especially beneficial for gaming and fast typing.  

### **Best Hall Effect Keyboards in 2025**  

1. **Wooting 60HE / Wooting 80HE** – One of the best for gaming with Rapid Trigger and full analog control.  
2. **SteelSeries Apex Pro (2023/2024 models)** – Adjustable actuation points from 0.2mm to 3.8mm.  
3. **Razer Huntsman V3 Pro** – Optical switches but offer similar benefits (adjustable actuation, fast response).  
4. **Gamakay LK75** – A budget-friendly option with Hall effect switches and decent customization.  
5. **Keychron Q1 HE (Upcoming)** – A Keychron Q-series keyboard rumored to feature Hall effect switches.  

If you’re gaming or want full control over actuation, Hall effect is great. For casual typing, a good mechanical switch can still be just as satisfying. Are you looking for a keyboard for gaming, work, or both?
