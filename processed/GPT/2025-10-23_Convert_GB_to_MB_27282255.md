# Convert GB to MB

### ASSISTANT
Let’s calculate it step-by-step carefully:  

1 GB = **1024 MB** (since we’re using binary units, which most systems do).  
So:  
128 GB × 1024 MB/GB =  

128 × 1024 = **131,072 MB**  

✅ **Answer: 128 GB = 131,072 MB**
