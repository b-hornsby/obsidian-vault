# Best Strain Selection

### ASSISTANT
Gumbo is believed to be an **indica-dominant strain**, but its exact lineage is a bit of a mystery. Some sources suggest it may be a **phenotype of Bubblegum** or have links to **Sherbet** genetics. However, the breeders have kept its full genetic background somewhat secretive.  

### **Effects:**  
If you’re looking for something that *really hits*, Gumbo is a great choice. It’s known for:  
- **Strong body high** – Expect deep relaxation, couch-lock, and a heavy, almost sedative effect.  
- **Euphoric & stoney head buzz** – Great for zoning out, watching movies, or just vibing.  
- **Hunger-inducing** – A serious case of the munchies is almost guaranteed.  
- **Sleepy & sedating** – Ideal for nighttime use or if you want to completely unwind.  

### **Best Uses:**  
- Pain relief, stress, insomnia, or just a strong, knockout high.  
- Not the best for productivity or social settings—it’s more of a “melt into the couch” strain.  

If you’re after **maximum potency**, this is probably the best pick from your list!
