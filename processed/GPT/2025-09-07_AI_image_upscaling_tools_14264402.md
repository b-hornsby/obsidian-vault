##  Choosing the Right Free Tool for You

- **Want total privacy and local processing?** → **Upscayl** or **Waifu2x** are your best bets.
- **Need something fast and online?** → Try **Upscale.media**, **Fotor**, or **Pixelcut**.
- **Looking for more variety?** → Explore **Tiny WOW**, **PicWish**, or **Icons8**, depending on your format and quality needs.
- **Prefer batch or offline workflows?** → **Upscayl** (desktop) or some web tools like **Fotor** support multiple images efficiently.
