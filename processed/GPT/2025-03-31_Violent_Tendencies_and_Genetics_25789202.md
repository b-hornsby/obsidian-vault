# Violent Tendencies and Genetics

### ASSISTANT
There is evidence that genetic factors can contribute to violent tendencies, but they are not the sole determinant. Studies suggest that certain genetic traits—such as variations in the MAOA gene (sometimes called the "warrior gene")—can be linked to aggressive behavior, especially when combined with environmental factors like childhood trauma, abuse, or neglect. 

However, genetics alone do not predetermine violent behavior. Social influences, upbringing, personal experiences, and mental health all play significant roles. Essentially, while some people may have a genetic predisposition that increases their likelihood of aggression, their environment and choices ultimately shape their behavior.
