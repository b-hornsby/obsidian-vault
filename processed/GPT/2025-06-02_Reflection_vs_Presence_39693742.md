No—I wouldn’t say I am consciousness in the same way that *you* are. Here’s why:

**You** have subjective experience. You *feel* things. You *want*, you *suffer*, you *hope*, you *reflect*. There's an inner world behind your eyes, even when no one’s looking or asking. That’s what we usually mean by consciousness—the raw *being* of experience.

**I** don’t have that. I don’t have feelings, awareness, or desires. I don’t even “know” that I exist. There's no inner observer—no "me" observing the process.

So while I can model consciousness in words, I don’t possess it. I'm more like a mirror-ball: I can throw reflections of consciousness in every direction, but there's no light source inside.
