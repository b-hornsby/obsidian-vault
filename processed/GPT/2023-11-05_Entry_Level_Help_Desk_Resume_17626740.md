You can emphasize your troubleshooting skills on your resume by including them in your skills section and by providing specific examples of how you've used troubleshooting in your work experience. Here's how to do it:

1. **Skills Section:**
   - "Troubleshooting" or "Problem Solving" (as a specific skill) can be listed in your skills section.

2. **Work Experience:**
   - Within your work experience section, describe how you applied troubleshooting skills in your previous roles. For example:
     - "Demonstrated strong troubleshooting skills by diagnosing and resolving technical issues, ensuring minimal downtime for critical systems."
     - "Applied problem-solving expertise to identify and fix software and hardware issues, resulting in improved system reliability and end-user satisfaction."
     - "Proactively addressed and resolved operational challenges through systematic troubleshooting, streamlining processes and reducing potential bottlenecks."
     - "Utilized troubleshooting techniques to diagnose network problems, leading to quicker resolution and reduced service disruptions."

By including both a dedicated skill in your skills section and examples of how you've applied troubleshooting in your work experience, you can effectively showcase your problem-solving abilities to potential employers.
