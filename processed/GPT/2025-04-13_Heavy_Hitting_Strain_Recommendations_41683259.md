### **1. It's a Wrapped Asset, Not the Main Token**
Wormhole extensions are usually **wrapped versions** of the original token on another chain. This means:
- The real asset is locked on the origin chain.
- A synthetic (wrapped) version is minted on the new chain.

**Why liquidity isn't locked**:  
The devs or bridge operators typically **control the minting and liquidity** of the wrapped version, so they’re not incentivized to lock it — especially for a meme project where the hype is short-lived.

### **3. It’s Treated as a “Bonus” Chain**
Most meme tokens treat cross-chain deployments like **extra reach, not core infrastructure**. That means:
- Devs don’t always bother with proper setups (like audits or locked liquidity).
- It’s more of a marketing move than a real expansion.

### **Red Flag or Nah?**
- **If the liquidity is unlocked AND controlled by the deployer** — that’s a rug pull risk.
- **If the main token has locked liquidity and a strong community**, the Wormhole extension is just a satellite — less risky but more volatile.
