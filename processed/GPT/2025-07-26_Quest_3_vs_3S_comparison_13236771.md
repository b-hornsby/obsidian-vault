# Quest 3 vs 3S comparison

### USER
what is the difference between the meta quest 3 and the 3s

### ASSISTANT
Here’s a detailed breakdown comparing the **Meta Quest 3** and **Meta Quest 3S**:

## 🎮 Display & Optics

- **Meta Quest 3**:
  - Uses premium **pancake lenses**, thinner and sharper.
  - **Display resolution**: 2064 × 2208 per eye (~30% more pixels than 3S).
  - **Field of View (FoV)**: approx. 110° horizontal citeturn0search33turn0search32turn0search22.

- **Meta Quest 3S**:
  - Reverts to **Fresnel lenses** (like Quest 2), making it bulkier and less sharp.
  - **Display resolution**: 1832 × 1920 per eye (same as Quest 2).
  - **FoV**: narrower at ~96°–96 horiz / 90 vertical citeturn0search3turn0search19turn0search22.

Many users note the optical downgrade is significant—once experienced, Fresnel optics feel lower quality citeturn0search2turn0search16.

## 👓 IPD Adjustment

- **Quest 3** offers a **smooth IPD wheel**, adjusting between ~53 mm and 75 mm in fine increments.
- **Quest 3S** only supports **three fixed IPD presets**: ~58 mm, 63 mm, and 68 mm—less flexibility, especially for users with larger IPDs citeturn0search3turn0search22.

## 💰 Storage & Pricing

- **Quest 3**: Available in **128 GB** and **512 GB** options. Originally priced at $499 and $649.
- **Quest 3S**: Offered in **128 GB** and **256 GB**, priced at **$299 (128 GB)** and **$399 (256 GB)** – a $200‑ish saving over Quest 3 citeturn0search33turn0search22turn0search1turn0news25.

## ✅ Which Should You Choose?

- **Pick **Quest 3** if**:
  - You value sharper visuals, wider FoV, premium optics, fine IPD adjustment, and the best mixed-reality experience.
  - You’re investing long‑term and want the higher-end VR/MR experience.

- **Pick **Quest 3S** if**:
  - You're upgrading from Quest 2, want all the same games, and care more about value.
  - You're comfortable with slightly lower visual quality and simpler IPD adjustment in exchange for a $200‑plus savings—and slightly longer battery life citeturn0news25turn0search13turn0search22.

### In summary:

- Both share the same high-end internal hardware and game compatibility.
- **Quest 3** offers superior optics, wider view, better mixed reality tracking, and finer customization.
- **Quest 3S** offers near-identical performance in a more budget‑friendly package with modest tradeoffs in visuals and comfort.
