# Life Path Calculation

### ASSISTANT
To calculate the **Life Path Number**, you reduce the birthdate digits to a single-digit or master number (11, 22, or 33).  

### Step 1: Break down the birthdate  
March 23, 1996 → **03 / 23 / 1996**  

### Step 2: Reduce each part  
- **Month:** 3 (March stays 3)  
- **Day:** 23 → 2 + 3 = **5**  
- **Year:** 1996 → 1 + 9 + 9 + 6 = **25** → 2 + 5 = **7**  

### Step 3: Add them together  
3 + 5 + 7 = **15** → 1 + 5 = **6**  

### Final Life Path Number: **6**  

A **Life Path 6** is associated with responsibility, care, and nurturing. People with this number tend to be natural protectors, drawn to helping others, creating harmony, and valuing family and community.
