# CEX vs DEX Explained

### USER
Is crypto.com a centralized exchange the same way Coinbase is? Explain to me what the difference is between that and decentralized exchange as simply as you can

### ASSISTANT
Yes, **Crypto.com is a centralized exchange (CEX)**—just like **Coinbase**.

### 🔁 Decentralized Exchange (DEX) — *like Uniswap or Raydium*:
- **No company runs it** — it’s just code on a blockchain.
- **You connect your wallet** (like MetaMask or Phantom) — no account needed.
- **You control your crypto and keys.**
- Trades happen directly between users (peer-to-peer).
- Usually **no KYC** (no ID or login).

> 🧠 Think of it like **a vending machine** that swaps tokens. You stay in control, but you need to know what you're doing.

If you're just starting, **CEXs are beginner-friendly**. But if you want **more control and privacy**, **DEXs** are the way to go—just make sure you know how to use a crypto wallet safely.
