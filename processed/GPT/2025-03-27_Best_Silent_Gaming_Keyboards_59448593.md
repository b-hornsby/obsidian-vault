# Best Silent Gaming Keyboards

### USER
What is the best silent sounding keyboard for gaming?

### ASSISTANT
If you’re looking for a **silent gaming keyboard**, you’ll want one with **linear switches** that offer minimal noise without sacrificing responsiveness. Here are some of the best options:  

### **Top Silent Gaming Keyboards**  

1. **SteelSeries Apex Pro Mini / Apex Pro TKL**  
   - Uses **OmniPoint adjustable switches** (customizable actuation, very quiet)  
   - Compact and high-quality build  

2. **Corsair K100 Air Wireless**  
   - **Ultra-thin mechanical switches** (Cherry MX Ultra Low Profile)  
   - Fast response time with near-silent keystrokes  

3. **Razer BlackWidow V4 Pro (with Yellow Switches)**  
   - **Razer Yellow linear switches** (quiet and smooth)  
   - Excellent RGB and macro support  

4. **Logitech G915 TKL (Tactile or Linear)**  
   - **Low-profile GL Linear switches** (quietest version)  
   - Wireless and premium build  

5. **Leopold FC660M or FC980M (Silent Red Switches)**  
   - Uses **Cherry MX Silent Red switches**  
   - Excellent dampening for minimal sound  

### **If You Prefer a Custom or DIY Approach:**  
- Look for keyboards with **Gateron Silent Red, Silent Ink Black, or Silent Alpaca switches**  
- Use **lubed stabilizers** and **sound-dampening foam** for extra quietness
