### 🔧 Pro Tips for Reframing:

- **Crop to 9:16 aspect ratio** and **zoom in** to keep your subject in-frame.
- Use **Auto Reframe** (Premiere Pro) or **Smart Reframe** (Resolve Studio) to automate the process.
- If available, animate **position and scale keyframes** to follow your subject throughout the clip.
- For aesthetic polish, consider a blurred or branded background behind the cropped video.
