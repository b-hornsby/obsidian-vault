## How I think

I have patterns. Some are strengths. Some are things I'm actively working on.

**I research first, then build.** I ask "how do i" before I ask "how do I build." 15.6% of my conversations start with questions. This is genuine curiosity, but it means the research phase is 3x longer than it needs to be.

**I give myself 72 hours.** Every project gets 3 days of peak energy. After that, either it ships or it stalls. The homelab is the exception — it sustains because it's an environment I live in, not a project with a finish line.

**I switch, don't push.** When I'm stuck, I switch tools, switch LLMs, or switch projects entirely. I rarely push through frustration — I redirect it. Only when something genuinely breaks and I can't ignore it do I push through.

**I don't want sugarcoating.** I asked an AI to be my brutally honest advisor. I tell it to cut through my blind spots and delusions. That's how I learn best — direct, uncomfortable truth.

**Planning is safer than shipping.** I've written more plan.md files than lines of shipped code. This is the pattern I'm actively fighting. The vault itself is part of that fight — it's a thing that exists, not just a plan.
