## Bugs found & fixed in downloader
1. `--local-dir-use-symlinks` flag does NOT exist in hf 1.17.0 -> every command rejected ("No such option"). Removed it; default --local-dir writes real files. This was silently failing the whole job at first launch.
2. `already_done()` counted `.cache` partial dirs as "done" -> would skip timed-out big models as complete. Fixed to ignore hidden dirs.
3. 30-min PER_MODEL_TIMEOUT too short for huge models -> GLM-5.2-colibri (352GB) and Qwen2.5-VL-72B (84GB) TIMED OUT and failed. Pixtral-Large (42GB) also partial when killed.
4. Hardened (patched both /tmp/download_models_vault.py and /tmp/download_gapfill_vault.py): GGUF items never fall back to full-repo pull on failure (only colibri uses full-repo, by design); added disk-headroom guard (skip if free < model_size+50GB).
